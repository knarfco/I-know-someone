Territory rules

  • Territory = trade × region (e.g., paving in Palm Beach County).
  • Seat cap: 3 seats per trade per region, then closed. Scarcity holds price.
  • Exclusive, full lock: no seats sold in their trade and territory. Highest price.
  • Exclusive, first look: seats allowed, but Exclusive client sees new finds 48–72 hours earlier.
  • Overlapping trades (e.g., land clearing vs site prep) defined in each agreement.
  • Everglades: no seats sold in earthwork in his territory.

Costs & cautions

  • Pass-through: servers, AI usage, bid-platform subscriptions, software licenses. At cost, or bundled with a stated ceiling.
  • Outside specialists (estimators, surety, legal): billed separately.
  • Bid cap: every tier states how many packages are included.
  • No success fees on government contracts without attorney review (federal contingent-fee rules).
  • Data terms: sell the finding, filtering and summaries, linking to official sources. Attorney review before selling seats.
  • Unit cost: one nightly sweep serves every client; each extra seat costs little to serve.
