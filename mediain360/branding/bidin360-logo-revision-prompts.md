# BidIn360 logo: revision prompts (round 2)

Reference: `bidin360-logo-draft-1.png` (round 1).

## What's working (keep)
- Three-arc ring icon with arrows (Find / Team / Win, a loop that never stops)
- Colors: orange, deep green, charcoal
- Heavy geometric sans-serif wordmark, "360" in orange (MediaIn360 family feel)

## What to fix
1. "BidIn" reads as "Bidln" or "Bidin": the capital I looks like a lowercase l.
2. The center symbol (dot over a V) reads as a person or a smiley face, and the V points down, which reads as "decline."
3. The three arcs are uneven: different lengths, gaps and arrowhead sizes.
4. Arc strokes are heavier than the letter strokes; the icon feels busier than the wordmark.
5. Icon is slightly too large and sits a little low against the wordmark.
6. It's a raster image with soft edges; we need clean vector.

## Prompt 1: main revision
> Refine this existing logo for "BidIn360," keeping the same overall concept, layout and colors, with these specific corrections:
>
> **Wordmark**
> - Spell it exactly "BidIn360" and make the capital "I" unmistakable: either give the "I" short horizontal serifs (top and bottom crossbars) while the rest of the word stays sans-serif, or set "In" in a different color from "Bid" so the word breaks clearly as Bid | In | 360.
> - Preferred color split: "Bid" in charcoal #1A1C19, "In" in deep green #03381C, "360" in orange #F26522 (match MediaIn360's orange exactly if provided).
> - Same heavy geometric sans-serif as now (Montserrat ExtraBold / Gilroy Heavy style). Tighten kerning slightly between "d" and "I" and between "n" and "3" so the word reads as one unit.
>
> **Icon**
> - Keep the circular ring of three arrows, but make it mathematically even: three identical 120° arcs, identical gaps (about 8% of the circumference each), identical arrowheads, all pointing clockwise.
> - Arc colors, clockwise from the top: orange (Find), deep green #03381C (Team), charcoal #1A1C19 (Win).
> - Stroke weight of the arcs should match the stem weight of the letters in the wordmark.
> - Replace the center dot-and-V with a single bold checkmark in orange, angled upward to the right (short left stroke, long right stroke). No dot, no face, no figure. Optional: a small orange bullseye dot can sit where the checkmark's long stroke ends, but only if it does not read as a head.
> - The center symbol should fill about 45% of the ring's inner diameter and be optically centered.
>
> **Lockup**
> - Icon height about 1.25 times the cap height of the wordmark, vertically centered on the letters' cap height (not the baseline).
> - Space between icon and wordmark equal to the width of the letter "B."
>
> **Style**
> Flat vector, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture. Crisp edges. Must still read clearly at 16 px (favicon) and as a square app icon. Industry-neutral: no tools, machines, buildings or gavels.
>
> **Deliver:** SVG and transparent PNG of (1) horizontal logo, (2) stacked logo (icon above wordmark), (3) icon-only square app tile, (4) one-color white-on-dark, (5) one-color black-on-light.

## Prompt 2: alternative center symbol (if the checkmark feels too generic)
> Same corrected BidIn360 logo, but the center of the three-arrow ring is a simple bullseye: two concentric circles (outer ring thin, center dot solid) in orange, with a short charcoal arrow entering the bullseye from the lower left and landing in the center. Flat vector, same stroke weights as the ring, no other changes.

## Prompt 3: alternative where the ring becomes the "0"
> Same BidIn360 wordmark with the corrected "I" and color split, but no separate icon. Instead, the "0" in "360" is replaced by the three-arrow ring (three equal 120° arcs, orange, green and charcoal, clockwise arrowheads) sized to match the height of the other numerals, with a small orange checkmark inside. Provide a separate icon-only version of that ring for the app tile.

## Prompt 4: tagline lockup (after the main logo is approved)
> The approved BidIn360 horizontal logo with the tagline "Find it. Team it. Win it." centered underneath in a light weight of the same sans-serif, charcoal gray #5B6058, cap height about 30% of the wordmark's cap height, no wider than the wordmark.

## Prompt 5: client edition lockup
> A co-brand lockup: "TerraAll Intelligence" in large bold sans-serif, deep green #03381C, on top. Underneath, smaller, the words "powered by" in light gray #8B9088, followed by the small BidIn360 horizontal logo at about 35–40% of the width of the client name. White background, left-aligned.

## Check before approving
- Read it at 16 px and 32 px: is "BidIn360" still legible, and does the icon still read as a ring?
- Black-and-white version still works.
- Trademark search (USPTO) and domain check (bidin360.com) done.
