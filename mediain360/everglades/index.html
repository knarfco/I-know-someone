Meet TerraAll Intelligence

One app · One database · One dashboard · Three engines

Project & Bid Finder Find the work.

Every night it visits public bid sites, pulls the bid packets, and spots the requirements. Only earthwork makes the list: clearing, excavation, ponds and canals, restoration, debris, drainage.

  • Federal, state, counties, cities, school boards, water districts, ports and airports
  • You set the dials: any size, any area, the kinds of work you want
  • Results land on your phone every morning

Prime Contractor & Relationship Finder Find the team.

Big programs are won by big prime contractors, and they need earthwork subs. This engine tracks who's winning the large contracts, where, and who needs help.

  • Who won what, from public award records
  • Which primes need clearing, excavation and site work subs
  • Their sub sign-up lists and outreach days, plus every relationship we build

Bid Builder Win the work.

Once you say "go," we turn the bid into a plan: a plain-English summary, a checklist of every form, the deadlines, and the first draft.

  • Every requirement and deadline on one page
  • A document library that makes every bid faster than the last
  • You check the price, finalize and sign
Built from scratch, just for this. Real build, real running costs. We'll work out the numbers together, in person.

Bids come as hundreds of pages of government language. We turn them into one page you can read in a minute. Flip it.

INVITATION TO BID NO. XX-XXXX. CANAL MAINTENANCE TASK ORDER

1.1 The Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, supervision and incidentals necessary to perform the removal, reshaping and disposal of accumulated sediment and vegetative material within the designated conveyance segments, in accordance with the Contract Documents, the Technical Specifications and all applicable federal, state and local regulations...

2.4 Offerors shall be pre-qualified members of the Multiple Award Task Order Contract pool and shall maintain throughout the term all licenses, certifications, bonding capacity and insurance coverages as set forth in Section 7, including but not limited to Commercial General Liability...

3.2 A mandatory pre-bid site visit shall be held on the date indicated in the Schedule of Events. Failure to attend shall render the offer non-responsive. Questions shall be submitted in writing no later than the deadline set forth herein...
