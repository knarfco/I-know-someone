    How it works

    From your scope to a signed-off building

    1. Send your scope. Project type, location, deadline, square footage and the tasks you need. Specs and plans help but aren't required.
    2. We confirm crew availability. A home crew or a travel crew, matched to the size and timing of the job.
    3. You get an itemized bid. Every task priced as its own line, by phase, with nothing buried.
    4. We mobilize on your schedule. Rough, final and punch cleans timed to your trades and inspections.
    5. Closeout on time. Walkthrough, punch items re-cleaned, checklists and photos for your closeout package.

    Our methods

    Capture it. Don't move it.

    Construction dust that is swept or blown just lands somewhere else. Our crews capture it with HEPA vacuums and microfiber, use water and pH-neutral products before stronger chemistry, and keep wash water out of storm drains.

    How we clean differently

    Above Eye Level Cleaning

    The ceiling gets cleaned once. Then it gets dusty again.
