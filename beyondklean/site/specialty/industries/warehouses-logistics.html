Specialty cleaning for warehouses & logistics

Distribution centers, cold storage and cross-dock facilities have large floors, high ceilings and constant equipment traffic. Specialty work focuses on height, floor condition and keeping fire protection systems clear.

Common needs

  • High-level dust removal from racking, joists and ductwork
  • Floor scrubbing, degreasing and tire mark removal
  • Cold storage and freezer cleaning
  • Dock leveler, pit and trench cleaning
  • Spill cleanup for oils, batteries and damaged freight
  • Light fixture and skylight cleaning

Rules and standards

  • NFPA 13 sprinkler clearance requirements that limit what can sit near sprinkler heads
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.67 vehicle-mounted platforms and 1910.178 powered industrial trucks
  • OSHA walking-working surfaces and fall protection rules, 29 CFR 1910 Subpart D
  • EPA and state rules for disposing of spilled products and wash water

Questions

Can high-level cleaning happen while the building operates?

Often yes, by zoning off aisles and working in sections. Contractors should plan lift paths and traffic control with the site.

Why do cold storage facilities need specialists?

Low temperatures change how chemicals, water and equipment behave. Crews need cold-weather gear, and some cleaning methods freeze or leave ice.

Do contractors bring their own lifts?

Usually, or they rent them. Confirm floor load limits, aisle widths and the operator training records the site requires.
