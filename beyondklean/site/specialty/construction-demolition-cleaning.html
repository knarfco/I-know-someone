Specialized Construction & Demolition Cleaning

This family covers cleanup tied to demolition, renovation, surface preparation and decommissioning. Buyers include general contractors, developers, property managers, plant owners, transportation agencies and public facility managers.

Construction cleanup often involves silica dust, lead paint, high-pH slurry, hazardous waste and stormwater permits. Specialists bring the equipment, training and paperwork to handle these without stopping the schedule or exposing the owner to fines.

Questions about construction

Do I need an asbestos survey before demolition?

EPA's asbestos NESHAP, 40 CFR 61 Subpart M, requires a thorough inspection for asbestos before demolition or renovation of most buildings, with notification for demolitions. State and local rules may add requirements.

Who is responsible for silica on a jobsite?

Each employer must protect its workers under OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1153. General contractors usually coordinate controls.

Where does construction waste go?

Debris goes to permitted facilities. Hazardous waste needs testing and manifests. Requirements vary by state.
