Industrial Shutdown & Turnaround Cleaning

This family covers the heavy industrial cleaning done during plant shutdowns and turnarounds: tanks, vessels, heat exchangers, lines, sumps and kilns. Buyers are turnaround planners, maintenance managers and procurement teams at refineries, chemical plants, mills and terminals.

Turnaround cleaning combines confined spaces, hazardous materials, high-pressure water and tight schedules. A delay in cleaning can hold up inspection and repair work across the unit. Specialist contractors bring trained crews, vacuum and waterblasting fleets, and the safety programs refineries and chemical plants require of contractors.

Questions about shutdowns & turnarounds

When should turnaround cleaning contractors be selected?

Large turnarounds are often planned a year or more ahead. Cleaning scope, equipment and crew sizes should be settled early so contractors can reserve trucks, waterblast units and trained staff.

What safety records do plants usually require?

Plants commonly ask for EMR, OSHA recordable and lost-time rates, a written safety program, drug and alcohol testing, and site-specific training. Many use third-party contractor prequalification services as well.

What does OSHA PSM require of contractors?

At sites covered by Process Safety Management (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119), the host must evaluate contractor safety performance, and the contractor must train its workers on the process hazards and the plant's emergency plan.
