Specialty

Mold & Microbial Remediation

This family covers assessment, removal and verification of mold and other microbial growth in buildings, HVAC systems and water systems, including Legionella control. Buyers include property managers, facility directors, healthcare and school operators, and owners of buildings affected by water damage.

Poorly controlled remediation can spread spores through a building, and a cleanup that misses the moisture source fails again. Several states license mold assessors and remediators, and healthcare buildings face water management expectations from CMS and accreditors.

Questions about mold remediation

Which states license mold work?

Florida, Texas, New York and Louisiana license mold work, and a few other states or cities have related rules. Requirements vary by state; check your state's licensing board before hiring.

Should the same company test and remove mold?

Many experts advise using separate firms to avoid conflicts of interest. Some states restrict it: New York bars the same contractor from both assessing and remediating the same property, and Florida bars an assessor's company from remediating a structure it assessed within the prior 12 months.

Is there a federal mold exposure limit?

No. There are no federal exposure limits for mold. Industry standards such as IICRC S520 and EPA guidance focus on removing growth and fixing moisture.
