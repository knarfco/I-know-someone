Hazardous Waste & Environmental Cleanup

This family covers removal, cleanup and disposal of regulated hazardous materials: asbestos, lead paint, PCBs, chemicals, fuels, mercury and contaminated soil. Buyers include facility managers, general contractors, property owners, environmental managers and public agencies.

Most of this work is governed by EPA, OSHA, DOT and state rules that require trained, accredited or licensed workers. Mistakes can expose people, spread contamination and leave the property owner or waste generator legally responsible for years.

Questions about hazardous waste

Who is responsible for hazardous waste after a contractor hauls it away?

Under RCRA, the business that generated the waste stays responsible for it from generation to final disposal. Hiring a contractor does not transfer that liability. Ask for copies of signed manifests and disposal certificates for your records.

Do I need an environmental consultant as well as a cleanup contractor?

Often, yes. Many projects use an independent consultant to test materials, write the specification and confirm cleanup, while the contractor does the removal. Some states require that separation for asbestos, lead or mold work.

Are licensing requirements the same in every state?

No. Federal rules set a baseline, but many states run their own asbestos, lead, tank and meth cleanup programs with separate licenses. Requirements vary by state; check your state's licensing board or environmental agency.
