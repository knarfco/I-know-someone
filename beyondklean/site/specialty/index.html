    Questions

    What is Beyond Klean Specialty?

    A guide and advertising platform for specialty cleaning and remediation. Buyers learn what a job involves and which credentials to ask for, then contact the specialists who advertise on the page.

    Does Beyond Klean perform specialty work?

    No. Beyond Klean provides marketing, procurement research, and consulting services. All bidding, contracts, project performance, and related responsibilities belong solely to the contractor.

    How do I find a specialist?

    Open the service you need, read the guide, then call an advertiser listed on the page or send one request through the Get quotes form.

    How are regulated jobs handled?

    Asbestos, lead, biohazard, radiological, hazardous waste and similar work may be performed only by properly licensed and certified specialists. Each of those pages lists the credentials to ask for. Always confirm them before hiring.
