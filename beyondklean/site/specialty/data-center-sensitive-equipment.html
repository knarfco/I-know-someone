Specialty

Data Center & Sensitive Equipment Cleaning

This family covers cleaning of data centers, telecom rooms, control rooms and other spaces where dust and residue can damage electronics. Buyers include data center operators, colocation providers, IT and facilities managers, utilities and manufacturers with control systems.

Conductive dust, zinc whiskers and corrosive smoke residues can cause equipment failures and downtime. Cleaning live, critical spaces requires static control, strict change procedures and staff who know what not to touch. Specialists also provide particle count data that equipment makers and insurers often ask for.

Questions about data center

What cleanliness level do data centers aim for?

Many hardware makers and ASHRAE guidance reference ISO 14644-1 Class 8 for data center air. Contractors can measure particle counts before and after cleaning to show results.

Do contractors touch the servers?

Usually only the outside of racks and enclosures. Internal cleaning of IT equipment is normally done by or with approval of the equipment owner or manufacturer.

Can cleaning be done without downtime?

Yes, in most cases. Crews work in live environments using static-safe methods and change control. Any work that could affect power or cooling is scheduled with operations.
