This family covers cleaning and decontamination of hospitals, clinics, laboratories, pharmacies and cleanrooms. Buyers include environmental services directors, infection preventionists, pharmacy managers, lab safety officers and cleanroom operators.

In these settings, cleaning protects patients, products and research from contamination. Specialists bring validated methods, approved disinfectants, gowning discipline and records that accreditors, regulators and quality teams expect.

Questions about healthcare & cleanrooms

Which disinfectants should a contractor use in healthcare?

Use EPA-registered hospital disinfectants with claims for the organisms of concern, applied at the label's contact time. EPA keeps lists such as List K for Clostridioides difficile and List P for Candida auris.

Does the contractor need bloodborne pathogen training?

Yes, where workers may contact blood or other potentially infectious materials. OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030 requires training, an exposure control plan and hepatitis B vaccination offers.

How is cleaning verified in these environments?

Common methods include fluorescent marker audits, ATP testing, environmental monitoring in cleanrooms and documented checklists. Ask what the contractor uses and how results are reported.
