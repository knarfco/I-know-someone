Fire, Smoke & Flood Restoration

This family covers cleanup and recovery after fires, smoke events, water losses, sewage backups and storms. Buyers include property managers, facility and plant managers, insurers, public agencies and businesses that need to reopen fast.

Every day of delay adds mold growth, corrosion and lost revenue, and insurers expect work to follow recognized standards with clear records. Restoration specialists bring drying equipment, residue testing, contents handling and documentation that holds up in a claim review.

Questions about fire & flood

Which standards apply to restoration work?

The IICRC publishes ANSI-accredited standards, including S500 for water damage restoration and S700 for fire and smoke damage restoration. Ask whether the contractor follows them and how they document compliance.

Will insurance pay for the work?

That depends on your policy and the cause of loss. A contractor can document conditions and work performed, but the insurer decides coverage. Keep photos, moisture logs, inventories and invoices together.

Should I wait for the adjuster before starting?

Most policies expect you to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage. Photograph conditions first, notify your insurer, then start emergency mitigation such as water extraction or board-up. Check your policy for specific notice terms.
