Specialty

Large Facility & Overhead Cleaning

This family covers recurring cleaning of ceilings, structure, fixtures and other surfaces that sit far above the floor in large occupied buildings. Buyers include warehouse and plant managers, venue operators, airport authorities, retail facility teams and school districts.

Overhead dust can fall into food, products, equipment and people below. In some plants it is also a fire and explosion hazard. Specialists bring lifts and operators, dust control methods, scheduling around operations and knowledge of the standards that apply to the facility.

Questions about overhead cleaning

Is overhead cleaning the same as post-construction cleaning?

No. The services in this family are recurring maintenance in occupied, operating buildings. Post-construction cleaning removes construction dust once, before turnover, and is listed separately.

How is the area below protected during overhead work?

Contractors usually cover equipment and product, set up exclusion zones and use HEPA vacuums instead of blowing dust. Ask for a written plan that shows how falling debris and dust will be controlled.

How often should overhead areas be cleaned?

It depends on dust load and what is below. Food plants and combustible dust facilities often set frequencies by inspection results, while retail and offices may clean once or twice a year.
