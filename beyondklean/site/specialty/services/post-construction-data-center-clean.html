Post-construction data center clean before commissioning

Final multi-phase cleaning of new or expanded data halls to remove construction dust before equipment is powered and commissioned.

Also called: data center construction clean, pre-commissioning clean, new data hall cleaning, fit-out clean

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

New data halls contain construction dust, drywall dust, metal shavings and packaging debris that can damage equipment once airflow starts. A post-construction clean is done in phases: rough clean after trades finish, a detailed clean of overhead structure, cable trays, busways, ceiling plenums and subfloor, then a final clean right before IT equipment arrives. Particle counts verify that the space meets the ISO 14644-1 class in the owner's specification. Contractors coordinate with commissioning teams so testing such as load bank work does not re-dirty the space. Controlled entry with sticky mats and shoe covers keeps the space clean after sign-off.

When you need it

  • New data hall construction or expansion
  • Before commissioning or load bank testing
  • Before installing IT equipment
  • After major mechanical or electrical retrofits

Credentials to ask for

  • Experience with data center construction phasing and commissioning schedules
  • Calibrated particle counters and ISO 14644-1 reporting
  • Construction site safety training, such as OSHA 10 or 30-hour
  • MEWP training for overhead structure and busway cleaning

What the scope includes

  • Rough clean after trade work
  • Detailed overhead, cable tray, busway and plenum cleaning
  • Subfloor cleaning before final tile installation
  • Final clean and controlled entry setup
  • Particle count verification and turnover report

How it is priced

Usually priced per square foot or lump sum per phase. Hall size, overhead complexity, subfloor depth, schedule pressure and the number of cleaning passes drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How many phases do you plan, and when?
  • What ISO class will you verify to?
  • How will you keep the space clean after you finish?
  • Can you work with our commissioning agent's schedule?

Questions about post-construction data center clean before commissioning

Why not use a regular construction cleaner?

General construction cleaning does not usually address subfloors, cable trays or particle count targets. Data halls need methods and testing that match equipment requirements.

When should the final clean happen?

As close as possible to IT equipment delivery, after trades finish. Late work by trades can re-dirty the space.

Who sets the cleanliness target?

The owner's specification, often based on hardware maker and ASHRAE guidance. Contractors verify it with particle counts.
