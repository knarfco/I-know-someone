Commercial roof cleaning

Cleaning of TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen and metal roofs to restore reflectivity, support inspections and protect roof warranties.

Also called: low-slope roof cleaning, membrane roof cleaning, cool roof cleaning, metal roof washing

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Commercial roof cleaning removes dirt, biological growth, exhaust residue and debris from low-slope and metal roofs. Reflective cool roofs lose solar reflectance as they get dirty, which can raise cooling load. Cleaning also makes membrane defects visible for inspection. Methods must follow the roofing manufacturer's guidance, which often limits pressure, water temperature and cleaners. Kitchen exhaust grease on membranes needs degreasers that will not harm the membrane. Workers near unprotected roof edges need fall protection under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.28, such as guardrails, warning lines or personal fall arrest.

When you need it

  • Annual or semiannual roof maintenance and inspection
  • Loss of reflectivity on white or cool roofs
  • Grease buildup around restaurant or kitchen exhaust fans
  • Before a warranty inspection or roof condition survey

Credentials to ask for

  • A written fall protection plan meeting OSHA 29 CFR 1910.28 for roof edges and skylights
  • Cleaning methods approved by the roof membrane manufacturer
  • Wastewater handling that follows local stormwater rules
  • Insurance covering roof damage and work at height

What the scope includes

  • Review of roof type, warranty terms and manufacturer cleaning limits
  • Fall protection setup, including skylight guarding
  • Debris removal, then low-pressure washing with approved cleaners
  • Degreasing around exhaust fans where needed
  • Photo report of membrane damage, ponding or failed seams

How it is priced

Usually priced per square foot of roof area. Drivers include roof size, membrane type, soiling, grease areas, access and wastewater capture.

Ask before you hire

  • Have you checked our roof manufacturer's cleaning requirements?
  • How will you protect workers near skylights and edges?
  • Will you document roof damage you find?

Questions about commercial roof cleaning

Can cleaning void a roof warranty?

It can if the methods are not approved by the manufacturer. Ask the contractor to confirm pressure limits and cleaners in writing before work starts.

Does a clean white roof really save energy?

A dirty reflective roof absorbs more heat. Cleaning can restore some of the lost reflectance, though results vary by membrane age and climate.

Are skylights a fall hazard?

Yes. OSHA treats skylights as holes that need guarding or covers. Workers have been killed falling through them.
