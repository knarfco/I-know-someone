Cleanroom cleaning and controlled environment housekeeping

Cleaning of ISO-classified cleanrooms using validated methods, cleanroom garments and approved agents set by the facility's own procedures.

Also called: clean room cleaning, controlled environment cleaning, ISO classified room cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Cleanroom cleaning follows the facility's written procedures, which set the agents, tools, sequence and frequency. ISO 14644-5 covers cleanroom operations, and guidance such as IEST-RP-CC018 addresses cleaning practices. Crews gown to the room's requirements, use low-lint wipes and mops, and clean from the cleanest areas toward the exits and from top to bottom. Pharmaceutical and compounding cleanrooms add rules such as FDA current good manufacturing practice, EU GMP Annex 1 or USP <797> and <800>, including rotation of disinfectants and sporicides. Because those rules vary, a buyer should match the contractor's experience to the specific type of cleanroom.

When you need it

  • Routine daily, weekly or monthly cleaning in classified spaces
  • Shutdown cleaning before requalification
  • Excursions in environmental monitoring results
  • New cleanroom startup after construction

Credentials to ask for

  • Training on ISO 14644 cleanroom practices and the facility's own SOPs
  • Gowning qualification as required by the facility
  • Experience in the relevant regulated setting (semiconductor, pharmaceutical, compounding pharmacy or medical device)
  • Documentation practices that meet the facility's quality system

What the scope includes

  • Review of facility SOPs, agents and gowning rules
  • Training and gowning qualification of crew members
  • Cleaning in the required sequence with approved agents and tools
  • Completion logs signed according to the quality system
  • Support for environmental monitoring or requalification as directed

How it is priced

Usually priced per shift, per room or per month under a service agreement. Room class, gowning level, frequency and documentation requirements drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Which cleanroom types and classes have you worked in?
  • Will you follow our SOPs, or do you bring your own?
  • How are your crews trained and gowning-qualified?

Questions about cleanroom cleaning and controlled environment housekeeping

Is cleanroom cleaning the same as data center cleaning?

No. Cleanrooms often have stricter classes, gowning rules and validated procedures. Data center cleaning borrows ISO 14644 class terms but is usually less strict.

Who decides which disinfectants to use?

The facility, through its quality system and SOPs. Contractors follow them.

Can any janitorial company clean a cleanroom?

Only if trained to the facility's procedures. Untrained crews can contaminate the space and cause monitoring failures.
