Telecom, switch room and network closet cleaning

Static-safe cleaning of central offices, carrier hotels, switch rooms and network closets, including cable racks and equipment frames.

Also called: central office cleaning, MDF/IDF room cleaning, telecom room cleaning, network closet cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Telecom spaces range from carrier central offices with overhead cable racking to small network closets in office buildings. Dust collects on equipment frames, overhead cable racks, battery stands and floors, and these rooms often lack the filtration of a full data center. Cleaning follows the same static-safe approach: HEPA vacuums, ESD-safe tools, no contact with equipment internals and no disturbance of cables or fiber. Battery rooms add hazards from electrolyte and energized terminals, so crews stay clear of terminals and follow site rules. Carrier sites may require escorts or security clearance. Network closets often double as storage, so clutter removal may be part of the scope.

When you need it

  • Dust buildup on frames and cable racks
  • Equipment overheating in poorly filtered rooms
  • Before network upgrades or audits
  • Multi-site network maintenance programs

Credentials to ask for

  • ESD control training and static-safe tools
  • Awareness of battery room hazards and site electrical safety rules
  • Background checks or clearances required by the carrier or owner
  • Experience with multi-site scheduling

What the scope includes

  • Access and escort coordination
  • HEPA vacuuming of floors, frames and overhead cable racks
  • Wiping of frame and cabinet exteriors
  • Removal of clutter and packaging where approved
  • Site log and photo report

How it is priced

Usually priced per room or per site visit, or per square foot for large central offices. Room count, travel, escort needs and overhead racking drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How do you work around battery systems?
  • Can you cover all our network closets on one schedule?
  • Will you remove stored items, or only clean?

Questions about telecom, switch room and network closet cleaning

Do small network closets need professional cleaning?

They often have poor airflow and filtration, so dust builds up quickly. Periodic cleaning can help equipment run cooler.

Can fiber be cleaned by these crews?

Fiber connector cleaning is usually done by network technicians, not cleaning crews. Crews avoid touching fiber.

Are battery rooms dangerous?

Batteries can hold high energy and may contain corrosive electrolyte. Crews keep clear of terminals and follow site rules.
