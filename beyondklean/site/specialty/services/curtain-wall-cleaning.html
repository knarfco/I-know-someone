Curtain wall cleaning

Cleaning of glass, spandrel panels and aluminum framing on curtain wall systems while protecting gaskets, seals and coatings.

Also called: curtain wall maintenance cleaning, glass curtain wall washing, unitized facade cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

A curtain wall is a non-structural skin of glass, spandrel panels and aluminum mullions hung from the building frame. Cleaning it covers more than vision glass: anodized or painted aluminum framing, spandrel glass, sunshades and fins all collect dirt and pollutants. The contractor uses cleaners that are safe for coated glass, finished aluminum and gaskets, and avoids high pressure that can push water past seals. Crews also look for failed sealant, loose pressure caps and blocked weep holes, which often show up first as streaks or staining during cleaning.

When you need it

  • Regular maintenance cycles on glass-clad towers
  • Streaking or staining appears below mullions or weep holes
  • Before a facade inspection so inspectors can see sealant and gasket condition
  • After nearby construction, wildfire smoke or heavy pollen seasons

Credentials to ask for

  • Access method training matched to the building (IWCA I-14.1, SPRAT/IRATA, or powered platform training)
  • Written methods that follow the glass and framing manufacturer's cleaning guidance
  • Insurance covering work at height and damage to the building envelope

What the scope includes

  • Review of the curtain wall system type, coatings and any manufacturer cleaning limits
  • Test cleaning in a small area to confirm the chemistry is safe for the finishes
  • Cleaning of vision glass, spandrel, mullions, caps and sunshades
  • Low-pressure rinse to avoid driving water past gaskets
  • Written report of sealant, gasket and weep hole issues observed

How it is priced

Typically priced per cleaning cycle or per square foot of facade. Cost depends on the area of glass and framing, number of drops, access equipment and the amount of fin or sunshade detail.

Ask before you hire

  • What products will you use on our anodized or painted aluminum?
  • How do you keep water from being forced past gaskets?
  • Will you report sealant or gasket problems you see?

Questions about curtain wall cleaning

Does curtain wall cleaning include the metal framing?

It should, but confirm in writing. Some window-only contracts clean the glass and leave mullions and caps dirty. A full curtain wall scope names the framing, spandrel and shading elements.

Can cleaning damage the curtain wall?

Yes, if the wrong chemicals or pressure are used. Acidic or abrasive products can etch coatings and finishes, and high pressure can force water into the wall. Test areas help prevent this.

Why do streaks appear below the mullions?

Dirt collects on horizontal caps and washes down in the rain. Streaks can also come from water leaving weep holes. Regular cleaning of the framing reduces this pattern.
