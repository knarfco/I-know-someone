Smoke odor removal

Treating lingering smoke odor after cleaning with air scrubbers, thermal fogging, hydroxyl generators, ozone or sealers.

Also called: fire odor removal, thermal fogging, ozone treatment, hydroxyl odor treatment

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Smoke odor removal works only after the residue causing it has been cleaned or removed. Remaining odor is then treated. Thermal fogging releases a fine deodorizing mist that follows the same paths smoke did. Hydroxyl generators break down odor molecules and can run in occupied space. Ozone generators work faster but ozone harms lungs and can degrade rubber and some fabrics, so treated spaces must be empty and ventilated before reentry. OSHA limits worker ozone exposure to 0.1 ppm as an eight-hour average. Sealing primers lock in odor on structure that cannot be fully cleaned.

When you need it

  • Odor persists after residue cleaning
  • Wildfire or neighboring-fire smoke entered the building
  • Occupants or tenants report odor before move-back
  • Odor rises on warm or humid days

Credentials to ask for

  • IICRC Odor Control Technician (OCT) certification
  • Written safety procedures for ozone and fogging, including occupancy controls
  • Awareness of the OSHA ozone exposure limit in 29 CFR 1910.1000
  • Air scrubbers with HEPA and carbon filtration

What the scope includes

  • Confirm odor sources have been cleaned or removed
  • Select treatment method based on occupancy and materials
  • Run treatment with posted access controls
  • Seal surfaces where needed
  • Ventilate and check odor before reentry

How it is priced

Usually priced per room or per square foot treated, or by equipment day. Cost depends on space volume, odor strength, method and number of treatment cycles.

Ask before you hire

  • Have all odor sources been removed first?
  • Will you use ozone, and how will you keep people out and confirm it is safe to return?
  • How many treatment cycles do you expect?

Questions about smoke odor removal

Is ozone safe to use?

Ozone is a lung irritant. It should be used only in unoccupied spaces, with people, pets and plants removed, and followed by ventilation before reentry.

Why does odor come back on hot days?

Heat and humidity release odor from residue left in hidden cavities. That usually means a source was missed.

What is the difference between fogging and air scrubbing?

Air scrubbers filter particles and some odor from the air. Fogging places deodorizer on surfaces and in cracks where smoke settled.
