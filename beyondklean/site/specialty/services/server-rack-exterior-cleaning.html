Server rack and cabinet exterior cleaning

Static-safe cleaning of the outside of server racks, cabinets, doors, blanking panels and cable managers without disturbing equipment.

Also called: rack cleaning, cabinet cleaning, server enclosure cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Rack exteriors collect dust on perforated doors, tops, blanking panels and cable managers. Dust on perforated doors can reduce airflow to servers. Cleaning uses HEPA vacuums with ESD-safe brushes, static-dissipative wipes and approved cleaners on the outside of enclosures. Crews do not open equipment or touch internal components unless the owner gives written approval. They avoid unplugging cables or bumping power switches. In colocation sites, cabinets belong to different customers, so access must be authorized for each one.

When you need it

  • Dust buildup on perforated doors and cabinet tops
  • Before customer tours or audits
  • As part of recurring white space cleaning
  • After construction or maintenance nearby

Credentials to ask for

  • ESD control training and static-safe tools
  • Written procedures for working near live equipment
  • Authorization process for customer cabinets in colocation sites

What the scope includes

  • Confirmation of which cabinets are in scope and authorized
  • HEPA vacuuming of doors, tops and blanking panels
  • Wiping of exteriors with approved products
  • Check that doors are closed and secured
  • Log of cabinets cleaned

How it is priced

Usually priced per rack or cabinet. Cabinet count, door type, access restrictions and escort requirements drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Do you open cabinet doors, and how do you get approval?
  • What tools do you use on perforated doors?
  • How do you avoid bumping power or cables?

Questions about server rack and cabinet exterior cleaning

Is it safe to clean racks with equipment running?

Yes, when crews use static-safe methods and stay on the outside of enclosures. Internal work needs owner approval.

Why clean rack doors?

Dust on perforated doors can reduce airflow and make equipment run warmer.

What about colocation customers?

Each customer's cabinet should only be cleaned with authorization. Most sites manage this through their ticketing system.
