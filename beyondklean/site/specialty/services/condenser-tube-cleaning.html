Condenser tube cleaning

Cleaning of steam surface condenser and large heat exchanger tubes with projectiles, brushes or hydroblasting to restore vacuum and efficiency.

Also called: surface condenser cleaning, condenser waterbox cleaning, tube projectile cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Condenser tubes foul with silt, biofilm, scale and debris from cooling water, which hurts vacuum and plant heat rate. Cleaning is done during outages through opened waterboxes. Common methods are shooting projectiles or scrapers through tubes with water pressure, rotary brushes, and hydroblasting for hard deposits. Waterboxes are often confined spaces, so entry rules apply. Debris such as shells and mussels is removed from tube sheets and boxes. Many owners pair cleaning with eddy current testing and tube plugging, and the contractor reports tubes that could not be cleared.

When you need it

  • Condenser vacuum or heat rate has worsened
  • Before eddy current tube testing
  • Macrofouling from shells or debris
  • Planned unit outage

Credentials to ask for

  • Confined space training per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 for waterbox entry
  • Experience with tube materials and projectile selection
  • OSHA 1910.269 plant training
  • High pressure waterjetting training where hydroblasting is used

What the scope includes

  • Open waterboxes and ventilate
  • Remove debris from tube sheets and boxes
  • Clean tubes by projectile, brush or hydroblast
  • Record tubes that could not be cleaned
  • Clean waterbox surfaces
  • Prepare for testing or closure

How it is priced

Often priced per tube or per condenser half, or by crew-shift. Tube count, deposit type and access drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Which method suits our tube material and deposits?
  • Will you collect and weigh debris for trending?
  • Do you coordinate with eddy current testers?
  • How do you handle plugged or failed tubes?

Questions about condenser tube cleaning

What is projectile cleaning?

Plastic or metal scrapers are shot through tubes with water pressure, scraping deposits as they pass. It is fast and suits most soft fouling.

Will cleaning damage thin-wall tubes?

Proper projectile and pressure choices avoid damage. Aggressive tools are used only when needed and after checking tube material.

Can condensers be cleaned online?

Some plants have online ball cleaning systems. These reduce fouling but usually do not replace outage cleaning.
