Chiller and condenser tube cleaning

Mechanical cleaning of chiller condenser and evaporator tubes to remove scale and fouling and restore heat transfer.

Also called: chiller tube brushing, condenser tube cleaning, heat exchanger tube cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Chiller tube cleaning restores heat transfer by removing scale, mud and biological fouling from the water side of condenser and evaporator tube bundles. After the chiller is shut down, isolated and drained, the crew removes the waterbox covers and runs rotating brushes or projectiles through each tube while flushing with water. Hard scale may need a controlled chemical descaling step. Tubes are inspected, and many owners schedule eddy current testing at the same time to find thinning or pitting. Gaskets are replaced and waterboxes are closed and torqued to the manufacturer's values. The work is usually timed with seasonal shutdowns.

When you need it

  • High condenser approach temperature or rising head pressure
  • Annual shutdown maintenance before cooling season
  • Water treatment problems that caused scale or fouling
  • Before eddy current testing of the tube bundle
  • Rising chiller energy use with no change in load

Credentials to ask for

  • Experience with the chiller manufacturer's maintenance procedures
  • Lockout/tagout training under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147
  • Rigging training for removing heavy waterbox covers
  • State mechanical or refrigeration license where required

What the scope includes

  • Shutdown, isolation and draining of the water side
  • Removal of waterbox covers and inspection of tube sheets
  • Brush or projectile cleaning of each tube with flushing
  • Optional chemical descaling and eddy current testing
  • New gaskets, reassembly and leak check
  • Report on tube condition and approach temperature after restart

How it is priced

Usually priced per chiller or per bundle, sometimes per tube for large jobs. Tube count, fouling type, waterbox access, rigging needs and add-on testing drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Will you clean both condenser and evaporator bundles?
  • How will you handle hard scale that brushing does not remove?
  • Can you perform or coordinate eddy current testing?
  • How long will the chiller be out of service?

Questions about chiller and condenser tube cleaning

Why does tube fouling matter?

A thin layer of scale insulates the tube and forces the compressor to work harder. Cleaning lowers approach temperature and energy use.

Is this the same as coil cleaning?

No. Coils transfer heat between air and fluid. Chiller tubes transfer heat between water and refrigerant inside a pressure vessel.

Does the refrigerant circuit get opened?

Not for water-side tube cleaning. Refrigerant work is separate and needs EPA Section 608 certified technicians.
