Mixer, blender and tank exterior cleaning

Exterior cleaning of mixers, blenders, process tanks, platforms and piping to remove spilled product, dust and grime without entering the vessel.

Also called: process vessel exterior cleaning, mixer cleaning, tank shell cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

This service covers the outside of process equipment: tank shells, mixer drives and gearboxes, agitator mounts, platforms, ladders, nozzles and nearby piping. It does not include vessel entry, which is a separate confined space service. Crews choose methods to suit the coating and insulation, such as hand wiping, foam cleaners, low-pressure washing or dry ice. Insulated tanks need care so water does not soak the insulation and cause corrosion under insulation. Motors and drives are covered or cleaned dry. Work at height on platforms or lifts follows the site's fall protection rules.

When you need it

  • Spilled powder or liquid product has built up on equipment exteriors
  • Before a customer or regulatory inspection
  • Ahead of painting or insulation repair
  • During planned shutdowns when the area is quiet

Credentials to ask for

  • Hazard communication training and knowledge of the products being cleaned off
  • Fall protection and aerial lift training
  • Lockout of mixer drives per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 when cleaning near moving parts
  • Hot work and site safety orientation for chemical plants

What the scope includes

  • Identify surfaces, insulation and electrical items that need protection
  • Lock out agitator drives and cover motors
  • Clean from top platforms down to the base
  • Clean ladders, handrails and grating
  • Collect wash water and residue for proper disposal

How it is priced

Priced per vessel or by crew-day. Number and height of vessels, access needs, type of residue and water collection requirements drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How will you keep water out of insulation and electrical enclosures?
  • Will you need lifts or scaffolding, and who supplies them?
  • How will you collect and dispose of wash water?
  • What will you do if you find leaks or damaged coating?

Questions about mixer, blender and tank exterior cleaning

Does exterior cleaning include inside the tank?

No. Interior cleaning usually requires confined space entry or automated tank cleaning equipment and is quoted separately.

Can pressure washing damage tank coatings?

High pressure can lift paint at chipped edges and drive water into insulation. Low pressure with the right detergent usually cleans without that risk.

Should product residue be tested before cleaning?

If the residue is unknown or may be hazardous, yes. The contractor needs the safety data sheet or test results to choose PPE and plan disposal.
