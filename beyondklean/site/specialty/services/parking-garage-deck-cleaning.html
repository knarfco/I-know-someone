Parking garage deck cleaning

Scrubbing and washing of parking decks, ramps and stairwells to remove oil, de-icing salts and grime while controlling wastewater.

Also called: parking structure power washing, garage scrubbing, parking deck degreasing

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Parking garage cleaning removes oil drips, tire residue, sand and chloride de-icing salts from decks, ramps, stairs and elevator lobbies. Salt and water that reach reinforcing steel speed up corrosion, so many structural engineers recommend periodic washing, especially after winter. Crews use ride-on scrubbers, surface cleaners and hot-water pressure washers, working level by level while traffic is rerouted. Wash water usually contains oil and solids. Most cities do not allow it in storm drains, so it is collected and sent through an oil-water separator or vacuum recovery and disposed of according to local rules. Joint sealants and traffic coatings must be protected.

When you need it

  • Spring washdown to remove winter de-icing salts
  • Oil staining and slippery areas near entrances and ramps
  • Before a structural condition assessment or coating repair
  • Routine monthly or quarterly maintenance in high-traffic garages

Credentials to ask for

  • Knowledge of local stormwater and sewer discharge rules for oily wash water
  • Equipment for wastewater recovery or confirmed use of the garage's oil-water separator
  • Traffic control plans for work in occupied garages
  • Experience with traffic-bearing membranes and joint sealants

What the scope includes

  • Sweeping and debris removal
  • Pre-treatment of oil stains and high-traffic lanes
  • Scrubbing or hot-water pressure washing level by level
  • Wastewater recovery and disposal as allowed by local rules
  • Cleaning of stairs, lobbies and drains, with a report of damaged coatings

How it is priced

Usually priced per square foot of deck or per parking space, sometimes per level. Drivers include deck area, staining, wastewater recovery, overnight or weekend timing and traffic control.

Ask before you hire

  • Where will the wash water go?
  • Will your equipment damage our traffic coating or joint sealants?
  • Can you work overnight to keep the garage open?

Questions about parking garage deck cleaning

Why wash a garage after winter?

De-icing salts carried in on vehicles soak into concrete and can reach reinforcing steel. Washing helps remove chlorides before they cause more corrosion.

Can we just hose it into the drains?

Usually not. Garage wash water carries oil, metals and solids. Many cities require it to go through a separator or be collected, so check local rules.

How long does a garage stay closed?

Most contractors work one level or section at a time so the garage stays open. Overnight work is common.
