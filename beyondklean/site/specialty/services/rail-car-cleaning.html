Rail car cleaning

Exterior washing and interior cleaning of passenger coaches, freight hoppers and tank cars, including residue removal before repair.

Also called: railcar washing, tank car cleaning, passenger coach cleaning, hopper car washout

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Rail car cleaning covers passenger coaches, freight hoppers and tank cars. Passenger work includes exterior washing, graffiti removal and interior detailing. Freight cars are cleaned to remove product residue before a new load or repair. Tank cars that carried hazardous materials stay regulated under DOT rules until cleaned and purged. Interior entry into tank cars is permit-required confined space work. Work in rail yards needs blue signal protection so cars are not moved while crews are on or under them.

When you need it

  • Before tank car repair, inspection or requalification
  • Commodity changes that require clean cars
  • Passenger cars due for scheduled cleaning
  • Graffiti or vandalism on rolling stock

Credentials to ask for

  • Blue signal protection procedures under 49 CFR Part 218 Subpart B when working on or under cars
  • Permit-required confined space program under 29 CFR 1910.146 for tank car entry
  • Wastewater permits, including 40 CFR Part 442 Subpart B for rail tank car cleaning where it applies
  • DOT hazmat training for crews handling residue tank cars

What the scope includes

  • Confirm car contents, last commodity and safety data
  • Set blue signal protection
  • Drain residue, wash and ventilate tank cars
  • Wash out hoppers or clean coach interiors and exteriors
  • Collect and dispose of residue and wash water
  • Issue cleaning certificates

How it is priced

Usually priced per car, with surcharges for difficult commodities or heavy residue. Cost depends on car type, commodity, waste handling and facility location.

Ask before you hire

  • Which commodities can you clean, and which are excluded?
  • Do you operate a permitted cleaning facility?
  • How do you handle blue signal protection?
  • What does your cleaning certificate show?

Questions about rail car cleaning

Is an empty tank car still hazardous?

Yes. A tank car that held hazardous material is treated as hazardous until cleaned and purged of residue. DOT rules cover how it is shipped and handled.

Can passenger cars be cleaned in the yard?

Yes, if blue signal protection is in place and wash water is handled under the yard's permits. Interior work can often be done during layovers.

Who regulates rail cleaning wastewater?

EPA's Transportation Equipment Cleaning guidelines and local pretreatment rules apply to many rail tank car cleaning facilities. Exterior-only washing is handled under general stormwater and sewer rules.
