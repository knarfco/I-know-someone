Mercury spill cleanup

Containment, recovery and vapor-level clearance after spills of elemental mercury from instruments, equipment or flooring.

Also called: elemental mercury cleanup, mercury decontamination, mercury vapor cleanup

Licensed, regulated work. Only properly licensed and certified specialists may perform it. Requirements vary by state and agency, so confirm credentials before hiring.

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Mercury spill cleanup recovers liquid mercury and reduces mercury vapor to safe levels. Spills come from broken thermometers, manometers, switches, dental equipment and some older rubberized gym floors. A contractor isolates the area, lowers the room temperature where possible, and uses a mercury vapor analyzer to map contamination. Beads are recovered with mercury vacuums, amalgam powders or sponges, never a regular vacuum. Porous materials like carpet are often removed. The space is ventilated and re-screened until vapor readings meet the target set by the health department or consultant. Recovered mercury is managed as hazardous or universal waste.

When you need it

  • Broken mercury thermometer or manometer in a lab, school or clinic
  • Mercury found under old equipment during renovation
  • Removal of mercury-containing polyurethane gym flooring
  • Spill tracked into carpet or HVAC returns

Credentials to ask for

  • OSHA HAZWOPER 29 CFR 1910.120 training where the cleanup falls under that standard
  • Use of a calibrated mercury vapor analyzer and staff trained to read it
  • RCRA hazardous waste and universal waste handling knowledge (40 CFR Parts 262 and 273)
  • State and local health department involvement may be required; requirements vary by state

What the scope includes

  • Isolate the area and shut down or isolate HVAC
  • Vapor screening to map contamination
  • Recover visible mercury and remove contaminated porous materials
  • Treat cracks and surfaces with amalgamating or binding agents
  • Ventilate and re-screen until clearance levels are met
  • Package and dispose of mercury waste with documentation

How it is priced

Usually time and materials or a project price based on spill size, surface types, area affected and screening rounds. Flooring removal and waste disposal add cost.

Ask before you hire

  • What vapor level will you use for clearance, and who set it?
  • Will you use a real-time mercury vapor analyzer?
  • How will you handle carpet and other porous materials?
  • How will the mercury waste be classified and shipped?

Questions about mercury spill cleanup

Why not use a regular vacuum on a mercury spill?

A regular vacuum heats and spreads mercury vapor, contaminating the vacuum and the air. Only equipment designed for mercury should be used.

Is a broken fluorescent lamp a mercury spill?

It releases a small amount of mercury, and EPA publishes cleanup steps for broken lamps. Large numbers of broken lamps in a facility may call for a contractor.

Can mercury contaminate a whole building?

Yes, if it is tracked on shoes or drawn into ventilation. Early isolation and screening limit the spread.
