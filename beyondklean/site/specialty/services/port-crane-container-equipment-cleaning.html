Port crane and container handling equipment cleaning

Degreasing and washing ship-to-shore cranes, rubber-tired gantries, straddle carriers and reach stackers for inspection and corrosion control.

Also called: STS crane cleaning, gantry crane washing, straddle carrier cleaning, RTG crane cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Port cranes and container handlers carry grease, salt and dust that hide cracks and speed corrosion. Cleaning removes old grease from sheaves, rails, trolleys and spreaders, and washes salt from booms, legs and machinery houses. Work happens at height, so crews use the crane's own access ways, aerial lifts or rope access. Equipment must be locked out and coordinated with operations. Grease and wash water must be captured, since crane rails often sit right at the wharf edge.

When you need it

  • Before structural or non-destructive testing inspections
  • Ahead of painting or corrosion repair
  • During planned maintenance windows
  • After a hydraulic oil leak on a crane or spreader

Credentials to ask for

  • Fall protection training and equipment for work at height
  • SPRAT or IRATA certification where rope access is used
  • Lockout and tagout procedures coordinated with the terminal
  • Aerial lift operator training

What the scope includes

  • Agree on a lockout plan and work window with operations
  • Set up containment for grease and wash water
  • Scrape and degrease sheaves, rails and spreaders
  • Wash salt from structure and machinery houses
  • Report cracks, corrosion or leaks found during work

How it is priced

Usually priced per crane or per machine, or by crew-day for larger programs. Cost depends on equipment size, access method, grease buildup and how much downtime the terminal allows.

Ask before you hire

  • How will you reach high areas, and who is trained for it?
  • How will you capture grease and wash water at the wharf edge?
  • How long will each crane be out of service?

Questions about port crane and container handling equipment cleaning

Why clean before inspection?

Inspectors need bare metal to find cracks and corrosion. Grease and dirt can hide defects in welds and pins.

Can crane cleaning damage electrical systems?

High-pressure water near motors, drives and junction boxes can cause faults. Crews should cover or avoid electrical components and follow the crane maker's guidance.

Is rope access safe for crane work?

Rope access is a recognized method when performed by certified technicians under SPRAT or IRATA practices. Each job should have a written rescue plan and a technician qualified to supervise.
