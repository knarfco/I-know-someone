VAV box and diffuser cleaning

Cleaning of VAV terminal boxes, reheat coils, diffusers and grilles where dust collects at the end of the air system.

Also called: terminal unit cleaning, register and grille cleaning, air distribution cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

VAV box and diffuser cleaning focuses on the terminal end of the air system, where velocity drops and dust settles. Technicians open the access panel on each terminal unit, vacuum the interior, clean the damper, and brush or vacuum the reheat coil. Flow sensors are cleaned carefully so the box still reads airflow correctly. Diffusers, grilles and registers are removed, washed or wiped, and reinstalled. Ceiling work above occupied space is done with drop cloths and portable HEPA vacuums. The contractor should coordinate with the controls vendor if boxes need recalibration after cleaning.

When you need it

  • Smudging on ceilings around diffusers
  • Hot or cold complaints traced to dirty reheat coils or sensors
  • Tenant fit-out or office refresh
  • Building commissioning or recommissioning
  • After duct cleaning, to finish the system

Credentials to ask for

  • ACR, the NADCA Standard, for air distribution components
  • HVAC controls knowledge or coordination with the building controls contractor
  • Ladder and lift training; OSHA fall protection where applicable
  • Insurance covering work in occupied offices

What the scope includes

  • Inventory and access of terminal units
  • Vacuuming of box interiors and dampers
  • Cleaning of reheat coils and airflow sensors
  • Removal and cleaning of diffusers, grilles and registers
  • Reinstallation, ceiling tile replacement and cleanup

How it is priced

Usually priced per VAV box and per diffuser. Box count, ceiling height and type, after-hours access and whether controls recalibration is included drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How will you protect furniture and floors below the work?
  • Will you clean the airflow sensors, and how?
  • Who handles recalibration if a box reads incorrectly afterward?
  • Can you work floor by floor after hours?

Questions about vav box and diffuser cleaning

Why do diffusers leave black marks on ceilings?

Air leaving the diffuser carries fine dust that deposits on the ceiling around it. Cleaning helps, but filtration upstream is the long-term fix.

Can VAV boxes be cleaned without duct cleaning?

Yes. Terminal units are a common standalone job when inspection shows main ducts are in good condition.

Will cleaning affect comfort control?

It often improves it. Dirty sensors and coils cause poor temperature control.
