Warehouse ceiling and roof structure cleaning

Cleaning of roof deck, bar joists, trusses, sprinkler lines and lighting in warehouses and distribution centers without stopping operations.

Also called: warehouse high cleaning, bar joist cleaning, roof deck cleaning, distribution center overhead cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Warehouses collect dust, cardboard fibers, forklift exhaust residue and bird debris on roof decks, bar joists, sprinkler piping and light fixtures. Cleaning is done from scissor lifts or boom lifts, working aisle by aisle between rack runs, usually on night or weekend shifts. Crews HEPA-vacuum structure and piping and protect stored goods and conveyors below with covers. Sprinkler heads are not sprayed, painted or bumped, since contaminated or damaged heads must be replaced under NFPA 25. The contractor coordinates lift routes with racking, automation and traffic patterns.

When you need it

  • Dust falling onto stored product or conveyors
  • Customer, retailer or food-grade warehouse audits
  • Before repainting the roof deck or installing new lighting
  • Bird activity in the structure

Credentials to ask for

  • MEWP operator training per ANSI/SAIA A92.24
  • A written plan for protecting stored product and conveyors
  • Awareness of NFPA 25 limits on contact with sprinkler heads
  • Insurance that covers damage to inventory and building systems

What the scope includes

  • Survey of ceiling height, rack layout and lift access routes
  • Covering of product and equipment below the work zone
  • HEPA vacuuming of roof deck, joists, piping and fixtures
  • Care around sprinkler heads, with damage reported immediately
  • Floor cleanup and zone sign-off

How it is priced

Usually priced per square foot of floor area or by the shift. Ceiling height, rack density, product protection and off-hours work drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How will you protect our inventory below?
  • What will you do if a sprinkler head is damaged or dirty?
  • Can you work around our shift schedule?

Questions about warehouse ceiling and roof structure cleaning

Can you clean over full racks?

Yes, with covers and careful vacuuming. Some zones may need product moved, depending on its sensitivity.

Why be careful with sprinkler heads?

Heads that are painted, loaded with dust or damaged may not work properly. NFPA 25 calls for replacing them, so contractors avoid contact.

How long does a large warehouse take?

It depends on height and layout. Contractors usually plan by zone over several shifts to avoid disrupting operations.
