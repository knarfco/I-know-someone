Overhead Cleaning · Specialty

High dusting programs

Scheduled dusting of vents, beams, ledges, pipes and fixtures above reach height, using HEPA vacuums and lifts in occupied buildings.

Also called: high dusting, overhead dusting service, high level cleaning, ceiling dusting

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

A high dusting program sets a recurring schedule for cleaning surfaces above about eight feet: diffusers, return grilles, pipes, beams, ledges, signs and fixtures. Crews use HEPA-filtered vacuums with extension wands, microfiber tools and lifts where needed. Vacuuming is preferred over dry dusting because it captures dust instead of spreading it. The program defines zones, frequencies and inspection points so the work is documented and repeatable. Most work is done at night or on weekends to avoid disrupting occupants.

When you need it

  • Visible dust on diffusers, ledges or pipes
  • Health or brand inspections that include overhead surfaces
  • Dust falling onto desks, merchandise or patients
  • As part of an infection prevention or housekeeping plan

Credentials to ask for

  • Aerial lift operator training per ANSI/SAIA A92.24 and OSHA requirements
  • HEPA-filtered vacuum equipment
  • A written zone map and frequency schedule
  • For healthcare, familiarity with infection control risk assessment (ICRA) practices

What the scope includes

  • Walk-through to map zones, heights and access needs
  • Agreement on frequencies and inspection criteria
  • Protection of furniture, product and equipment below
  • HEPA vacuuming and wipe-down of overhead surfaces
  • Logged completion and photos by zone

How it is priced

Usually priced per visit or per square foot of floor area served, often on an annual contract. Drivers are ceiling height, density of pipes and fixtures, access equipment and off-hours scheduling.

Ask before you hire

  • Do you use HEPA vacuums or dry dusting?
  • How do you document what was cleaned each visit?
  • Can you work during our off-hours?

Questions about high dusting programs

Why HEPA vacuums instead of dusters?

Dusters and blowers move dust around and let it settle again. HEPA vacuums capture fine particles so they do not fall back.

What counts as high dusting?

Most programs start at surfaces above normal reach, around eight feet. The exact line is set in the contract.

Can high dusting be done while the building is open?

Some low-risk areas can, but most work is scheduled after hours for safety and to avoid dust falling on people.
