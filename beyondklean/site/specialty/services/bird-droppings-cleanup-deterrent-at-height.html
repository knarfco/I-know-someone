Bird droppings cleanup and bird deterrent installation at height

Safe removal of accumulated bird droppings from ledges, canopies and structures, with deterrents to stop roosting and nesting.

Also called: pigeon droppings removal, guano cleanup, bird control installation, bird spike and netting installation

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Accumulated bird droppings on ledges, canopies, beams and signs corrode materials and can carry fungi linked to histoplasmosis and cryptococcosis. Removal starts with wetting the material to keep dust down. Workers wear respiratory protection and disposable coveralls, and dry sweeping is avoided. NIOSH publication 2005-109 on histoplasmosis describes these precautions. After cleaning and disinfecting, crews install deterrents such as netting, spikes, wire systems or slope covers. Pigeons, house sparrows and European starlings are not protected under the Migratory Bird Treaty Act. Many other species are, and active nests of protected birds cannot be disturbed without authorization.

When you need it

  • Heavy droppings on ledges, canopies, signs or loading docks
  • Bird nesting in building openings, beams or equipment
  • Complaints from tenants or customers about droppings near entrances
  • Before facade inspection or repair where droppings block access

Credentials to ask for

  • A respiratory protection program under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134 for workers handling droppings
  • Familiarity with NIOSH guidance on histoplasmosis prevention
  • Knowledge of the Migratory Bird Treaty Act and species identification
  • Access training for lifts, rope access or roof fall protection

What the scope includes

  • Survey of bird species, roosting areas and nesting activity
  • Wetting and removal of droppings with dust control
  • Disinfection and bagging of waste for disposal
  • Installation of netting, spikes, wire or other deterrents
  • Follow-up inspection of deterrents

How it is priced

Cleanup is often priced per square foot or per visit. Deterrents are priced per linear or square foot installed. Height, access, droppings volume and deterrent type drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • What respiratory protection will your workers use?
  • Which bird species are present, and are any protected?
  • What deterrent do you recommend, and how long does it last?

Questions about bird droppings cleanup and bird deterrent installation at height

Are bird droppings dangerous?

Dried droppings can contain fungal spores that cause lung infections when inhaled. Most risk comes from disturbing large, dry accumulations, which is why wetting and respirators are used.

Can we remove any bird nest?

Not always. Many birds and their active nests are protected by federal law. Pigeons, house sparrows and starlings are common exceptions.

Do deterrents hurt birds?

Netting, spikes and wire systems are designed to keep birds from landing, not to harm them. Proper installation and maintenance matter.
