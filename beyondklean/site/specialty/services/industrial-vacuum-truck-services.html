Industrial vacuum truck services

Liquid vacuum trucks, air movers and vacuum loaders for removing sludge, liquids, catalysts and dry bulk material from industrial sites.

Also called: vac truck services, liquid vacuum truck, air mover services, vacuum loading

Licensed, regulated work. Only properly licensed and certified specialists may perform it. Requirements vary by state and agency, so confirm credentials before hiring.

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Industrial vacuum services use liquid vacuum trucks, high-powered air movers and vacuum loaders to recover liquids, sludges, slurries and dry solids. Common uses include tank and sump cleanouts, spill recovery, catalyst and sand removal, and transferring waste to roll-off boxes. Trucks handling flammable liquids need grounding and bonding and controlled venting of exhaust, as described in API RP 2219 for petroleum service. Hauling hazardous materials or hazardous waste requires DOT hazmat compliance and, for hazardous waste, EPA transporter registration. Drivers need CDLs with the right endorsements.

When you need it

  • Turnaround cleaning of tanks, vessels and sumps
  • Spill or leak cleanup
  • Removing spent media, catalyst or sand
  • Routine removal of process waste or wastewater

Credentials to ask for

  • Commercial driver's licenses with hazmat and tanker endorsements as needed
  • DOT hazardous materials compliance (49 CFR Parts 171-180)
  • EPA hazardous waste transporter ID and state permits when hauling hazardous waste
  • Familiarity with API RP 2219 for vacuum trucks in petroleum service
  • HAZWOPER training per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120 where applicable

What the scope includes

  • Confirm material type, safety data sheet and waste profile
  • Set up truck with grounding, bonding and exhaust venting
  • Vacuum material from the source
  • Transfer to approved containers or haul directly
  • Clean up hoses and work area
  • Provide manifests or bills of lading

How it is priced

Typically priced by the hour per truck with a crew, plus disposal per gallon or ton. Material type, travel time, standby time and disposal fees drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Which truck type fits our material: liquid vac, air mover or vacuum loader?
  • Are your drivers and trucks set up for hazmat and hazardous waste?
  • How do you control vapors and static on flammable liquids?
  • What is your minimum call-out time and standby rate?

Questions about industrial vacuum truck services

What is the difference between a liquid vac and an air mover?

A liquid vacuum truck uses a pump to pull liquids and light sludge. An air mover uses high airflow to move dry solids, heavy sludge and material over longer distances.

Why do vacuum trucks need grounding?

Moving flammable liquids through hoses builds static charge. Grounding and bonding reduce the chance of a spark igniting vapors.

Who signs the waste manifest?

The generator, usually the plant owner, signs as generator. The transporter signs to accept the load, and the receiving facility signs on arrival.
