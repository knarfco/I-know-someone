Facade washing for stone, precast, EIFS and metal panel

Material-specific washing of stone, brick, precast concrete, EIFS and metal panel facades to remove soiling, biological growth and pollution.

Also called: building facade cleaning, exterior wall washing, masonry cleaning, EIFS cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Facade washing removes atmospheric soiling, algae, mildew and runoff stains from exterior walls. Each material needs a different method. Limestone and marble react to acids. Brick and precast often tolerate mild detergents and controlled pressure. EIFS is a thin coating over insulation and can be gouged by high pressure. Painted or coil-coated metal panels need non-abrasive, pH-appropriate cleaners. A qualified contractor tests methods on small areas, starts with the gentlest effective approach, protects glass and landscaping, and controls runoff. Guidance such as the National Park Service Preservation Brief 1 and Brick Industry Association Technical Note 20 is often used.

When you need it

  • Dark streaking, biological growth or pollution crusts on the walls
  • Before facade inspections, sealant replacement or coating work
  • Rebranding, sale or lease-up of a property
  • Recurring maintenance on buildings in high-pollution or humid areas

Credentials to ask for

  • Documented experience with the specific facade material on your building
  • Access training for the equipment used (aerial lift operator training per ANSI/SAIA A92.24, swing stage or rope access)
  • A written runoff and wastewater plan that follows local stormwater rules
  • For historic buildings, familiarity with National Park Service Preservation Brief 1

What the scope includes

  • Identification of each facade material and any coatings or sealers
  • Test panels with two or three methods, reviewed with the owner
  • Protection of glass, sealants, landscaping and adjacent surfaces
  • Pre-wetting, cleaning and thorough rinsing using the approved method
  • Runoff capture or diversion, then final inspection with the owner

How it is priced

Most often priced per square foot of facade or as a lump sum per elevation. Drivers include material type, degree of soiling, access method, chemical needs and runoff controls.

Ask before you hire

  • Which test panels will you do, and can we approve them before full work starts?
  • What pressure and water temperature will you use on our material?
  • How will you protect windows and sealants from your cleaners?
  • How will you manage wastewater?

Questions about facade washing for stone, precast, eifs and metal panel

Is pressure washing safe for every facade?

No. High pressure can erode soft stone, open mortar joints and gouge EIFS. Many materials are cleaned better with low pressure, detergents and dwell time.

Why do contractors insist on test panels?

The same product can work on one stone and stain another. Test panels show the result and any side effects before the whole building is cleaned. They also give both parties an agreed standard of clean.

How long does a facade stay clean?

It depends on climate, pollution, shading and how water flows across the wall. Some owners add a breathable water repellent or biocide after cleaning, but only where it suits the material.
