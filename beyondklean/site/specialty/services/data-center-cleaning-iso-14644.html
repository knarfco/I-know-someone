Data center cleaning to ISO 14644-1 class targets

Recurring cleaning of data center white space to an ISO 14644-1 airborne particle class, with particle counts before and after.

Also called: data center cleaning, server room cleaning, critical environment cleaning, white space cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Data center cleaning removes particles that can clog server intakes, carry moisture or bridge circuits. Hardware makers commonly cite ISO 14644-1 Class 8 for data center air, often attributed to ASHRAE TC 9.9 guidance. The work covers floors, rack exteriors, cable ladders, CRAC and CRAH unit exteriors, perimeter surfaces and doors. Crews use HEPA vacuums, static-dissipative mops, ESD-safe tools and approved cleaners. Before and after cleaning, airborne particle counts are taken at set points. Work follows the site's change management, access control and method of procedure (MOP) rules.

When you need it

  • Quarterly or semiannual maintenance schedules
  • Particle counts above target or dust visible on intakes
  • Before audits, customer tours or warranty reviews
  • After maintenance or equipment moves that stir up dust

Credentials to ask for

  • Training in ESD control, with static-safe equipment consistent with ANSI/ESD S20.20 principles
  • Calibrated particle counter and reporting to ISO 14644-1 classes
  • Experience following data center MOPs, access control and change management
  • Background checks and NDAs if required by the site

What the scope includes

  • Baseline particle counts at agreed locations
  • HEPA vacuuming and damp cleaning of floors and surfaces
  • Wiping of rack exteriors, cable ladders and cooling unit exteriors
  • Cleaning of entry areas and sticky mats
  • Final particle counts and a written report with photos

How it is priced

Usually priced per square foot of white space, per visit or on an annual contract. Space size, rack density, access rules, escort needs and testing scope drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • What ISO class will you test to, and where will you sample?
  • What ESD controls do your crews use?
  • Can you follow our MOP and change approval process?

Questions about data center cleaning to iso 14644-1 class targets

What is ISO 14644-1 Class 8?

It is a cleanroom classification based on the number of airborne particles per cubic meter. Many hardware makers use it as the target for data center air.

Why measure particles if the room looks clean?

Fine particles are not visible. Counts give objective data before and after cleaning and can help with warranty and audit questions.

How often should a data center be cleaned?

Many sites clean quarterly, with subfloor cleaning less often. Sites with outside air economizers or nearby construction may need more.
