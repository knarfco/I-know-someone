Commercial dryer exhaust cleaning

Lint removal from commercial and multi-unit dryer exhaust ducts, transitions and fans to reduce fire risk and improve drying time.

Also called: laundry dryer vent cleaning, lint duct cleaning, commercial laundry exhaust cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Commercial dryer exhaust cleaning removes lint from dryer transitions, individual and common exhaust ducts, booster fans and termination points in laundries, hotels, hospitals and apartment buildings. Crews use rotary brushes, air whips and high-volume vacuums, working from the termination back toward the dryers or the reverse depending on layout. Lint traps and lint collectors are emptied and cleaned. Code requirements for commercial dryer exhaust are in the International Mechanical Code as adopted locally. The contractor should measure airflow or static pressure and report crushed, disconnected or unsupported duct sections that need repair.

When you need it

  • Dryer cycles take longer than normal
  • Hot dryer cabinets, burning smell or lint at the termination
  • Routine fire prevention in hotels, laundromats and senior housing
  • After a dryer fire or insurance inspection finding
  • Apartment buildings with shared vertical dryer risers

Credentials to ask for

  • Certified Dryer Exhaust Technician (C-DET) through the Chimney Safety Institute of America
  • Knowledge of the locally adopted International Mechanical Code for dryer exhaust
  • Fall protection and roof access training for rooftop terminations
  • Liability insurance covering work in occupied buildings

What the scope includes

  • Inspection of transitions, ducts, booster fans and terminations
  • Brushing and vacuuming of each dryer run and common risers
  • Cleaning of lint traps, lint collectors and termination dampers
  • Airflow or static pressure readings before and after
  • Report of damaged, crushed or noncompliant duct

How it is priced

Usually priced per dryer or per vent run, with lump sums for large laundries or buildings. Number of dryers, duct length, riser height, roof access and lint load drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Do your technicians hold the C-DET credential?
  • Will you clean the full run, including common risers and terminations?
  • Will you report code issues such as plastic transitions or screws inside the duct?
  • Can you schedule around laundry operating hours?

Questions about commercial dryer exhaust cleaning

How often should commercial dryer exhaust be cleaned?

It depends on load. Busy laundries may need cleaning every few months; lighter use may need it annually. Long cycle times are a sign you are overdue.

Can the dryer service company do this?

Some can, but many appliance technicians clean only the dryer cabinet. Ask whether the full duct run is included.

What is a common riser?

It is a shared vertical duct serving dryers on several floors. It can clog in the middle where no single tenant can see it.
