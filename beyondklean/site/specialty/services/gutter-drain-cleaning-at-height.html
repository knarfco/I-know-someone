Gutter, scupper and roof drain cleaning at height

Clearing of gutters, scuppers, roof drains and downspouts on commercial buildings to prevent ponding, leaks and overflow.

Also called: commercial gutter cleaning, roof drain clearing, scupper cleaning, downspout cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Blocked roof drainage causes ponding, roof leaks, ice damming and, in severe cases, structural overload. This service clears leaves, sediment, nests and debris from gutters, internal roof drains, overflow drains and scuppers, then flushes downspouts and leaders. Internal drains may need drain cables or water jetting. On multistory buildings, gutters are reached from lifts, rope access or the roof with fall protection. Crews replace missing strainers and note damaged drain bodies or sealant so the roofer can repair them. Work is usually timed before and after leaf drop and before freezing weather.

When you need it

  • Fall leaf drop and spring seed and pollen season
  • Ponding water on the roof after storms
  • Overflow from gutters or scuppers onto walkways
  • Interior leaks near roof drains

Credentials to ask for

  • Fall protection plan for roof edges and lift or rope access training as applicable
  • Equipment for drain cabling or jetting on internal drains
  • Insurance covering roof work and water damage

What the scope includes

  • Roof walk to locate all drains, scuppers and gutters
  • Hand removal of debris and bagging off the roof
  • Flushing of downspouts and clearing of internal drains as needed
  • Replacement of missing drain strainers when supplied
  • Photo report of damaged drains, gutters or ponding areas

How it is priced

Priced per visit, per linear foot of gutter or per drain. Building height, number of drains, debris load and access method drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Do you clear internal drain lines or only the drain bowls?
  • How do you get debris off the roof?
  • Will you photograph problem areas?

Questions about gutter, scupper and roof drain cleaning at height

How often should roof drains be cleared?

Most commercial roofs need at least two cleanings a year. Buildings under trees may need more.

Why do drain strainers matter?

Strainers keep debris from entering the drain line, where blockages are harder to clear. Missing strainers should be replaced.

Can ponding damage the roof?

Standing water can speed membrane aging and add load to the structure. Clear drains help prevent it.
