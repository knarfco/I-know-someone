Category 3 floodwater and sewage cleanup

Cleanup of buildings hit by sewage or contaminated floodwater: extraction, removal of porous materials, cleaning and drying.

Also called: black water cleanup, contaminated flood cleanup, sewage backup restoration

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

IICRC S500 classifies water that contains sewage, floodwater from outside or other gross contamination as Category 3. Recovery focuses on the building. Crews set containment to protect unaffected areas, extract water and solids, and remove porous materials that absorbed contaminated water, such as carpet, pad, insulation and the lower part of drywall. Remaining structure is cleaned, treated with an antimicrobial where appropriate, and dried. Waste is bagged and disposed of. For sewage spills that involve biohazard response in occupied healthcare or public spaces, see the biohazard and sewage cleanup services.

When you need it

  • A sewer main or lateral backs up into a building
  • River or coastal floodwater enters the structure
  • A toilet overflow spreads beyond a tiled area
  • Water sits long enough to become contaminated

Credentials to ask for

  • IICRC WRT and ASD certifications, with methods consistent with ANSI/IICRC S500
  • Personal protective equipment and a respiratory protection program
  • Hepatitis A and tetanus vaccination guidance for workers, per public health advice
  • Waste hauling and disposal arrangements that meet local rules

What the scope includes

  • Isolate the area and protect occupants
  • Extract water and solids
  • Remove affected porous materials
  • Clean and apply antimicrobial as appropriate
  • Dry the structure and verify
  • Dispose of waste and document

How it is priced

Typically priced per square foot affected plus equipment days and disposal. Cost depends on contamination depth, materials removed, containment and drying time.

Ask before you hire

  • Which materials will you remove, and how high will you cut walls?
  • How will you keep contamination from spreading?
  • How will you show the area is clean before rebuilding?

Questions about category 3 floodwater and sewage cleanup

Can carpet be cleaned after a sewage backup?

Generally not. Carpet and pad soaked by sewage are discarded because they cannot be reliably decontaminated.

Is it safe to stay in the building?

Occupants should stay out of affected areas. Containment and air filtration can let unaffected areas stay in use.

Is sewage backup covered by insurance?

Many property policies exclude sewer backup unless you bought a specific endorsement. Check your coverage and limits with your agent.
