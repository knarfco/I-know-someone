Aircraft interior deep cleaning

Deep cleaning of cabins, seats, carpets, galleys and lavatories with products that will not harm certified interior materials.

Also called: aircraft interior detailing, cabin deep clean, aircraft carpet and seat cleaning, lavatory and galley deep clean

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Interior deep cleaning goes beyond turn cleaning between flights. Crews shampoo or extract carpets, clean seat covers and leather, detail sidewalls, bins and window shades, and sanitize galleys and lavatories. Cabin materials are certified for flammability, so products and methods must not alter them. Water use is limited to protect electronics and floor panels. Removing seats, panels or carpet is maintenance and must involve certificated personnel. Bodily fluid cleanup follows bloodborne pathogen procedures.

When you need it

  • Scheduled heavy checks or deep clean intervals
  • Before aircraft delivery, lease return or sale
  • After illness, spills or bodily fluid events
  • Odor or stain complaints from passengers

Credentials to ask for

  • Products and methods that will not alter cabin materials certified for flammability under 14 CFR 25.853
  • Bloodborne pathogens training under 29 CFR 1910.1030 for fluid cleanup
  • Certificated personnel for any seat, panel or carpet removal under 14 CFR Part 43
  • Airport badging for crews working at the gate or hangar

What the scope includes

  • Protect electronics, sensors and entertainment screens
  • Vacuum and extract carpets with low-moisture methods
  • Clean seat covers, leather and belts
  • Detail panels, bins, shades and tray tables
  • Sanitize galleys and lavatories
  • Final inspection and odor check

How it is priced

Usually priced per aircraft by type and cabin configuration, or by labor hour for business jets. Cost depends on cabin size, seat material, soil level and available ground time.

Ask before you hire

  • How do you choose products that are safe for our cabin materials?
  • Who removes seats or panels if needed?
  • How much moisture do your carpet methods use?

Questions about aircraft interior deep cleaning

Why worry about flammability?

Cabin materials are tested for flammability as part of certification. Some cleaners and treatments can change how they burn, so use products approved by the aircraft or material maker.

How long does a deep clean take?

A narrowbody cabin may take one or two shifts. Larger cabins or business jets with leather and wood may take longer.

Can odors be removed?

Most odors respond to cleaning the source. Persistent odors may need replacement of foam or carpet.
