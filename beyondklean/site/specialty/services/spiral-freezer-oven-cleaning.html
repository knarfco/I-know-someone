Spiral freezer and oven cleaning

Deep cleaning of spiral freezers, proofers, tunnel ovens and fryers, including belts, drums, evaporators and exhaust components.

Also called: spiral freezer sanitation, industrial oven cleaning, tunnel oven cleaning, fryer cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Spiral freezers, spiral ovens, proofers and fryers are large enclosed systems that need scheduled deep cleaning beyond daily washdown. For freezers, the system is defrosted, then belts, drums, evaporator coils, fans and enclosure panels are foamed, scrubbed, rinsed and sanitized. Ovens and fryers need carbon and grease removal from belts, burners, hoods and stacks, often with heated caustic or soaking. Many of these units are large enough that entry counts as confined space work. The contractor coordinates with maintenance on belt removal or rotation and returns the unit dry and ready for startup.

When you need it

  • Scheduled defrost and deep clean cycles for spiral freezers
  • Carbon or grease buildup affecting product quality
  • Environmental monitoring positives in freezer enclosures
  • Fire risk from grease in fryer exhaust or ovens

Credentials to ask for

  • Permit-required confined space training under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 where entry is needed
  • Lockout/tagout under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147
  • Chemical safety training for heated caustic and degreasers
  • Experience with the equipment manufacturer's cleaning instructions

What the scope includes

  • Shutdown, defrost or cooldown and lockout
  • Removal of debris and gross soil from belts and drums
  • Foam, scrub and rinse of coils, fans and enclosures; caustic soak for ovens and fryers
  • Sanitizing of food-contact parts
  • Inspection and photo report before startup

How it is priced

Usually priced per unit cleaning event or per labor shift. Unit size, soil level, confined space requirements and downtime window drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Have you cleaned this make and model before?
  • Is entry required, and how will you run the confined space permit?
  • How do you protect evaporator fins and belt components?
  • How long will the unit be down?

Questions about spiral freezer and oven cleaning

Why are spiral freezers a sanitation concern?

They are cold, wet and hard to reach inside, which can allow Listeria to persist if cleaning is incomplete.

Can daily sanitation handle this?

Daily crews clean accessible surfaces. Periodic deep cleans reach coils, fans and enclosure interiors.

Do oven cleanings need special chemicals?

Baked carbon usually needs strong alkaline cleaners and sometimes heat or soaking, chosen to suit the oven's materials.
