Industrial cooling tower cleaning and disinfection

Cleaning of cooling tower basins, fill, drift eliminators and distribution decks, with disinfection to control Legionella risk.

Also called: cooling tower cleanout, cooling tower basin cleaning, fill cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Cooling towers collect silt, scale, algae and biofilm in basins, fill and distribution decks. Fouling raises cooling water temperature and creates conditions for Legionella growth. Cleaning usually starts with disinfection, followed by draining, sludge removal from the basin, washing of fill and drift eliminators, and cleaning of nozzles and decks. A final disinfection may follow before return to service. ASHRAE Standard 188 requires a water management program for buildings and sites with cooling towers, and some cities and states have their own cooling tower rules. Basins and cells can be confined spaces.

When you need it

  • Visible biofilm, algae or sludge in the basin
  • High approach temperature or poor cooling
  • Positive Legionella test or water management program trigger
  • Seasonal start-up or shutdown

Credentials to ask for

  • Knowledge of ASHRAE Standard 188 and the site's water management program
  • Cooling Technology Institute guidance familiarity
  • Confined space training per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 for basin and cell entry
  • Respiratory protection during disinfection and pressure washing
  • Local cooling tower registration or inspection rules; requirements vary by state and city

What the scope includes

  • Coordinate with the water treatment vendor on disinfection
  • Disinfect and drain the tower
  • Remove basin sludge and debris
  • Clean fill, drift eliminators and distribution decks
  • Clear nozzles and strainers
  • Refill, re-disinfect if specified and document work

How it is priced

Priced per cell or per tower, or by crew-day. Tower size, sludge volume, fill condition and disinfection requirements drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Will you disinfect before cleaning to protect your crew and the public?
  • Do you coordinate with our water treatment vendor?
  • How will you protect fill from damage?
  • What documentation will you provide for our water management program?

Questions about industrial cooling tower cleaning and disinfection

Why disinfect before cleaning?

Cleaning breaks up biofilm and sends aerosols into the air. Disinfecting first reduces the chance of spreading Legionella to workers and nearby people.

How often should cooling towers be cleaned?

Many owners clean at least once a year, often more in dusty settings or warm climates. The water management program should set the frequency.

Can fill be cleaned or must it be replaced?

Light fouling can be washed out. Fill that is heavily scaled, collapsed or brittle usually needs replacement.
