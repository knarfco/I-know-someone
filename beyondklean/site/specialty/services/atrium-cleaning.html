Atrium and lobby high cleaning

Cleaning of interior atrium glass, ledges, railings, art and fixtures in multistory lobbies, malls and hospitals.

Also called: atrium glass cleaning, lobby high cleaning, interior high glass cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Atriums combine tall interior glass, skylights, balconies, railings, planters, art and hanging fixtures. Many cannot be reached with standard lifts because of floor loads, finished stone or obstacles. Contractors may use compact spider lifts with non-marking tracks, rope access from structure or balconies, or water-fed poles. Floors and furniture are protected and walkways closed below the work. Interior work avoids the dripping and overspray that would be acceptable outdoors. Cleaning hanging art and installations may need the artist's or owner's guidance.

When you need it

  • Dust and streaks on interior atrium glass and skylights
  • Cobwebs and dust on hanging fixtures or art
  • Before holiday decorating or special events
  • Routine semiannual or annual cleaning

Credentials to ask for

  • Spider lift or MEWP operator training, or SPRAT/IRATA certification for rope access
  • Floor load and floor protection planning
  • Experience working in occupied lobbies with public below

What the scope includes

  • Survey of heights, obstacles and floor load limits
  • Selection of access method and floor protection
  • Cleaning of interior glass, skylight undersides, ledges and railings
  • Dusting of fixtures and approved art
  • Cleanup and reopening of walkways

How it is priced

Usually a lump sum per cleaning or a recurring contract. Height, access method, glass area and floor protection drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Which lift fits our floor load and doorways?
  • How will you protect our stone floors?
  • Will you need approval to clean the hanging art?

Questions about atrium and lobby high cleaning

Can rope access be used indoors?

Yes, when there are approved anchor points above. It can reach areas lifts cannot.

Do spider lifts damage floors?

They are lighter than many lifts and have non-marking tracks, but floor protection is still standard.

How often should atrium glass be cleaned?

Many buildings clean interior high glass once or twice a year.
