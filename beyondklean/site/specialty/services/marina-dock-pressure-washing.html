Marina and dock pressure washing

Washing docks, piers, boardwalks and fuel docks to remove algae, bird waste and oil without sending wash water into the marina.

Also called: dock cleaning, pier washing, boardwalk pressure washing, floating dock cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Marina pressure washing cleans fixed and floating docks, gangways, boardwalks, pump-out stations and fuel docks. Algae and bird droppings make walking surfaces slippery and stain decking. Crews choose pressure and nozzles by deck material, since wood, composite and concrete each react differently. Because the work sits over water, chemicals must be limited and runoff controlled. Many marinas use mats, berms or vacuum recovery so wash water does not drain straight into the slips, and some state clean marina programs set specific practices.

When you need it

  • Spring commissioning before the boating season
  • Slippery decks from algae or bird waste
  • Oil or fuel staining around fuel docks
  • Before an event, sale or insurance inspection

Credentials to ask for

  • A written plan for wash water and chemical control over open water
  • Familiarity with your state's clean marina program practices, where one exists
  • General liability insurance covering work over water
  • Personal flotation devices and water rescue procedures for crews

What the scope includes

  • Walk the docks to note materials, damage and drain points
  • Set up recovery or filtration where chemicals or oil are present
  • Wash decking, cleats, rails and gangways
  • Treat oil stains at fuel docks with approved methods
  • Report loose boards, fasteners or float damage

How it is priced

Typically priced per square foot of dock surface or per linear foot of dock, sometimes as a seasonal contract. Cost depends on material, buildup level, water recovery needs and access by land or boat.

Ask before you hire

  • What pressure and cleaners will you use on our decking material?
  • How will you keep wash water and chemicals out of the marina?
  • Can you work around boaters and slip holders?

Questions about marina and dock pressure washing

Can high pressure damage wood docks?

Yes. Too much pressure raises the grain and gouges wood. Composite decking also has maker limits. Ask the contractor what settings they use.

Are cleaning chemicals allowed over water?

Many cleaners can harm aquatic life. Contractors should use plain water where possible and capture runoff when chemicals are needed.

How often should docks be washed?

Most marinas wash at least once a season. Docks in warm climates or with heavy bird activity often need washing more often to stay safe underfoot.
