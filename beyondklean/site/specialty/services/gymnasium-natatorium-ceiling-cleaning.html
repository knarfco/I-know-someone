Gymnasium and natatorium ceiling cleaning

Cleaning of gym and indoor pool ceilings, structure, ducts and fixtures, with floor protection and attention to pool corrosion.

Also called: gym ceiling cleaning, pool ceiling cleaning, natatorium structure cleaning, aquatic center overhead cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Gyms collect dust and debris on joists, ducts, basketball goals and light fixtures. Wood sport floors must be protected from lifts and debris. Natatoriums are harder. Chloramines from pool water create corrosive air that attacks steel structure, fasteners and fixtures, and condensation can drip contaminants into the pool. Overhead cleaning in pools may need lifts on the deck, rope access, or temporary platforms over the water. The pool may need to be closed and its water chemistry coordinated with the operator. Crews report corrosion they see, since it can be a structural concern.

When you need it

  • Before sports seasons or facility rentals
  • Debris falling onto courts or into pools
  • Corrosion, rust staining or flaking paint overhead in natatoriums
  • During annual pool shutdowns

Credentials to ask for

  • MEWP operator training, or SPRAT/IRATA certification for rope access over water
  • Floor protection methods for wood sport floors
  • Experience working in pool environments and with aquatic staff
  • Insurance for work in schools and public facilities

What the scope includes

  • Coordination with athletic or aquatic staff and closure planning
  • Floor or pool deck protection
  • Cleaning of structure, ducts, fixtures and equipment
  • Debris control to keep material out of the pool
  • Report of corrosion, loose fixtures or failed coatings

How it is priced

Usually a lump sum per facility. Ceiling height, access method, floor protection and pool closure requirements drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How will you protect our wood floor?
  • How will you keep debris out of the pool?
  • Will you report corrosion you see?

Questions about gymnasium and natatorium ceiling cleaning

Why do natatorium ceilings corrode?

Pool chemicals produce chloramines that build up in indoor air. With humidity, they corrode metal over time.

Should the pool be drained for cleaning?

Not always. Many contractors work with the pool full and closed, using covers or careful methods. Others time work with scheduled drain-downs.

How often should gym ceilings be cleaned?

Many schools clean during summer break each year or every few years.
