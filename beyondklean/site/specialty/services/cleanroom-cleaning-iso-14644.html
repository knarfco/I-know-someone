Cleanroom cleaning (ISO 14644)

Contamination-controlled cleaning of ISO 14644-classified cleanrooms using gowning, low-particle supplies and documented procedures.

Also called: controlled environment cleaning, ISO cleanroom cleaning, semiconductor cleanroom cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Cleanroom cleaning keeps particle and contamination levels within the room's ISO 14644-1 classification. Crews gown according to the room's protocol, bring in only cleanroom-compatible wipers, mops and solutions, and clean from cleanest to least clean areas and top to bottom. ISO 14644-5 addresses cleanroom operations, and IEST recommended practices give detailed cleaning methods. Work is often done during shutdowns or between shifts and must not disrupt airflow or process equipment. Records show the areas cleaned, materials and lots used, and who performed the work. Particle counts or monitoring results confirm the room remains in specification.

When you need it

  • Routine daily, weekly and monthly cleaning schedules
  • Restart after shutdown, maintenance or construction
  • Particle count excursions
  • Initial cleaning before cleanroom certification

Credentials to ask for

  • Training in gowning and cleanroom behavior for the room class
  • Working knowledge of ISO 14644-1 and ISO 14644-5
  • Use of IEST recommended practices for cleanroom housekeeping
  • Documented training records and SOPs

What the scope includes

  • Review of room class, SOPs and approved materials
  • Gowning and material transfer into the room
  • Cleaning of ceilings, walls, surfaces and floors in sequence
  • Removal of waste through approved pathways
  • Logs and support for particle count checks

How it is priced

Usually priced per cleaning event by square footage and room class, or as a monthly contract. Room class, frequency, gowning requirements and supply costs drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • What room classes do your staff routinely clean?
  • How do you train and qualify staff for gowning?
  • Which wipers, mops and solutions do you use, and are they compatible with our process?
  • What records will you leave after each cleaning?

Questions about cleanroom cleaning (iso 14644)

Does ISO 14644 tell me how to clean?

ISO 14644-1 sets classification by particle count. Cleaning practice is covered by ISO 14644-5 and industry guidance such as IEST recommended practices.

Can regular janitorial staff clean a cleanroom?

Only with specific cleanroom training and materials. Ordinary supplies and methods shed particles.

Does cleaning certify the room?

No. Certification is a separate test of airflow, filters and particle counts, usually done by an independent certifier.
