USDA and FDA facility deep cleaning

Full-facility deep cleaning for food plants during shutdowns, after regulatory findings or before restarting idle operations.

Also called: regulatory deep clean, plant shutdown cleaning, food facility sanitation overhaul

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

A facility deep clean resets the whole plant rather than a single line. It is common after a regulatory finding, during an annual shutdown, before restarting an idle building, or after a recall. Crews work room by room from top to bottom: overheads, walls, equipment exteriors and interiors, floors and drains, then sanitize. USDA FSIS-inspected establishments must meet sanitation performance standards in 9 CFR Part 416, and FDA-registered facilities must meet the current good manufacturing practices in 21 CFR Part 117 Subpart B. The plant's food safety team sets acceptance criteria, and the contractor supplies the labor, equipment and records needed to show what was done.

When you need it

  • Noncompliance record or warning letter citing sanitation
  • Recall or major contamination event
  • Restart of an idle facility or newly acquired plant
  • Annual shutdown with full-plant scope

Credentials to ask for

  • Experience in USDA-inspected or FDA-registered facilities
  • GMP training consistent with 21 CFR Part 117 or 9 CFR Part 416
  • Aerial lift, lockout/tagout and confined space training as needed
  • Capacity to staff large crews over short shutdown windows

What the scope includes

  • Plan by area, with priorities set by the food safety team
  • Top-down cleaning of structures, equipment and floors
  • Sanitizing and inspection by area
  • Coordination with maintenance for repairs found during cleaning
  • Daily progress reports and final documentation

How it is priced

Usually priced as a lump sum for the shutdown or per labor hour with a not-to-exceed figure. Plant size, equipment density, ceiling height, shutdown length and crew size drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How many people can you staff, and how quickly?
  • How will you document each area for regulators or auditors?
  • Who will supervise on each shift?
  • How do you handle repairs found during cleaning?

Questions about usda and fda facility deep cleaning

Does a deep clean resolve a regulatory finding?

It may address the condition, but regulators usually also expect a root cause and corrective actions in your written procedures.

How long does a full-plant deep clean take?

It depends on size and access. Many are planned for a few days to a week of shutdown.

Can the contractor talk to the inspector?

The plant normally handles regulatory communication. The contractor supplies records and supports the plant.
