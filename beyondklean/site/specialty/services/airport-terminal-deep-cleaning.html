Airport terminal deep cleaning

Periodic deep cleaning of concourses, restrooms, jet bridges, baggage areas and high surfaces in airport terminals.

Also called: terminal floor care, concourse deep clean, jet bridge cleaning, baggage area cleaning

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Terminal deep cleaning handles tasks that daily custodial staff cannot reach: stripping and refinishing floors, extracting carpet, cleaning high glass and ceilings, detailing restrooms, and washing jet bridges and baggage claim equipment. Work is scheduled overnight or in low traffic periods, with barriers to keep passengers safe. Crews working past security need badges or escorts. Lifts must avoid sensors, signs and fire protection devices.

When you need it

  • Annual or seasonal deep clean programs
  • Before peak travel seasons or events
  • After construction or renovation
  • Customer satisfaction scores drop for cleanliness

Credentials to ask for

  • Security Identification Display Area (SIDA) or sterile area badging under TSA airport security programs
  • Aerial lift training for high work
  • Bloodborne pathogens training for restroom and fluid cleanup
  • General liability insurance meeting airport requirements

What the scope includes

  • Plan work zones with airport operations
  • Strip, refinish or polish hard floors
  • Extract carpets and clean seating
  • Clean high glass, signs and ceilings
  • Deep clean restrooms and jet bridges

How it is priced

Usually priced per square foot or per task area, sometimes as a recurring contract. Cost depends on night work, security badging, lift needs and terminal size.

Ask before you hire

  • How many of your workers are already badged at our airport?
  • Can you work overnight and around passengers?
  • How will you protect sensors and signs?

Questions about airport terminal deep cleaning

Why use a specialist instead of daily custodians?

Deep cleaning needs equipment, lifts and crews that daily staff may not have. It also needs planning around flights.

Do all workers need badges?

Workers in secured areas need badges or escorts. Badging can take weeks, so plan ahead.

How often should terminals be deep cleaned?

Many airports deep clean high traffic areas yearly or seasonally. Restrooms and floors may need more frequent work.
