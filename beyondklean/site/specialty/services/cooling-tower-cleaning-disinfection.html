Cooling tower cleaning and disinfection

Draining, cleaning and disinfecting cooling towers to remove scale, sludge and biofilm and support a Legionella water management program.

Also called: cooling tower Legionella cleaning, tower basin cleaning, cooling tower remediation

Licensed, regulated work. Only properly licensed and certified specialists may perform it. Requirements vary by state and agency, so confirm credentials before hiring.

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Cooling tower cleaning removes sludge, scale, algae and biofilm from basins, fill, drift eliminators and distribution decks, then disinfects the system. Many programs disinfect with an oxidizing biocide before and after the physical cleaning so workers are not exposed to contaminated aerosols. Crews drain the basin, remove sediment, clean or replace fill, and flush the system. ASHRAE Standard 188 requires a water management program for buildings with cooling towers, and ASHRAE Guideline 12 gives cleaning and disinfection guidance. Some jurisdictions, such as New York City, have their own registration, inspection and cleaning rules. Workers wear respiratory protection suited to Legionella exposure.

When you need it

  • Scheduled cleaning under the building's water management program
  • Positive Legionella sample or a public health investigation
  • Seasonal startup after a tower has been idle
  • Visible biofilm, algae or heavy sediment in the basin
  • Local code deadlines for cleaning and disinfection

Credentials to ask for

  • Familiarity with ASHRAE Standard 188 and ASHRAE Guideline 12
  • OSHA respiratory protection program (29 CFR 1910.134) for aerosol exposure
  • Pesticide applicator certification where your state requires it for biocide application
  • Confined space procedures under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 if entering basins or enclosed sections
  • Any local cooling tower registration or certification; requirements vary by jurisdiction

What the scope includes

  • Pre-cleaning disinfection with an oxidizing biocide
  • Draining and removal of sludge and debris from the basin
  • Cleaning of fill, drift eliminators, nozzles and distribution decks
  • Inspection of drift eliminators and mechanical parts
  • Refill, post-cleaning disinfection and return to service
  • Written report for the water management program records

How it is priced

Usually priced per tower or per cell as a lump sum. Tower size, sediment volume, fill condition, access, after-hours work and waste disposal drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • Will you disinfect before cleaning to protect workers and the public?
  • How will you document the work for our ASHRAE 188 water management program?
  • What respiratory protection will your crew wear?
  • How will you dispose of sludge and chlorinated water?

Questions about cooling tower cleaning and disinfection

Who sets cleaning frequency for a cooling tower?

The building's water management program, the equipment manufacturer and local law. Some cities require cleaning on a fixed schedule.

Is cleaning the same as water treatment?

No. Water treatment controls chemistry day to day. Cleaning physically removes the sediment and biofilm that treatment cannot reach.

What happens after a positive Legionella result?

The program's corrective action plan applies. It usually calls for disinfection, cleaning and follow-up sampling, and may involve the local health department.
