Zinc whisker remediation

Identification and removal of conductive zinc whiskers from electroplated raised floor tiles, pedestals and other components.

Also called: zinc whisker removal, tin whisker remediation, metal whisker cleanup

Get quotes See specialists

What it is

Zinc whiskers are tiny conductive metal filaments that can grow from some electroplated steel surfaces, such as the undersides of older raised floor tiles and pedestals. When disturbed, they can become airborne and enter power supplies, causing shorts and failures. Hardware makers often require data centers to be free of zinc whiskers. Remediation starts with sampling, often with conductive tape examined under a microscope, to confirm whiskers are present. Affected tiles and parts are replaced with non-whiskering parts or encapsulated, with careful HEPA vacuuming to avoid spreading whiskers. Equipment may need inspection by its manufacturer.

When you need it

  • Unexplained power supply or equipment failures
  • Older raised floor systems with electroplated parts
  • Before or after moving raised floor tiles
  • Hardware maker requirements or warranty disputes

Credentials to ask for

  • Experience sampling for zinc whiskers and working with laboratories that use microscopy
  • ESD-safe HEPA equipment and containment methods
  • Coordination with the raised floor manufacturer on replacement parts

What the scope includes

  • Sampling of tiles, pedestals and other suspect surfaces
  • Lab confirmation of whisker presence
  • Work plan for replacement, encapsulation or cleaning
  • Controlled removal with HEPA vacuuming and containment
  • Post-remediation sampling and report

How it is priced

Sampling is usually priced per sample or per survey. Remediation is priced per tile, per square foot or lump sum. The extent of contamination, replacement parts and live-environment controls drive cost.

Ask before you hire

  • How will you sample, and who examines the samples?
  • Will you replace or encapsulate affected parts?
  • How will you keep whiskers from spreading during removal?

Questions about zinc whisker remediation

What causes zinc whiskers?

They can grow from some zinc electroplated surfaces over time. Older raised floor tiles and pedestals are a common source.

Can zinc whiskers be seen?

They are very thin and hard to see. Sampling with tape and a microscope is used to confirm them.

Is cleaning alone enough?

Usually not, since whiskers can keep growing. Replacement or encapsulation of the source is often needed.
