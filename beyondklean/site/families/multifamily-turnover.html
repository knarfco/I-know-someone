Questions about multifamily turnover

How long does it take to clean a new apartment unit?

A typical unit final clean takes a few crew-hours, depending on size, finishes and how much construction dust is left. Consistent checklists keep units uniform.

Are appliances cleaned inside and out?

They should be: remove packaging, film and labels, then clean interiors and exteriors so the unit is ready to show.

Who cleans amenity spaces in a new apartment community?

The same cleaning contractor, usually before leasing tours start. Clubhouses, fitness rooms and pool areas are prioritized because they sell the property.

What is a model unit clean?

A detailed clean of the units used for tours, often repeated as they get foot traffic during lease-up.

Do units need re-cleaning before move-in?

Often yes, if units sat empty after final clean. A light dust and floor clean before move-in keeps the first impression right.

Can dryer vents be clogged from construction?

Yes. Drywall dust and debris can enter vent lines during construction, and a quick check before turnover can prevent fire risk and complaints.

Do trash rooms and chutes need cleaning at turnover?

Yes. They collect construction debris and odors, and chute doors and rooms should be clean before residents move in.

What is the biggest multifamily cleaning challenge?

Schedule. Units are released on a rolling basis, and cleaning has to match the pace of inspections and lease-up.
