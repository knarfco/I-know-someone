Questions about mechanical & iaq

Can a cleaning crew clean an electrical room?

Only non-invasive cleaning of floors, walls and enclosure exteriors, and only where the electrical contractor or facility allows it. Nothing inside energized equipment, and no liquids near live gear.

What is construction indoor air quality management?

A plan, often required on LEED projects, to keep dust and contaminants out of the HVAC system and building during construction: protecting ducts, filtering returns, housekeeping and scheduling.

Should HVAC filters be changed before occupancy?

Yes. Filters that ran during construction are loaded with dust. Green building standards call for new filtration media before occupancy.

What is a building flush-out?

Running the HVAC system with outdoor air for a set period after construction, before or during early occupancy, to dilute contaminants from new finishes. A thorough final clean should come first.

Is duct cleaning part of a construction cleanup?

Duct exteriors are. Duct interior cleaning is a specialty, often done to NADCA standards, and is only needed when ducts were contaminated during construction.

Why were the ducts capped during construction?

To keep construction dust out of them. If caps were missed or torn, dust gets in, and duct cleaning may be needed before turnover.

Who cleans the rooftop units?

The exterior of rooftop units and curbs can be cleaned by the cleaning contractor. Coils and internal components belong to the mechanical contractor.

Does Beyond Klean provide IAQ testing?

No. Air quality testing is done by independent industrial hygiene or commissioning professionals. Our network handles the cleaning and documentation that supports it.
