Questions about walls & ceilings

Can you wash freshly painted walls?

Not right away. Most latex paints need days to weeks to cure fully, depending on the product. Early cleaning should be light spot cleaning with neutral cleaners and soft microfiber, not scrubbing.

How do you clean acoustic ceiling tiles without staining them?

With dry methods first: dry sponges, soft brushes and HEPA vacuuming. Liquid cleaners can leave water rings on mineral fiber tile. Badly soiled tiles are often cheaper to replace.

Who cleans the ceiling grid after construction?

It should be in the cleaning contractor's final clean scope. Grid gets fingerprints from tile setters and dust from above-ceiling work, and it is a common punch item.

Should air diffusers be cleaned before turnover?

Yes. Diffusers and return grilles catch construction dust and often show dark streaks soon after the HVAC starts. Face cleaning is part of final clean; duct interior cleaning is a separate specialty.

What is the best way to remove scuffs from walls?

Start with the gentlest option: a damp microfiber cloth, then a neutral cleaner, then a melamine sponge used lightly. Aggressive rubbing burnishes flat paint and leaves a shiny patch.

Do wallcoverings need special cleaning?

Yes. Vinyl wallcovering tolerates mild cleaners; fabric and natural-fiber wallcoverings need dry methods. Always follow the manufacturer's care instructions.

Are wall and ceiling smudges really punch-list items?

Yes. Architects and owners routinely list fingerprints, overspray and smudges on walls and ceilings during the punch walk.

What about walls above 10 feet?

Upper walls, ledges and soffits need lifts or poles and are usually part of the overhead scope. They collect dust the same way decks and ducts do.
