Questions about doors & millwork

Should the labels on fire-rated doors be removed?

No. The fire rating label on the door edge and frame must stay in place and readable. Cleaners should work around it and never paint over or remove it.

How do you clean new cabinets after construction?

Vacuum dust from inside boxes and drawers, then wipe with a mild cleaner suitable for the finish. Avoid ammonia, abrasive pads and excess water, which can damage wood finishes and edge banding.

What cleaner is safe for quartz countertops?

Mild, pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths. Strong alkalis, solvents and abrasive pads can dull or discolor the resin in engineered quartz.

How should brass and stainless door hardware be cleaned?

With a soft cloth and a cleaner made for that finish. Abrasives scratch, and some metal polishes strip protective lacquer from brass and bronze.

Do toilet partitions need special cleaning?

Powder-coated, plastic laminate, solid plastic and stainless partitions each have recommended cleaners. Most need only a neutral cleaner; sticker and adhesive removal needs care on powder coat.

Is cleaning inside drawers part of a final clean?

On a well-written scope, yes. Inspectors and owners open them, and they collect construction dust and packaging debris.

What is the most common millwork punch item?

Dust, adhesive and pencil marks on cabinet faces and inside boxes, followed by fingerprints and protective film left on edges and hardware.

Who removes the blue protective film on new hardware and appliances?

Usually the cleaning crew at final clean, unless the installer is required to. Film left too long in sun or heat becomes hard to remove.
