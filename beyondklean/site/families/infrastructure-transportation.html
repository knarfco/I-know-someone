Infrastructure & Transportation Cleaning

Transportation and public works projects bring their own access rules, phasing and environmental limits. This family covers airports, rail and transit stations, cruise terminals, parking garages, EV charging sites, fleet maintenance buildings, marinas and public infrastructure. Our crews work within badging, track access, traffic control and stormwater requirements, and schedule cleaning around live operations so each phase opens clean and ready for inspection.

Questions about infrastructure & transportation

Can your crews work in airports and transit stations that stay open?

Yes. Most of this work happens behind barriers or during night windows next to live operations. Badging, escorts and access windows are arranged through the general contractor with the airport or transit authority.

How do you keep debris away from tracks, runways and the water?

By controlling it at the source. Crews bag debris immediately, track tools, cover gaps and drains, and avoid washing near tracks, airside pavement or waterfront edges where loose material could escape.

What happens to wash water on garages, docks and public sites?

It is recovered with scrubbers or containment rather than sent to storm drains or open water. Where drains route to approved separators, the project's environmental plan decides what is allowed.

Do transportation projects require special training for cleaners?

Often. Airports require security badging, rail projects require track safety training, and road work requires traffic control awareness. Our crews complete what each project specifies before starting.

Can cleaning be phased to match a terminal or station reopening?

Yes. We clean each phase as the GC releases it, then return for a touch-up before the owner or agency walk, so every section opens on schedule.

Do you handle public works projects with prevailing wage requirements?

Yes. When bid documents require prevailing wage or certified payroll, we price the work accordingly and provide the payroll reporting the agency asks for on each pay period.

What equipment do you use for parking garages and large decks?

Compact ride-on sweepers and scrubbers sized for garage clearances, HEPA vacuums for stair towers and lobbies, and recovery equipment for wash water. Crews check clearance and ramp grades first.

How is infrastructure and transportation cleaning priced?

Usually per square foot for buildings and decks, by the day for night or escorted work, or as a lot for a defined phase. Access time, night premiums and containment needs affect the price.
