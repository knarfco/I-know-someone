Questions about elevators & stairs

Who cleans the elevator pit?

Pits collect debris during construction and must be clean for inspection. The cleaning contractor can remove debris only with the elevator contractor's lockout and permission.

Why do elevator sills need cleaning?

Debris in sill tracks can keep doors from closing properly and cause service calls. Sills should be vacuumed clean before turnover.

When is the elevator cab protection removed?

Near the end of the project, after heavy deliveries and furniture moves are complete. The cab is then detailed.

How are stairwells cleaned after construction?

Top to bottom: dust rails and ledges, clean treads and nosings, then floors, so dust does not fall onto cleaned areas.

Are escalators cleaned by the cleaning contractor?

Only exterior surfaces and only with the escalator contractor's permission and lockout. Comb plates and step cleaning are specialty work.

What does a lobby final clean include?

Floors, entry glass, walls, signage, furniture, elevator lobbies and any feature walls or lighting. It is the most visible space in the building.

Should stair nosings be cleaned separately?

They need attention because they collect grime and adhesive residue. Nosings are often a visible punch item.

Can corridors be cleaned while tenants move in?

Yes, with phased cleaning that follows move-in schedules and focuses on high-traffic routes.
