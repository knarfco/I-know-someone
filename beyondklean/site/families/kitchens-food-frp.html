Questions about kitchens & food facilities

What is FRP?

Fiberglass reinforced plastic: the washable, textured wall panels used in commercial kitchens, restrooms and food areas. It is installed with adhesive and trim, and it needs non-abrasive cleaning.

Who cleans a new restaurant before opening?

A post-construction cleaning contractor handles the building and equipment exteriors. Hood and duct cleaning, ice machine sanitizing and refrigeration startup are usually done by their own specialty contractors.

Is kitchen hood cleaning part of construction cleaning?

The hood exterior is. Hood and grease duct interior cleaning is a regulated specialty under NFPA 96 and is done by certified hood cleaning contractors.

What is the difference between cleaning and sanitizing a food surface?

Cleaning removes soil and residue. Sanitizing reduces microorganisms to safe levels and must be done with registered sanitizers at label concentrations. Food-contact surfaces need both, in that order.

How do you remove construction dust from a walk-in cooler?

Vacuum and wipe panels, shelving and floors with food-safe cleaners before the box is started up and stocked. Dust in a running cooler can end up on product.

What do health inspectors look for at a new restaurant opening?

Clean, intact, cleanable surfaces; no construction debris; working handwash sinks; clean equipment; and no dust over food areas. Overhead dust is a common finding.

Can grocery departments open in phases?

Yes. Grocery openings often turn over by department, and cleaning must follow each department's handover date, especially deli, bakery and meat rooms.

Why is stainless steel streaky after cleaning?

Streaks come from residue, hard water or wiping against the grain. Clean with a neutral cleaner, rinse, dry and finish with the grain.
