Questions about restrooms & fixtures

How do you remove stickers from new toilets and sinks?

Peel the label, then remove the adhesive with a product that is safe for vitreous china and chrome. Razor blades can scratch glazes and plated finishes.

Can acidic bowl cleaners be used on new restroom fixtures?

They should be avoided at turnover. New chrome, grout and some stone are sensitive to acids. Neutral cleaners and non-acidic descalers are safer for initial cleaning.

What is caulk haze?

A thin film of silicone or sealant left on surfaces around a joint after caulking. It attracts dirt and looks smeary. It needs a remover that will not damage the surrounding finish.

Should restroom cleaning include floor drains?

Yes. Floor drains often collect grout, debris and mortar during construction and should be checked and cleaned so they drain freely at turnover.

Who cleans the restroom accessories?

The cleaning contractor during final clean: soap and towel dispensers, grab bars, mirrors, hand dryers and partitions.

Is disinfecting part of a construction restroom clean?

Cleaning is. Disinfecting is a separate step that requires EPA-registered products used according to the label, and it is only claimed when actually done that way.

How clean does a restroom need to be for the owner walk?

Free of dust, labels, residue, smears and debris, with fixtures polished and drains clear. Inspectors look under sinks, behind toilets and inside stalls.

Do eyewash stations need cleaning before turnover?

The exterior should be cleaned. Flushing and testing is the facility's responsibility under its safety program.
