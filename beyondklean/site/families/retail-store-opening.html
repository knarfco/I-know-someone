Questions about retail & store openings

When should the cleaning crew start on a new store?

After fixtures are installed and before merchandise is set. Most store openings need a fixture clean, a merchandise-set support clean and a final pre-opening clean.

What is a store opening clean?

The final clean of a new or remodeled store before it opens: floors, glass, fixtures, fitting rooms, stockrooms and restrooms, timed around merchandise delivery and the opening date.

Can remodels be cleaned overnight while the store stays open?

Yes. Overnight crews clean after each night's construction so the store can trade the next morning. It takes coordination with the remodel schedule.

How do national retailers handle cleaning for many locations?

Through rollout programs: one scope, one checklist and coordinated crews across many sites, so every store opens to the same standard.

Who cleans gondola shelving?

The cleaning contractor, after the fixture installer is done and before stocking. Shelves, uprights, kick plates and price channels all collect dust.

Does store opening cleaning include the ceiling?

It should for open-ceiling stores. Dust from the deck falls onto merchandise and fixtures after opening if it is not cleaned first.

How long does a store opening clean take?

It depends on size and how many trades are still working. A small store may need a day; a big box store may need multiple shifts over several days.

Do restaurant chains use the same approach?

Yes. Chains typically standardize their opening clean across locations and add kitchen-specific items for health inspection.
