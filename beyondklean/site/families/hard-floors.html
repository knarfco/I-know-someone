Hard Floors

Floors are the largest finished surface in most buildings and the first thing an owner judges. Each floor type needs a different first cleaning: concrete, resilient vinyl, tile, stone, terrazzo, wood and epoxy. This family covers initial care that matches manufacturer requirements and protects the floor warranty.

Concrete floors

Why it matters. New slabs carry mud, sealer haze, tire marks and curing residue; floor appearance drives the owner's first impression.

Our method. Auto-scrubbing with neutral or low-alkaline solutions and controlled water recovery avoids acid etching and runoff.

Questions about hard floors

Does new VCT need to be stripped before waxing?

New VCT has a factory coating that must be removed or prepared before floor finish will bond. That is why new VCT gets an initial scrub or strip and multiple coats of finish, not just a mop and wax.

Can LVT be waxed?

Most luxury vinyl tile is designed to be maintained without wax. Applying finish or using the wrong cleaner can void the manufacturer's warranty. Follow the manufacturer's maintenance guide.

How do you remove grout haze from new tile?

With a grout haze remover suited to the tile and grout type, applied after the grout has cured enough. Acidic removers are not safe on marble, limestone, travertine or some grouts.

How is a new concrete floor cleaned?

Usually with an auto-scrubber, a neutral or mildly alkaline cleaner and pads chosen for the finish. Polished and sealed concrete need gentler pads and cleaners than raw slabs.

What is efflorescence?

A white, powdery salt deposit that appears when water moves through concrete, masonry or grout and evaporates. It needs a remover matched to the surface, and the moisture source should be found.

Is polishing concrete part of cleaning?

No. Concrete grinding and polishing is a flooring specialty with heavy equipment and silica controls. Cleaning contractors maintain polished concrete; polishing contractors create it.

How long before new floors can be cleaned?

Tile grout, floor finishes, epoxy and wood coatings each have cure times. Cleaning too early can damage them. The installer's cure schedule should be in the cleaning plan.

Who removes tire and forklift marks from new warehouse floors?

The cleaning contractor at final clean, using scrubbers with appropriate pads and cleaners. Non-marking tires on lifts reduce the problem.
