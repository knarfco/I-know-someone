Specialty cleaning contractors: get matched to real projects

Beyond Klean doesn't sell leads. We scope projects, qualify them and route them to verified contractors with the right specialty and capacity. You price and perform the work.

Network tiers

  • Applicant. Information received, not yet verified. No project routing.
  • Verified generalist. Identity, insurance and experience verified. Routed general construction cleaning scopes.
  • Verified specialist. Additional technical qualifications and references. Routed matching specialty scopes.
  • Conditional specialist. Regulated or higher-risk capabilities, reviewed case by case. Manual approval only.
