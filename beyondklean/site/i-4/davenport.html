Questions about Davenport

Do you provide construction cleaning in Davenport?

Yes. Davenport is in Polk County on the I-4 corridor, and we cover all 3 of its ZIP codes we serve here (33837, 33896, 33897). Send the address, square footage and schedule and we return an itemized bid.

How far is Davenport from I-4?

The Davenport ZIP codes we serve sit 1 to 4 miles from the interstate, about 15 minutes or less for a crew. Along the corridor, its closest neighbors are Haines City and Kissimmee.

What kinds of buildings do you clean in Davenport?

Mostly apartments, condos and housing, hotels and hospitality, retail stores and openings. Every project gets the same rough, final and touch-up sequence, and specialty work such as floors, glass and high dusting can be added to the same bid.

Where do public agencies in Davenport post cleaning bids?

Polk County agencies post solicitations through their own purchasing portals, including Polk County BOCC Procurement Division, Polk County Public Schools Purchasing, Florida Polytechnic University Procurement. We watch those channels and bid as a prime or as a subcontractor to the general contractor.
