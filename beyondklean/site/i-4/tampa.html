Questions about Tampa

Do you provide construction cleaning in Tampa?

Yes. Tampa is in Hillsborough County on the I-4 corridor, and we cover all 24 of its ZIP codes we serve here (33602, 33603, 33604, 33605, 33606, 33607, 33609, 33610, 33611, 33612, 33613, 33614, 33615, 33616, 33617, 33618, 33619, 33621, 33624, 33625, 33629, 33634, 33637, 33647). Send the address, square footage and schedule and we return an itemized bid.

How far is Tampa from I-4?

The Tampa ZIP codes we serve sit 0 to 10 miles from the interstate, about 15 minutes or less for a crew. Along the corridor, its closest neighbors are Gibsonton.

What kinds of buildings do you clean in Tampa?

Mostly offices and tenant improvements, hospitals, clinics and medical offices, hotels and hospitality, schools and universities. Every project gets the same rough, final and touch-up sequence, and specialty work such as floors, glass and high dusting can be added to the same bid.

Where do public agencies in Tampa post cleaning bids?

Hillsborough County agencies post solicitations through their own purchasing portals, including City of Tampa Purchasing, Hillsborough County BOCC Procurement Services, Hillsborough County Public Schools Procurement Services. We watch those channels and bid as a prime or as a subcontractor to the general contractor.
