I-4 Corridor

Construction cleaning in Hillsborough County, FL

Hillsborough County is the western anchor of I-4, which begins at I-275 in downtown Tampa. The county holds Tampa's office core, Port Tampa Bay, Tampa International Airport, the University of South Florida and MacDill Air Force Base, plus fast-growing suburbs east of the city in Brandon, Riverview and Plant City.
