Central Florida · Tampa to Daytona Beach

Construction cleaning along the I-4 corridor

Interstate 4 runs about 132 miles across Central Florida, from downtown Tampa to I-95 at Daytona Beach. It ties together two major metros, the logistics belt of Polk County, the theme-park and convention district of Orlando and Kissimmee, the office parks of Seminole County and the coastal market of Volusia County. We cover every ZIP code within about 15 minutes of the interstate, end to end.

Questions about the I-4 corridor

What is the I-4 corridor?

Interstate 4 across Central Florida, about 132 miles from downtown Tampa to I-95 at Daytona Beach, through Plant City, Lakeland, Orlando, Sanford and DeLand.

Which ZIP codes do you cover on I-4?

All 123 ZIP codes whose centers sit within about 10 miles of the interstate, roughly 15 minutes of driving, across 6 counties. The full list is on this page.

Do you bid public work on the corridor?

Yes. Counties, cities, school districts, colleges, airports and transit agencies along I-4 post cleaning and construction solicitations on their own portals. We bid as a prime or as a subcontractor to the general contractor.

Can one bid cover sites in several corridor cities?

Yes. Multi-site work such as retail rollouts, school programs and apartment portfolios can be priced on one bid with a line for each site.
