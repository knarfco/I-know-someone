Questions about Thonotosassa

Do you provide construction cleaning in Thonotosassa?

Yes. Thonotosassa is in Hillsborough County on the I-4 corridor, and we cover all 1 of its ZIP code we serve here (33592). Send the address, square footage and schedule and we return an itemized bid.

How far is Thonotosassa from I-4?

The Thonotosassa ZIP codes we serve sit about 4 miles from the interstate, about 15 minutes or less for a crew. Along the corridor, its closest neighbors are Seffner and Valrico.

What kinds of buildings do you clean in Thonotosassa?

Mostly warehouses and distribution centers, apartments, condos and housing. Every project gets the same rough, final and touch-up sequence, and specialty work such as floors, glass and high dusting can be added to the same bid.

Where do public agencies in Thonotosassa post cleaning bids?

Hillsborough County agencies post solicitations through their own purchasing portals, including Hillsborough County BOCC Procurement Services, Hillsborough County Public Schools Procurement Services, University of South Florida Procurement Services. We watch those channels and bid as a prime or as a subcontractor to the general contractor.
