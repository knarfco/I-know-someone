I-4 Corridor

Construction cleaning in Polk County, FL

Polk County sits halfway between Tampa and Orlando, and its I-4 frontage has become one of Florida's busiest logistics belts. Distribution centers, industrial parks and new housing fill the corridor around Lakeland, Auburndale, Winter Haven, Haines City and Davenport.
