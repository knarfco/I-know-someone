Mezzanine underside cleaning

Mezzanine underside cleaning removes dust and debris from the bottom of a mezzanine deck, its beams, joists, lights and sprinkler lines, along with the supporting columns.

Also called mezzanine soffit cleaning

Why it matters

The space under a mezzanine is usually working space: stockrooms, offices or production areas. Dust from the underside falls directly on people and product below, and the low clearance makes it easy to overlook.

How we approach it

Crews use scissor lifts or rolling platforms sized to the clearance and HEPA vacuum the deck underside, framing and fixtures, then damp-wipe columns and bracing.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Mezzanine footprint and clearance below
  • Structure type: bar joist, steel beam or wood
  • Items stored below that need covering
  • Lights, sprinkler and conduit under the deck

Watch-outs

  • Low clearance limits lift choice; check heights before scheduling equipment.
  • Grating mezzanines let dust fall through from above; clean the top surface first.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about mezzanine underside cleaning

Why clean under a mezzanine if no one sees it?

Because people and product are directly below it. Dust from the underside falls onto workstations and stock.

What equipment is used under a low mezzanine?

Small scissor lifts, rolling platforms or extension poles, depending on the clearance.

Should the top of the mezzanine be cleaned first?

Yes, especially if it is grating or has gaps. Clean top to bottom so nothing falls onto a cleaned surface.

Is mezzanine cleaning part of warehouse turnover?

It should be. It is often missed because it is neither the main ceiling nor the floor.

What else is mezzanine underside cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "mezzanine soffit cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
