Refrigerated case detailing

Refrigerated case detailing cleans grocery and convenience store display cases, reach-ins, coffin cases and multidecks inside and out, including shelves, glass doors, frames, kick plates and drain pans, before startup and stocking.

Why it matters

Refrigerated cases display food directly to shoppers. Construction dust inside cases ends up on product, and dirty glass doors look neglected.

How we do it

Crews clean cases before startup or stocking, using food-safe cleaners and avoiding coils.

Common questions

How are refrigerated display cases cleaned after construction?

Shelves are removed where designed to, interiors and doors are cleaned with food-safe products and exteriors and kick plates are wiped.

When should refrigerated cases be cleaned?

Before startup and stocking, when they are empty and warm.

Who cleans refrigerated case coils?

Refrigeration technicians; cleaning crews do not touch coils or fans.

Are case kick plates and bumpers cleaned?

Yes, they collect construction dust and scuffs at floor level.

What else is refrigerated case detailing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "reach-in case cleaning", "refrigerated display cleaning", "reach-in refrigeration cleaning" and "reach-in cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
