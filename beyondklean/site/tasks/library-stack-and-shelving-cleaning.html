Library stack and shelving cleaning

Library stack and shelving cleaning cleans library shelving, stacks, reading rooms and study areas after construction, before the collection is moved in.

Why it matters

Dust on shelves transfers to books and archives and can damage them. Once books are shelved, cleaning shelves becomes slow and risky.

How we do it

Crews vacuum and wipe shelves top down before the collection arrives, and clean reading rooms, study carrels and service desks.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Shelving extent
  • Collection move date
  • Reading and study areas
  • Special collections rooms

Watch-outs

  • Cleaning around shelved books needs the library's approval.
  • Tall stacks may need ladders or lifts.

Common questions

Why clean library shelves before books are moved in?

Construction dust transfers to books and can damage them, and cleaning around shelved books is slow.

When are library shelves cleaned?

After construction ends and before the collection move, while the shelves are still empty and easy to reach.

Are reading rooms cleaned?

Yes, reading rooms, study carrels and service desks are cleaned.

Who moves library collections?

Specialized library movers, who coordinate with library staff to keep collections in order.

What else is library stack and shelving cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "library cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
