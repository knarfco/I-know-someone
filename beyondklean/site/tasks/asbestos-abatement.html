Asbestos abatement

Asbestos abatement removes asbestos-containing materials under federal, state and local rules by licensed abatement contractors.

Also called asbestos removal

Why it matters

Asbestos fibers cause serious disease. Disturbing it without controls is illegal and dangerous.

How we approach it

Never routed to cleaning crews; only licensed abatement contractors after review.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Survey results
  • Licensing
  • Notifications
  • Clearance

Watch-outs

  • Stop work if suspect material is found.
  • Notifications may be required.

Questions about asbestos abatement

What if asbestos is found during construction?

Stop work and call a licensed abatement contractor.

Can cleaners remove asbestos?

No.

Is asbestos still found in buildings?

Yes, in older buildings.

Is clearance testing required?

Often.

What else is asbestos abatement called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "asbestos removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
