Pre-certificate of occupancy clean

A pre-certificate of occupancy clean prepares a building for the building official's final inspection: clearing exits, cleaning fire and life-safety equipment, opening access to equipment rooms and making inspected areas presentable.

Also called CO inspection clean

Why it matters

The certificate of occupancy inspection focuses on life safety. Blocked exits, debris in equipment rooms and dirty or covered devices can delay the certificate, which delays move-in and often payment.

How we approach it

Crews walk the inspection route with the GC, clear exits and stairs, clean equipment rooms, make sure fire devices are visible and finish with a general clean of public areas.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Inspection date and route
  • Exits, stairs and corridors
  • Fire and life-safety devices
  • Equipment rooms

Watch-outs

  • Some labels, such as energy labels, may need to remain until the inspector sees them.
  • Never cover or block fire devices while cleaning.

Questions about pre-certificate of occupancy clean

What is a certificate of occupancy?

The document from the local building authority that allows a building or space to be legally occupied. It follows a final inspection.

Can a dirty building fail a certificate of occupancy inspection?

Debris in exits, blocked equipment and obstructed fire devices can cause failures or delays, even when construction is complete.

What should be cleaned before the CO inspection?

Exit routes, stairs, equipment rooms, fire and life-safety devices, and the public areas on the inspection route.

Who schedules the certificate of occupancy inspection?

The GC schedules it with the building department once the project is ready.

What else is pre-certificate of occupancy clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "CO inspection clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
