Pre-certificate of occupancy clean

A pre-certificate of occupancy clean prepares the building for the building official's final inspection before a certificate of occupancy is issued.

Also called CO inspection clean

Why it matters

The CO inspection checks life-safety systems and finished conditions. Debris, blocked exits and dirty equipment rooms are common issues.

How we approach it

Crews clear exits, equipment rooms and inspection areas and complete a building-wide clean.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Inspection date
  • Exit routes
  • Equipment rooms
  • Areas

Watch-outs

  • Blocked exits fail inspection.
  • Labels may need to stay.

Questions about pre-certificate of occupancy clean

What is a certificate of occupancy?

Permission from the building authority to occupy the building.

Does cleaning affect the CO inspection?

Debris and blocked exits can cause problems.

What is cleaned before CO?

Exits, equipment rooms and inspection areas.

Who schedules the CO inspection?

The GC.

What else is pre-certificate of occupancy clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "CO inspection clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
