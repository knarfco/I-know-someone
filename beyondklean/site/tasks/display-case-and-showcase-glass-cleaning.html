Display case and showcase glass cleaning

Display case and showcase glass cleaning cleans retail, museum, food and jewelry display cases, including glass, shelves, frames, interiors and lighting, before merchandise is placed.

Also called showcase cleaning

Why it matters

Display cases hold merchandise and food; dust and fingerprints inside them are visible and affect sales and inspection.

How we approach it

Crews clean interiors before stocking, wipe glass shelves and frames, and clean exterior glass last.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of cases
  • Lighting inside
  • Food or merchandise
  • Glass and frame types

Watch-outs

  • Lighting in cases may be hot.
  • Food cases need food-safe cleaners.

Questions about display case and showcase glass cleaning

When should display cases be cleaned?

Before stocking, then touched up after merchandise is set.

Are refrigerated food display cases cleaned the same way?

They use food-safe cleaners and care around refrigeration components.

Are display case interiors cleaned?

Yes, shelves, floors and walls of the case are cleaned before merchandise goes in.

Who cleans showcases in a new store?

The cleaning crew cleans display cases during the store opening clean, before stocking and again as a touch-up after merchandise is set.

What else is display case and showcase glass cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "showcase cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
