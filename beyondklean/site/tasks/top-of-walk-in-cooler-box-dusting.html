Top of walk-in cooler box dusting

Top of walk-in cooler box dusting removes dust and debris from the top panels of walk-in coolers and freezers, including condensing units and lines located on top of the box.

Why it matters

The top of a walk-in is a hidden dust shelf in a food facility. Health inspectors and food safety auditors check it, and debris there can attract pests.

How we do it

Crews access the top safely from a ladder or lift, remove debris by hand, HEPA vacuum panels and damp-wipe with food-safe cleaners. Refrigeration components are left to the refrigeration contractor.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of walk-in boxes and panel area on top
  • Clearance between box top and ceiling
  • Refrigeration equipment on top
  • Food-safe products required

Watch-outs

  • Do not walk on cooler tops unless the manufacturer confirms they can carry the load.
  • Refrigeration lines and coils belong to the refrigeration contractor.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Beyond Klean can put this surface on a recurring high-dusting schedule after the building opens. Ask for a recurring bid.

Common questions

Do health inspectors check the top of walk-in coolers?

Many do, because it is a common place for dust, debris and pest activity in food facilities.

Can you stand on top of a walk-in cooler?

Only if the manufacturer confirms it is rated for foot traffic. Most crews work from a ladder or lift.

What equipment sits on top of a walk-in cooler?

Often condensing units, refrigerant lines and electrical conduit, plus construction debris.

Is cleaning the top of a walk-in a construction or maintenance task?

Both. It should be done at turnover and then on a routine schedule.

What else is top of walk-in cooler box dusting called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "cooler top cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
