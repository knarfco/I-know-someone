Insulated metal panel wall cleaning

Insulated metal panel wall cleaning cleans the metal-faced insulated panels used for coolers, freezers, food processing rooms and cold storage, removing film, dust and residue with food-safe, non-abrasive products.

Why it matters

Metal panels in cold rooms collect dust and film, and must be clean for food safety.

How we do it

Crews remove protective film, wash panels with food-safe cleaners and soft brushes, rinse and dry, paying attention to seams and corners.

Watch-outs

  • Abrasive pads scratch panel coatings and create places for bacteria to grow.
  • Moisture left in seams of cold rooms can freeze.

Common questions

Where are insulated metal panels used?

In walk-in coolers and freezers, cold storage, food processing rooms and some clean manufacturing spaces.

How are insulated metal panels cleaned?

With food-safe, non-abrasive cleaners and soft brushes, followed by rinsing and drying.

Can moisture damage insulated metal panels?

Water left in seams of cold rooms can freeze and damage seals, so panels are dried.

Are insulated panels inspected in food facilities?

Yes, inspectors and auditors check that walls are clean, intact and cleanable.

What else is insulated metal panel wall cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "cooler wall panel cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
