Trim, casing and crown molding cleaning

Trim, casing and crown molding cleaning removes dust, caulk smears, paint drips and fingerprints from door casings, window trim and crown molding.

Also called trim cleaning

Why it matters

Trim has many small ledges and profiles that hold dust. Caulk and paint on trim are common punch items.

How we approach it

Crews dust profiles with microfiber, remove caulk smears carefully and spot-clean painted trim gently.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of trim and crown
  • Paint and caulk residue
  • Profiles
  • Heights

Watch-outs

  • Scrubbing fresh paint damages it.
  • Caulk smears may need touch-up rather than removal.

Questions about trim, casing and crown molding cleaning

Why is crown molding dusty?

Its top edge is a ledge that collects dust.

How are caulk smears on trim removed?

Carefully, with plastic tools and mild removers.

Is trim cleaning part of final clean?

Yes.

Should trim be dusted before floors?

Yes, clean high before low.

What else is trim, casing and crown molding cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "trim cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
