Trim, casing and crown molding cleaning

Trim, casing and crown molding cleaning removes dust, caulk smears, paint drips and fingerprints from door casings, window trim and crown molding.

Why it matters

Trim has many small ledges and profiles that hold dust. Caulk and paint on trim are common punch items.

How we do it

Crews dust profiles with microfiber, remove caulk smears carefully and spot-clean painted trim gently.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of trim and crown
  • Paint and caulk residue
  • Profiles
  • Heights

Watch-outs

  • Scrubbing fresh paint damages it.
  • Caulk smears may need touch-up rather than removal.

Common questions

Why is crown molding so dusty after construction?

Its top edge forms a ledge near the ceiling where drywall and sanding dust settle.

How are caulk smears on trim removed?

Carefully, with plastic tools and mild removers, so the caulk joint and paint are not damaged.

Is trim cleaning part of the final clean?

Yes, door casings, window trim and crown molding are dusted and wiped.

Should trim be cleaned before floors?

Yes, cleaning high before low keeps dust from falling onto clean floors.

What else is trim, casing and crown molding cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "trim cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
