Window label and sticker removal

Window label and sticker removal takes off manufacturer labels, energy rating stickers and handling labels from new glass without scratching it or leaving adhesive.

Also called glass sticker removal; NFRC label removal

Why it matters

Every new window arrives with labels, and they are a standard final clean and punch item. Removing them badly leaves adhesive or scratches.

How we approach it

Crews confirm whether inspectors need labels in place, peel labels slowly, and remove adhesive with glass-safe removers and lubricated blades where allowed.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of windows with labels
  • Code inspection requirements for labels
  • Glass coatings
  • Adhesive removers

Watch-outs

  • Energy labels may need to stay until inspection.
  • Dry scraping can scratch glass.

Questions about window label and sticker removal

Should window stickers be removed before inspection?

Ask the GC; energy labels sometimes must stay until the inspector sees them.

How do you remove adhesive from window labels?

With glass-safe removers and careful, lubricated scraping.

Can heat help remove window stickers?

Gentle warming can soften adhesive.

Who removes window labels?

The cleaning crew at final clean.

What else is window label and sticker removal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "glass sticker removal" and "NFRC label removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
