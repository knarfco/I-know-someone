High-rise exterior glass cleaning

High-rise exterior glass cleaning cleans windows on tall buildings using rope access, swing stages or building maintenance units, by certified crews with fall-protection plans.

Why it matters

High-rise window work is one of the most hazardous cleaning tasks. It requires engineered anchors, trained crews and specific insurance.

How we do it

Only qualified specialists are assigned, with verified training, insurance and a written plan that matches the building's anchorage.

Watch-outs

Common questions

Who cleans high-rise windows after construction?

Specialized contractors trained and equipped for rope access, swing stages or building maintenance units.

What is rope access window cleaning?

Cleaning performed by technicians suspended on ropes from certified roof anchors, using two independent lines.

Are roof anchors required for high-rise window cleaning?

Yes, certified anchors or permanent access equipment are required for suspended work.

How does weather affect high-rise window cleaning?

High winds, storms and lightning stop work for safety, which can affect schedules.

What else is high-rise exterior glass cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "rope access window cleaning" and "swing stage window cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
