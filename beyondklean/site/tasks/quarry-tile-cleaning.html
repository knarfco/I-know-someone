Quarry tile cleaning

Quarry tile cleaning cleans and degreases unglazed quarry tile floors in commercial kitchens and food areas, removing construction dust, grout haze and grease so floors are clean and slip-resistant for health inspection.

Also called quarry tile degreasing; quarry tile kitchen floor degreasing; kitchen floor degreasing

Why it matters

Quarry tile is porous and absorbs grease, and kitchens need clean, slip-resistant floors for health inspection.

How we approach it

Crews scrub with degreasers and deck brushes or machines, then rinse and squeegee to drains.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Kitchen area
  • Grease level
  • Drains
  • Sealer

Watch-outs

  • Grease left on quarry tile makes floors dangerously slippery.
  • Some strong degreasers and acids damage grout.

Questions about quarry tile cleaning

What is quarry tile?

A dense, unglazed clay tile used in commercial kitchens because it is durable and slip-resistant.

How are quarry tile floors degreased?

With kitchen degreasers, deck brushes or floor machines, then rinsed and squeegeed to the floor drains.

Should quarry tile be sealed?

Often yes, to help resist grease and staining, following the tile manufacturer's guidance.

Why is quarry tile used in commercial kitchens?

It handles heavy traffic, heat and water and stays slip-resistant.

What else is quarry tile cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "quarry tile degreasing", "quarry tile kitchen floor degreasing" and "kitchen floor degreasing". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
