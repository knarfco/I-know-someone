Model unit and show suite cleaning

Model unit and show suite cleaning keeps model apartments, model homes and sales suites spotless during construction and lease-up, with scheduled cleaning and touch-ups before tours.

Also called model home cleaning

Why it matters

Models sell the project. They are toured repeatedly while construction continues nearby, so dust settles daily and fingerprints accumulate.

How we approach it

Crews clean models on a recurring schedule, touch up before scheduled tours and events, and care for staging furniture and decor with gentle methods.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of models and sales suites
  • Tour and event schedule
  • Staging furniture and decor
  • Recurring frequency

Watch-outs

  • Construction dust from nearby work settles daily.
  • Staging decor is often rented; handle with care.

Questions about model unit and show suite cleaning

Why do model units need frequent cleaning?

They are toured constantly while construction continues nearby, so dust and fingerprints build up quickly.

How often should a model unit be cleaned?

Commonly weekly, with touch-ups before major tours or events.

Is staging furniture cleaned?

Yes, furniture and decor are dusted and wiped gently.

Who arranges model unit cleaning?

The developer or the leasing and sales team, usually on a recurring schedule through lease-up.

What else is model unit and show suite cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "model home cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
