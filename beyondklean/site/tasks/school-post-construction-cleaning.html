School post-construction cleaning

School post-construction cleaning prepares new or renovated K-12 and campus buildings for students, covering classrooms, corridors, gyms, cafeterias, restrooms and offices before the first day of school.

Why it matters

Schools open on fixed dates set by the academic calendar. Many identical rooms must reach the same standard, and students and staff arrive days after construction ends.

How we do it

Crews work room by room with consistent checklists, use low-odor products, and schedule gym and cafeteria cleaning around floor finishing and kitchen inspection.

Common questions

When should a new school be cleaned before opening?

After trades finish and before furniture and staff move in, with a touch-up pass in the days before students arrive.

Do school cleaning crews need background checks?

Many school districts require background checks for anyone working on school property, so crews are screened before they are assigned.

Why do schools ask for low-odor cleaning products?

Students and staff arrive soon after cleaning, and strong odors cause complaints and can affect sensitive students.

Is the school cafeteria kitchen cleaned for health inspection?

Yes. Cafeteria kitchens are cleaned to food service standards before the health department's opening inspection.

What else is school post-construction cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "school construction cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
