Pendant and suspended light cleaning

Pendant and suspended light cleaning removes construction dust from decorative and linear pendants, their shades, diffusers, cables and canopies, usually in offices, restaurants and retail spaces.

Also called pendant cleaning

Why it matters

Pendants hang in sightlines over tables, counters and desks. Dust on their tops and shades is visible, and anything that falls lands where people eat and work.

How we approach it

Crews steady each pendant by hand, vacuum tops with soft HEPA brushes, then clean shades with methods matched to the material: glass, metal, fabric or acrylic.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Pendant count, sizes and materials
  • Mounting height and access over furniture
  • Fragile or specialty fixtures
  • Manufacturer care guidance

Watch-outs

  • Do not pull or swing pendants by their cables; it can loosen connections or knock them out of level.
  • Fabric shades should be cleaned dry.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about pendant and suspended light cleaning

How do you clean a pendant light without moving it?

Hold the pendant steady with one hand while cleaning with the other, using a soft brush vacuum and microfiber.

Can glass pendant shades be removed for cleaning?

Only if they are designed to be removed; many decorative shades are cleaned in place to avoid breakage.

Why clean pendant lights before a restaurant opens?

Dust from pendant tops falls onto tables and food, and pendants are at eye level for guests.

Do fabric pendant shades need special cleaning?

Yes, they are cleaned with a soft dry brush or low-suction vacuum, never liquid.

What else is pendant and suspended light cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "pendant cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
