Kitchen hood exterior cleaning

Kitchen hood exterior cleaning cleans the outside surfaces of commercial kitchen hoods, including the canopy, edges, lights and accessible filter frames, without entering the hood plenum or ductwork.

Also called hood exterior cleaning

Why it matters

Hoods are the most visible equipment in a kitchen. Dust and film on them are seen by inspectors and owners.

How we approach it

Crews clean exterior stainless with food-safe cleaners, wipe light lenses and leave filters and plenum interiors to certified hood cleaners.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Hood count and size
  • Exterior surfaces
  • Light fixtures
  • Coordination with hood cleaning contractor

Watch-outs

  • Hood interiors and ducts are a regulated specialty.
  • Fire suppression nozzles must not be disturbed.

Questions about kitchen hood exterior cleaning

Is hood exterior cleaning the same as hood cleaning?

No. Exterior cleaning covers visible surfaces; hood and duct cleaning is regulated work under NFPA 96.

Can cleaning crews touch fire suppression nozzles?

No. Suppression systems are serviced only by licensed contractors.

Should hood filters be cleaned at turnover?

They should be clean and properly installed, often by the hood contractor.

Why clean hood exteriors before opening?

They are visible and collect construction dust.

What else is kitchen hood exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "hood exterior cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
