Kitchen hood exterior cleaning

Kitchen hood exterior cleaning cleans the outside surfaces of commercial kitchen hoods, including the canopy, edges, lights and accessible filter frames, without entering the hood plenum or ductwork.

Why it matters

Hoods are the most visible equipment in a kitchen. Dust and film on them are seen by inspectors and owners.

How we do it

Crews clean exterior stainless with food-safe cleaners, wipe light lenses and leave filters and plenum interiors to certified hood cleaners.

Common questions

Is kitchen hood exterior cleaning the same as hood cleaning?

No. Exterior cleaning covers the visible canopy and edges. Hood plenum and grease duct cleaning is regulated work under NFPA 96 done by certified contractors.

Can cleaning crews touch fire suppression nozzles in a kitchen hood?

No. Suppression nozzles, links and piping are serviced only by licensed fire suppression contractors and must not be disturbed.

Should hood filters be cleaned before a restaurant opens?

Filters should be clean and correctly installed before cooking starts. The hood contractor or kitchen staff usually handles them.

Why clean hood exteriors before opening?

Hoods are the most visible equipment in the kitchen, and construction dust on them is noticed by inspectors and owners.

What else is kitchen hood exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "hood exterior cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
