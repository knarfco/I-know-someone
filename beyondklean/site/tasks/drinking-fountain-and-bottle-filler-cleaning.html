Drinking fountain and bottle filler cleaning

Drinking fountain and bottle filler cleaning cleans the exteriors, basins, spouts, grilles and drains of drinking fountains and bottle filling stations, removing labels, film and construction dust.

Also called water fountain cleaning

Why it matters

Fountains are used immediately after occupancy and must be clean. Construction dust and labels are common.

How we approach it

Crews remove labels and film, clean basins and exteriors with food-safe cleaners and wipe spouts.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Units and types
  • Finishes
  • Labels
  • Filter status

Watch-outs

  • Do not change filters unless authorized.
  • Use food-safe products near spouts.

Questions about drinking fountain and bottle filler cleaning

How are drinking fountains cleaned after construction?

Basins, spouts and exteriors are cleaned with food-safe products, and labels and film are removed.

Do bottle filling stations have filters?

Many do. Filters are installed and changed by facility staff or the plumber.

Are drinking fountains part of the final clean?

Yes, they are cleaned with the restrooms and corridors.

Should new drinking fountains be flushed?

Plumbers or facility staff flush new fixtures to clear the lines before use.

What else is drinking fountain and bottle filler cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "water fountain cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
