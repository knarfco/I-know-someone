Room-by-room cleaning checklist sign-off

Room-by-room cleaning checklist sign-off records each room's cleaning against a written checklist and gets the GC's signature before the owner walk, so nothing is missed and completion is documented.

Why it matters

Large buildings have hundreds of rooms. Without checklists, rooms get skipped and items like cabinet interiors and door tops are forgotten.

How we do it

Crews complete a checklist per room, the crew lead inspects, and the GC walks and signs off rooms or floors before the owner walk.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Checklist items per room type
  • Inspection process

Watch-outs

  • Checklists that are too generic miss room-specific items.
  • Sign-off should happen before trades return for punch work.

Common questions

Why use room-by-room cleaning checklists?

They make sure every item in every room is cleaned and give a clear record of completion.

Who signs off on cleaning checklists?

The cleaning crew lead first, then the GC's superintendent or project manager.

Are checklists different for each room type?

Yes, restrooms, kitchens, offices and mechanical rooms each need their own items.

What happens to signed checklists?

They are kept for closeout records and help resolve later disputes.

What else is room-by-room cleaning checklist sign-off called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "cleaning checklist". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
