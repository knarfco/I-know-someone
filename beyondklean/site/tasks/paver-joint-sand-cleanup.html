Paver joint sand cleanup

Paver joint sand cleanup sweeps excess joint sand and polymeric sand haze off pavers after installation, so sand does not track into the building and polymeric haze does not harden on the surface.

Also calledpaver sand cleanup

Why it matters

Loose sand on pavers is tracked onto new floors, where it scratches finishes. Polymeric sand left on the surface can harden into a hazy film.

How we do it

Crews sweep and blow excess sand off with leaf blowers or brooms right after installation, working with the installer before polymeric sand is activated.

Watch-outs

  • Polymeric sand haze is hard to remove once activated.
  • Sand tracked inside scratches floors.

Common questions

Why is paver sand tracked into buildings?

Excess sand left on the surface sticks to shoes and wheels, and it scratches new floors.

What is polymeric sand?

Joint sand mixed with binders that harden when wet, used to lock pavers and resist weeds.

Can polymeric sand haze be removed?

It can sometimes be removed with specific haze removers, but prevention is much easier.

Who is responsible for paver sand cleanup?

Usually the paver installer, with final cleanup by the cleaning crew.

What else is paver joint sand cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "paver sand cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
