Door face cleaning

Door face cleaning removes dust, fingerprints, labels, pencil marks and smudges from both faces of wood, metal, laminate and glass doors.

Also called door cleaning

Why it matters

Doors are touched by everyone and inspected closely. Factory labels, handling marks and overspray on door faces are frequent punch items.

How we approach it

Crews remove labels, clean faces with finish-safe cleaners and microfiber, and check both sides of every door against a door schedule.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Door count by type and finish
  • Labels and protective films
  • Overspray or adhesive
  • Door schedule for tracking

Watch-outs

  • Wood door finishes can be damaged by harsh cleaners.
  • Do not remove fire rating labels.

Questions about door face cleaning

How should new wood doors be cleaned?

With mild, wood-safe cleaners and soft cloths.

Why are door faces a common punch item?

They collect fingerprints, labels and marks from trades.

Should door labels be removed?

Factory stickers yes; fire rating labels no.

Are both sides of doors cleaned?

Yes, plus edges and tops.

What else is door face cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "door cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
