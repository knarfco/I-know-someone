Vacant space turnover cleaning

Vacant space turnover cleaning cleans empty commercial suites, retail spaces and units between tenants or after renovation, so they can be shown, inspected or leased.

Why it matters

Vacant spaces collect dust, dead insects and debris, and prospective tenants form opinions in the first minute of a showing.

How we do it

Crews clean the full space top down, remove leftover debris, clean restrooms and glass, and offer touch-ups before showings.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Space size and condition
  • Utilities status: water and power
  • Showing schedule
  • Debris left by previous tenant

Watch-outs

  • Without water or power, some tasks may need workarounds.
  • Spaces re-soil while vacant; plan touch-ups.

Common questions

What is vacant space turnover cleaning?

Cleaning an empty space so it is ready to show, inspect or lease.

How often should a vacant space be cleaned?

Once thoroughly when it becomes vacant, then a quick touch-up before each showing, because dust settles while it sits empty.

Do utilities need to be on for turnover cleaning?

Water and power make it much easier; crews can work around them if needed.

Who arranges vacant space cleaning?

Usually the property manager or landlord, sometimes the leasing broker.

What else is vacant space turnover cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "vacant tenant turnover". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
