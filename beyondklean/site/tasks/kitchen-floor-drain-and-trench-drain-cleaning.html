Kitchen floor drain and trench drain cleaning

Kitchen floor drain and trench drain cleaning removes debris, grout and mortar from kitchen floor drains, floor sinks and trench drains.

Also called trench drain cleaning

Why it matters

Kitchen drains handle heavy water loads. Construction debris causes backups and odors.

How we approach it

Crews remove grates, clear debris, vacuum and reinstall grates.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Drain types and count
  • Grates
  • Debris
  • Plumber coordination

Watch-outs

  • Grates can be heavy.
  • Do not wash debris into drains.

Questions about kitchen floor drain and trench drain cleaning

What is a floor sink?

A drain receptor for equipment drains.

Why clean trench drains?

Debris causes backups.

Who clears clogged drains?

Plumbers.

Are drains checked at health inspection?

Often.

What else is kitchen floor drain and trench drain cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "trench drain cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
