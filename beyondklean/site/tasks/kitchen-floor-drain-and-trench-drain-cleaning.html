Kitchen floor drain and trench drain cleaning

Kitchen floor drain and trench drain cleaning removes grout, mortar, debris and dust from commercial kitchen floor drains, floor sinks and trench drains, and checks that grates and strainers are clean and in place.

Why it matters

Kitchen drains handle large volumes of water from the first day. Construction debris causes backups, standing water and odors, and inspectors check drains at opening.

How we do it

Crews lift grates, remove debris by hand and with vacuums, clean grates and strainers and report slow drains to the plumber instead of flushing debris further down.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Drain types and count
  • Grates and strainers
  • Debris present
  • Plumber coordination

Watch-outs

  • Grates are heavy; lift with proper technique and gloves.
  • Washing debris into drains moves the clog further down the line.

Common questions

What is a floor sink in a commercial kitchen?

A recessed drain receptor that collects indirect waste from equipment like ice machines, steam tables and sinks.

Why clean trench drains before a kitchen opens?

Construction debris causes backups and standing water as soon as the kitchen starts using water.

Who clears a clogged kitchen drain?

The plumber, using drain equipment. Cleaners remove debris they can reach and report slow drains.

Are kitchen drains checked at health inspection?

Often. Inspectors look for clean, working drains and no standing water.

What else is kitchen floor drain and trench drain cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "trench drain cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
