Door threshold and sweep cleaning

Door threshold and sweep cleaning removes debris, mortar, sealant and dirt from door thresholds, saddles, sweeps and bottom seals at entrances and interior doors.

Also called threshold cleaning

Why it matters

Thresholds collect mortar, sealant and grit from construction. Dirty sweeps let air and water in and look unfinished.

How we approach it

Crews scrape and clean thresholds, vacuum sweeps and wipe metal surfaces, taking care not to damage seals.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of thresholds
  • Materials
  • Sealant and mortar residue
  • Door sweeps and seals

Watch-outs

  • Scraping can damage threshold finishes.
  • Do not pull or adjust sweeps.

Questions about door threshold and sweep cleaning

What is a door threshold?

The strip at the bottom of a doorway that bridges two floors and helps seal the door.

Why clean door sweeps after construction?

Debris in sweeps prevents a good seal against air, water and pests.

How is mortar removed from a threshold?

Carefully, with scrapers and cement removers safe for the threshold finish.

Are thresholds part of the final clean?

Yes. Thresholds and sweeps at every exterior and interior door are cleaned during the final clean.

What else is door threshold and sweep cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "threshold cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
