Pre-flush-out final cleaning

Pre-flush-out final cleaning completes the final clean before the building flush-out, so dust is removed before air is circulated through the system.

Also called flush-out prep clean

Why it matters

Flushing out a dusty building spreads dust through the HVAC system.

How we approach it

Crews schedule final cleaning before flush-out and coordinate with commissioning.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Flush-out schedule
  • Final cleaning
  • Commissioning
  • Filters

Watch-outs

  • Flush-out before cleaning spreads dust.
  • Schedule coordination is critical.

Questions about pre-flush-out final cleaning

What is a building flush-out?

Running HVAC with outdoor air to remove contaminants.

Why clean before flush-out?

So dust is not spread.

Who schedules flush-out?

The GC and commissioning agent.

Is flush-out required?

On some green building projects.

What else is pre-flush-out final cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "flush-out prep clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
