Access panel cleaning

Access panel cleaning removes dust, fingerprints and overspray from ceiling and wall access doors, including their frames and hardware.

Also called ceiling hatch cleaning

Why it matters

Access panels are opened by trades during construction and get fingerprints and smudges. They also collect dust on their frames.

How we approach it

Crews wipe panel faces and frames with damp microfiber and check that panels are fully closed after cleaning.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of access panels
  • Finishes
  • Fingerprints and overspray
  • Locking hardware

Watch-outs

  • Do not leave panels open.
  • Painted panels may need touch-up.

Questions about access panel cleaning

Why are access panels dirty?

Trades open them repeatedly with dirty hands.

Are access panels cleaned at final clean?

Yes.

What if an access panel is painted shut?

Report it so the painter or trade can free it.

Do access panels need to stay closed?

Yes, especially fire-rated panels.

What else is access panel cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "ceiling hatch cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
