Engineered wood floor cleaning

Engineered wood floor cleaning cleans engineered hardwood floors with methods and products that protect the factory finish and the thin real-wood wear layer.

Also called engineered floor cleaning

Why it matters

Engineered wood has a thin wear layer and factory finish. Wrong cleaners can dull or damage it.

How we approach it

Crews use dry methods first, then the manufacturer's recommended cleaner with barely damp microfiber, keeping water away from seams.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Area
  • Manufacturer
  • Finish type
  • Seams

Watch-outs

  • Water at seams can swell the core and edges.
  • Some cleaners leave residue that dulls the factory finish.

Questions about engineered wood floor cleaning

What is engineered wood flooring?

Flooring with a real wood top layer bonded to plywood or fiberboard layers.

How is engineered wood cleaned?

With dry methods and the manufacturer's recommended cleaner on barely damp microfiber.

Can engineered wood floors be refinished?

Sometimes, depending on the thickness of the wear layer.

Is engineered wood sensitive to water?

Yes. Water at seams and edges can swell the core and lift the wear layer, so cleaning uses minimal moisture.

What else is engineered wood floor cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "engineered floor cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
