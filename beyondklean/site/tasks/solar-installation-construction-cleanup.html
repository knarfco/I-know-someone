Solar installation construction cleanup

Solar installation construction cleanup clears packaging, pallets, cardboard, wire scraps, hardware and trash from solar farm and rooftop array sites during and after installation, leaving rows, roads and equipment pads ready for inspection.

Why it matters

A large solar build produces enormous amounts of cardboard and pallets. Loose debris blows into panels, blocks drainage, and leaves hardware in rows where mowers and vehicles travel.

How we do it

Crews walk rows in lines, pick and bag debris, stack pallets, collect hardware with magnets, and coordinate with the electrical contractor on energized blocks before entering.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Acreage or megawatt size
  • Cardboard and pallet volume
  • Energized blocks and access rules
  • Recycling and haul-off arrangements
  • Row and road walk frequency

Watch-outs

  • Energized blocks are entered only with the electrical contractor's approval.
  • Wind can scatter cardboard across rows if it is not secured.
  • Crews never step on or lean against installed modules.

Common questions

How is cardboard handled on a solar farm build?

It is collected daily, flattened and loaded into recycling containers. Leaving it loose invites wind to scatter it across rows and neighboring property.

Do you clean solar panels during construction cleanup?

Panel washing is a separate scope. Construction cleanup focuses on debris, packaging and hardware. Module cleaning can be added under the site's electrical rules if requested.

Why pick up hardware and wire scraps between rows?

Loose bolts, clips and wire scraps damage mowers and tires and can puncture cables. Magnetic sweeps and line walks remove them before the site is handed over.

Can cleanup crews work in energized solar blocks?

Only with the electrical contractor's approval and under site safety rules. Crews avoid touching combiner boxes, cables and modules, and stay on approved walking paths.

How often should a solar project site be cleaned?

Daily debris pickup near active installation, with full row walks weekly and a final sweep before substantial completion and the owner's inspection.

How is solar construction cleanup priced?

Often by the day or by lot for a block of rows, based on acreage, packaging volume and how much hauling is included.

What else is solar installation construction cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "solar farm construction cleanup" and "solar project cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
