Overhead exhaust fan exterior cleaning

Overhead exhaust fan exterior cleaning removes dust and debris from wall and ceiling exhaust fans, their grilles, guards and housings in restrooms, kitchens, warehouses and mechanical spaces.

Why it matters

Exhaust fans pull air across their grilles, so dust builds up fast and is visible. In kitchens and restrooms, dirty exhaust grilles are a common inspection note.

How we do it

With fans off, crews vacuum grilles and guards with HEPA brush heads and damp-wipe housings. Grilles that are designed to be removed are cleaned and reinstalled.

Watch-outs

  • Large exhaust fans must be locked out before guards are touched.
  • Kitchen exhaust hoods and grease ducts are a separate regulated specialty.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Beyond Klean can put this surface on a recurring high-dusting schedule after the building opens. Ask for a recurring bid.

Common questions

Do restroom exhaust fans need cleaning at turnover?

Yes. Their grilles collect drywall dust during construction and show it clearly.

Is kitchen exhaust fan cleaning the same as hood cleaning?

No. Hood and grease duct cleaning is regulated under NFPA 96 and done by certified contractors.

Should exhaust fans be turned off before cleaning?

Yes, and larger fans should be locked out by the authorized trade.

Can exhaust grilles be removed for cleaning?

If they are designed to be removed, yes, and they are reinstalled afterward.

What else is overhead exhaust fan exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "exhaust fan dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
