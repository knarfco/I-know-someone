Property management turnover cleaning

Property management turnover cleaning cleans spaces for property managers between tenants or after work orders, following the manager's checklist and timeline.

Why it matters

Property managers need fast, consistent turnovers to keep vacancy low. Each day a unit sits dirty is lost rent.

How we do it

Crews follow the property manager's checklist, take photos for the file and report damage or repairs needed.

Watch-outs

  • Turnaround windows are often short.
  • Damage must be reported, not cleaned over.

Common questions

What is property management turnover cleaning?

Cleaning between tenants or after work, to the property manager's standard.

Who sets the turnover checklist?

The property manager sets the checklist, often with before-and-after photos required for the file.

How fast is a typical turnover?

Often within a few days, depending on size and condition.

Do turnover crews report damage?

Yes, damage and repair needs are reported to the manager.

What else is property management turnover cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "PM turnover". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
