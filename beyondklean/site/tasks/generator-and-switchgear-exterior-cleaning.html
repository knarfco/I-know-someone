Generator and switchgear exterior cleaning

Generator and switchgear exterior cleaning cleans the outside of emergency generators, enclosures and switchgear lineups under the direction of the electrical contractor. It is conditional work.

Also called switchgear dusting

Why it matters

Generators and switchgear are critical life-safety and power equipment, often energized or ready to start automatically.

How we approach it

Requests are reviewed and routed only with the electrical contractor's approval, using dry methods and respecting automatic start hazards.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Equipment and energized status
  • Electrical contractor approval
  • Automatic start lockout
  • Dry cleaning methods

Watch-outs

  • Generators can start automatically.
  • Arc-flash boundaries must be respected.

Questions about generator and switchgear exterior cleaning

Can cleaning crews clean a generator?

Only the exterior, with approval and lockout from the responsible trade.

Why is switchgear cleaning conditional?

It is energized, high-voltage equipment with serious hazards.

Who cleans inside switchgear?

Qualified electrical workers.

Why are generators dangerous to clean?

They can start automatically during a power event.

What else is generator and switchgear exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "switchgear dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
