Overhead piping exterior cleaning

Overhead piping exterior cleaning removes dust, overspray and labels from exposed plumbing, gas, process and hydronic piping, insulation jackets and hangers in open-ceiling and mechanical spaces.

Also called pipe dusting

Why it matters

Pipe runs are long and stacked, so they hold a surprising amount of dust. In food facilities, dirty overhead piping above prep or storage areas is an inspection finding.

How we approach it

HEPA vacuum with brush heads, then damp microfiber on painted pipe and PVC jacketing. Insulation is vacuumed gently without compressing it. Pipe identification labels are left in place.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of piping by system
  • Insulated versus bare pipe and jacket types
  • Height and access
  • Gas and process lines that require special care

Watch-outs

  • Do not step on, hang from or lean on piping or hangers.
  • Pipe identification labels and flow arrows are required; do not remove them.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about overhead piping exterior cleaning

Should pipe labels be removed during cleaning?

No. Pipe identification and flow arrows are required for safety and maintenance. Clean around them.

Can insulated pipes be wiped?

PVC or metal jacketing can be damp-wiped. Exposed fiberglass insulation should only be vacuumed gently.

Why does overhead piping matter in a kitchen?

Dust and debris above food prep and storage can fall into food. Health inspectors look up.

Is overhead piping cleaned with the ductwork?

Usually yes, in the same lift pass, to save time and equipment.

What else is overhead piping exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "pipe dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
