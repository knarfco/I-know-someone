Glass & Windows · Construction window cleaning

Interior window cleaning

Interior window cleaning at construction turnover cleans the inside face of every window, plus frames, sills and tracks, removing dust, labels, paint specks and fingerprints.

Why it matters

Interior glass is seen every day by occupants, and construction residue on it shows in sunlight. It is one of the last finishing touches before an owner walk.

How we do it

Crews clean frames and sills first, scrape only where the glass type allows with fresh lubricated blades, then wash and squeegee the glass and detail edges.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number and size of windows
  • Glass types and coatings
  • Labels and debris on glass
  • Access and heights

Watch-outs

  • Scraping coated or tempered glass can cause scratches; check the glass type first.
  • Cleaning before trades finish leads to re-cleaning.

Common questions

When should interior windows be cleaned on a construction project?

Near the end, after painting, caulking and window treatment installation are complete, and right before the owner walk.

Is interior window cleaning included in a final construction clean?

Usually, but it should be listed in the scope with sills, frames and tracks so nothing is missed.

Why do windows look streaky after cleaning?

Streaks come from dirty water, too much detergent or dust falling from frames that were cleaned after the glass.

Are window sills and frames cleaned with the glass?

Yes, frames and sills are cleaned first so dust does not fall onto clean glass.

What else is interior window cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "inside window washing". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
