Construction IAQ photo documentation

Construction IAQ photo documentation records duct protection, housekeeping and cleaning with dated photos for green building submissions.

Also called IAQ plan photos

Why it matters

Green building credits require proof that IAQ measures were followed, often photos on multiple dates.

How we approach it

Crews photograph protection and cleaning on scheduled dates and organize them for submission.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Required dates
  • Photo subjects
  • Organization
  • Submission

Watch-outs

  • Missing photos can cost the credit.
  • Photos must be dated.

Questions about construction iaq photo documentation

Why are IAQ photos needed?

To document green building compliance.

How many dates are required?

Often at least three.

What do IAQ photos show?

Duct protection, housekeeping and cleaning.

Who submits IAQ documentation?

The GC or LEED consultant.

What else is construction iaq photo documentation called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "IAQ plan photos". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
