Bar joist cleaning

Bar joist cleaning removes construction dust and debris from the open-web steel joists that support the roof or floor deck. Each joist has top and bottom chords and diagonal web members, so cleaning means working around many small surfaces from a lift.

Also called joist dusting; open web joist cleaning

Why it matters

Bar joists are everywhere in warehouses, big-box stores and gyms. Their open webs trap dust on dozens of short ledges per joist, and that dust sheds onto racking, merchandise and court floors.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum chords and web members with narrow HEPA crevice and brush tools, working one joist space at a time so nothing falls into a section already cleaned. Damp microfiber follows only where the joist is painted and the finish allows it.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of joists, span length and joist spacing
  • Clear height to the bottom chord
  • Joist finish: shop primer, painted or galvanized
  • Bridging, bracing and attached hangers included or excluded

Watch-outs

  • Joist bridging and hanger rods create snag points for vacuum hoses; crews must avoid loading or bending them.
  • Do not hang tools, lines or lights from joists without the structural engineer or GC's approval.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about bar joist cleaning

What is a bar joist?

An open-web steel joist made of top and bottom chords connected by a zigzag of web members. It is the lightweight framing seen in most warehouse and retail ceilings.

How long does it take to clean bar joists?

It depends on joist count, height and how dirty they are. The open web makes it slower per square foot than a flat deck, so joists are often priced by the joist space or bay.

Should joists and deck be cleaned at the same time?

Yes. They are cleaned from the same lift position, deck first and then joists, which avoids moving the lift twice through the same bay.

Do joists need cleaning again after the building opens?

In dusty environments like warehouses and stores they do. Quarterly or semiannual high dusting keeps the buildup from becoming a visible problem.

What else is bar joist cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "joist dusting" and "open web joist cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
