Gym and athletic hardwood floor cleaning

Gym and athletic hardwood floor cleaning cleans maple sport floors with methods approved by the floor manufacturer and sport flooring associations.

Also called gym floor cleaning

Why it matters

Gym floors have specialized finishes and game lines. Wrong cleaners make them slippery or damage finish.

How we approach it

Crews dust mop with treated or microfiber mops, then clean with approved gym floor cleaners and minimal moisture, keeping game lines and the finish intact.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Floor area
  • Finish system
  • Game lines
  • Approved products

Watch-outs

  • Wrong cleaners make gym floors slippery and dangerous.
  • Water damages maple sport floors.

Questions about gym and athletic hardwood floor cleaning

How are gym floors cleaned after construction?

With dust mops and gym floor cleaners approved by the floor system manufacturer, using minimal moisture.

Can a gym floor be wet mopped?

Only with minimal moisture from an approved system; standing water damages maple.

Why do gym floors become slippery?

Residue from improper cleaners and dust on the surface reduce traction.

Who recoats a gym floor?

Sport floor finishing contractors recoat gym floors, usually through screen-and-recoat maintenance once a year.

What else is gym and athletic hardwood floor cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "gym floor cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
