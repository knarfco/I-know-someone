Sidewalk pressure washing

Sidewalk pressure washing removes construction mud, concrete slurry, paint, rust and grime from new sidewalks and walkways before turnover, with wash water collected rather than sent to storm drains.

Also called sidewalk cleaning

Why it matters

Sidewalks are the first surface every visitor walks on. Mud and slurry stains also track straight into the new building.

How we approach it

Crews sweep first, pre-treat stains, use surface cleaners at pressures safe for new concrete, and recover or contain wash water.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Square footage of walks
  • Concrete age and finish
  • Stains present
  • Storm drain protection

Watch-outs

  • High pressure on young concrete can etch the surface.
  • Wash water must not enter storm drains.

Questions about sidewalk pressure washing

When can new concrete be pressure washed?

After it has cured enough; very young concrete can be etched by high pressure, so follow the concrete contractor's guidance.

Can pressure washing water go into storm drains?

Generally no. Wash water should be recovered or contained.

What is a surface cleaner?

A rotating pressure washing attachment that cleans flat concrete evenly without stripes.

Is sidewalk cleaning part of final clean?

Yes, exterior walks are usually cleaned before the owner walk.

What else is sidewalk pressure washing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "sidewalk cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
