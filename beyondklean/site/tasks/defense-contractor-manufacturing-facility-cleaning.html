Defense contractor manufacturing facility cleaning

Defense contractor manufacturing facility cleaning is the post-construction cleaning of new or expanded plants that build defense and aerospace products, following the facility's export-control, visitor and safety rules.

Why it matters

Defense plants control who enters and what they see. Production areas also need dust-free conditions before equipment and products move in.

How we do it

Crews follow the facility's visitor, escort and export-control rules, clean only areas they are admitted to and use low-dust methods around production equipment.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Production floor square footage
  • Equipment exteriors as permitted
  • Offices and support areas
  • Escort and visitor rules
  • Low-dust final pass before startup

Watch-outs

  • Export-control and visitor rules can restrict which crew members enter.
  • Photography and phones may be prohibited in production areas.
  • Production equipment may only be touched with owner approval.

Common questions

Do you clean defense manufacturing plants after construction?

Yes. Our crews clean new and expanded production areas, offices and support spaces, following the facility's visitor and export-control rules.

Who sets the access rules at a defense plant?

The facility sets export-control, escort and visitor rules, and our crews follow them exactly, including any limits on phones or cameras.

Can your crews clean around production equipment?

Yes, using HEPA vacuums and damp wiping on equipment exteriors only where the owner approves.

How do you handle restricted areas in a defense plant?

Crews enter only where the facility admits them, with escorts where required, and leave anything off-limits untouched.

Is defense plant cleaning priced differently?

Usually a little higher than standard industrial work because escorts, access steps and documentation slow production.

What else is defense contractor manufacturing facility cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "defense manufacturing cleaning" and "aerospace and defense plant cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
