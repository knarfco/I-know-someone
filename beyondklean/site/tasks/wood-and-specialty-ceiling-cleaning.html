Wood and specialty ceiling cleaning

Wood and specialty ceiling cleaning removes dust from linear wood, baffle, perforated wood, metal mesh and specialty ceiling systems with methods matched to each material.

Also called linear wood ceiling cleaning

Why it matters

Specialty ceilings are often feature elements in lobbies and restaurants. They are expensive, hard to reach and easy to damage.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum with soft brushes and wipe only where the finish allows, following the ceiling manufacturer's care guidance.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Ceiling system and finish
  • Manufacturer guidance
  • Height and access
  • Removable panels

Watch-outs

  • Wood ceilings can water-stain.
  • Panels may come loose if pushed.

Questions about wood and specialty ceiling cleaning

How are wood ceilings cleaned after construction?

With soft brush vacuuming and, if the wood is sealed, barely damp microfiber.

What is a linear wood ceiling?

A ceiling made of parallel wood slats or planks, often with acoustic backing.

Can specialty ceiling panels be removed for cleaning?

Some can, following the manufacturer's instructions; others are cleaned in place.

Who cleans feature ceilings in lobbies and restaurants?

Cleaning crews with lift access and experience with specialty finishes.

What else is wood and specialty ceiling cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "linear wood ceiling cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
