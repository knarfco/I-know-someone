Parking garage construction cleanup

Parking garage construction cleanup removes concrete dust, curing compound residue, form debris, overspray and trash from new parking structures, cleaning decks, ramps, stairs, elevator lobbies and drains so the garage is ready for striping, inspection and opening.

Why it matters

New garages hold large amounts of concrete dust and slurry. Left behind, it clogs drains, ruins striping adhesion and gets tracked into the building on the first day.

How we do it

Crews sweep and scrub decks with ride-on equipment, clean stair towers and lobbies, clear trench drains, and contain wash water rather than flushing it to storm systems.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Deck square footage and levels
  • Striping schedule
  • Trench and floor drains
  • Stair towers and elevator lobbies
  • Wash water containment

Watch-outs

  • Wash water from garage decks must not go straight to storm drains.
  • Striping and sealers need dry decks, so timing matters.
  • Low clearance limits which sweepers and scrubbers fit.

Common questions

How do you clean a new parking garage before it opens?

Power sweep every level, scrub decks to remove slurry and dust, clean stair towers and elevator lobbies, then clear drains so the garage is ready for striping and inspection.

Should a garage be cleaned before or after striping?

Before. Striping paint needs a clean, dry deck. A light touch-up sweep after striping removes any dust that settled while lines cured.

Can garage wash water go down the floor drains?

Only if those drains connect to an approved oil separator or sanitary system. Otherwise water is recovered with the scrubber or contained and disposed of properly.

What equipment fits under low garage ceilings?

Compact ride-on sweepers and scrubbers built for parking structures. Crews check posted clearance and ramp grades before bringing equipment in.

Do you clean garage stairwells and elevator lobbies too?

Yes. Stair towers, handrails, lobby glass, elevator doors and signage are part of a full garage final clean.

How much does parking garage cleanup cost?

Usually priced per square foot of deck, influenced by levels, slurry buildup, drain count and whether wash water must be collected and hauled.

What else is parking garage construction cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "parking structure cleaning" and "new garage cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
