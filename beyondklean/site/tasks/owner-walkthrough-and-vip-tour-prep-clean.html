Owner walkthrough and VIP tour prep clean

An owner walkthrough and VIP tour prep clean makes the areas on a tour route presentable for owners, investors, tenants or officials visiting the jobsite.

Why it matters

Site tours shape how owners and investors see the project. A clean tour route signals a well-run job.

How we do it

Crews clean the tour route, entrances, model areas and restrooms, and clear materials and debris from sightlines without disrupting work elsewhere.

Common questions

What is a VIP tour prep clean?

A focused clean of the route and areas visitors will see during a site tour, including entrances and restrooms.

How far in advance should site tour cleaning be done?

The day before or the morning of the tour, so the route stays clean.

Is the whole site cleaned for an owner walkthrough?

Usually only the tour route and visible areas, while active work zones stay off limits.

Who requests tour prep cleaning?

The GC or the owner's representative requests it, usually a day or two before investors, tenants or officials visit.

What else is owner walkthrough and vip tour prep clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "tour-ready clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
