Polished concrete maintenance clean

Why it matters

Common questions

How do you clean polished concrete floors?

Remove grit with a dust mop or vacuum first, then auto-scrub with a pH-neutral cleaner and the maintenance pads the polishing contractor recommends.

Why does polished concrete look dull after cleaning?

Usually from cleaner residue, the wrong pads or acidic products. Neutral cleaners and proper rinsing restore the shine.

Can polished concrete be waxed?

Polished concrete is normally not waxed. Its shine comes from mechanical polishing and is maintained with pads and cleaners.

Do acidic cleaners damage polished concrete?

Yes, acids etch the surface and leave dull spots that need re-polishing.

What else is polished concrete maintenance clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "polished concrete cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
