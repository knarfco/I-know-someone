Polished concrete maintenance clean

A polished concrete maintenance clean cleans polished and densified concrete floors with neutral cleaners and polishing pads that keep the shine.

Also called polished concrete cleaning

Why it matters

Polished concrete is a finish; the wrong cleaner or pad dulls it, and grit scratches it.

How we approach it

Crews dust mop or vacuum grit first, then auto-scrub with neutral cleaner and diamond-impregnated maintenance pads where recommended.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Polished area
  • Polishing level
  • Grit control
  • Manufacturer pads and cleaners

Watch-outs

  • Acidic cleaners etch polished concrete.
  • Grit acts like sandpaper on polished floors.

Questions about polished concrete maintenance clean

How do you clean polished concrete floors?

Remove grit with a dust mop or vacuum first, then auto-scrub with a pH-neutral cleaner and the maintenance pads the polishing contractor recommends.

Why does polished concrete look dull after cleaning?

Usually from cleaner residue, the wrong pads or acidic products. Neutral cleaners and proper rinsing restore the shine.

Can polished concrete be waxed?

Polished concrete is normally not waxed. Its shine comes from mechanical polishing and is maintained with pads and cleaners.

Do acidic cleaners damage polished concrete?

Yes, acids etch the surface and leave dull spots that need re-polishing.

What else is polished concrete maintenance clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "polished concrete cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
