Open ceiling deck cleaning

Open ceiling deck cleaning removes construction dust, overspray and debris from the exposed metal or concrete deck above a space with no finished ceiling. It is done from lifts near the end of construction, before floors get their final clean and before merchandise or furniture arrives.

Why it matters

In an open-ceiling building the deck is part of the finished room. Dust left on it drifts down onto new floors, fixtures and food areas for months and is the first thing a sharp-eyed owner notices when they look up.

How we do it

Crews work in lift-sized bays from the far wall toward the exit, vacuuming deck flutes with HEPA attachments on extension wands rather than blowing or brushing dust down. Painted decks get only dry or barely damp microfiber so the finish is not streaked.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Total deck area in square feet and the height to the underside of the deck
  • Deck type: painted metal, galvanized, acoustic or concrete
  • Lift type, who supplies it and floor loading limits for the slab
  • Whether MEP runs, joists and fixtures in the same zone are included

Watch-outs

  • Overspray from ceiling paint often hides under dust; vacuuming will not remove it and it should be priced separately if the spec calls for a uniform finish.
  • Lifts must travel on protected floors. Unprotected polished concrete or new VCT can be marked by lift tires.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Beyond Klean can put this surface on a recurring high-dusting schedule after the building opens. Ask for a recurring bid.

Common questions

How do you clean an exposed metal deck without making a mess below?

Capture the dust where it sits. Crews use HEPA vacuums with brush heads on extension poles from a lift and cover finished surfaces below. Dry brushing or blowing simply moves the dust to the floor and fixtures.

Is it worth cleaning a painted deck that looks clean from the floor?

Usually yes. Dust that is invisible from 25 feet still falls as fixtures vibrate and HVAC cycles. Retail and food tenants often find it on product within weeks if the deck is skipped.

When should the deck be cleaned in the construction sequence?

After all overhead trades, including sprinkler, electrical and HVAC balancing, are done above that area, and before the final floor clean. Cleaning before the last overhead trade leaves usually means paying twice.

Can the deck be cleaned in sections while other work continues?

Yes. Large buildings are typically cleaned in bays or column lines that match the lift plan, so other trades can keep working in areas that are not under the lift.

What else is open ceiling deck cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "exposed deck cleaning" and "metal deck dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
