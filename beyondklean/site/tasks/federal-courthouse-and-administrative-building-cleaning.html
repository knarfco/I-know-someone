Federal courthouse and administrative building cleaning

Federal courthouse and administrative building cleaning is the construction final clean of courtrooms, chambers, jury suites, holding areas and offices, carried out within the building's security screening and access rules.

Why it matters

Courtrooms carry high-finish millwork, stone and glass that show every smudge, and court calendars set hard opening dates. Security procedures also add time that must be planned into the clean.

How we do it

Our crews pass through security screening with an approved tool and supply list, clean courtrooms top down, protect millwork finishes and coordinate secure areas with the prime contractor.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Courtrooms, chambers and jury rooms
  • Holding cells and secure corridors
  • Security screening and approved tool lists
  • Millwork, stone and glass finishes

Watch-outs

  • Every tool and chemical may be screened at entry, so submit lists ahead of time.
  • Wood paneling and benches need finish-safe cleaners and soft cloths.
  • Secure corridors and holding areas often require escorted work only.

Common questions

How do cleaning crews get through courthouse security?

Crews pass the building's security screening each day. We submit names, tool lists and chemical lists in advance through the prime contractor so equipment is cleared quickly.

What areas of a courthouse need special cleaning care?

Courtroom millwork, judges' benches, stone floors, jury box seating and glass partitions need finish-safe products and careful detailing, since they are the most visible surfaces.

Are holding cells cleaned during courthouse construction?

Yes, as part of the final clean, with escort where the facility requires. Crews clean detention fixtures, floors and walls and account for every tool before leaving the area.

When should the courthouse final clean be scheduled?

After furniture and audio-visual installation and before the agency walk. Courtrooms often get a second touch-up right before the first scheduled hearing.

Can you clean an administrative building while staff move in?

Yes. We phase floors ahead of the move schedule and keep a small crew on hand to clean up after furniture installers and movers.

Do courthouse projects need background checks for cleaning staff?

Usually. Background checks and badging are completed as the facility requires and are arranged through the prime contractor before crews arrive on site.

What else is federal courthouse and administrative building cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "courthouse construction cleaning" and "administrative building final clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
