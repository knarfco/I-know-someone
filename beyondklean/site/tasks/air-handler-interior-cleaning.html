Air handler interior cleaning

Air handler interior cleaning cleans the coils, drain pans, fan sections, plenums and internal surfaces of air handling units. It is specialty work performed by qualified HVAC cleaning contractors.

Why it matters

If construction dust reached the inside of an air handler, it coats coils and drain pans, reduces efficiency and can affect indoor air quality from the first day of occupancy.

How we do it

Requests are assigned only to qualified HVAC hygiene contractors after review, coordinated with the mechanical contractor and documented for the owner.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Evidence that internal contamination occurred
  • Unit access and lockout
  • Coil cleaning method and chemicals
  • Documentation and verification

Watch-outs

  • Coil fins bend easily; improper methods damage them.
  • Internal cleaning may affect equipment warranties if not coordinated.

Common questions

When does a new air handler need internal cleaning?

When construction dust or debris got inside, usually because the unit ran without proper filtration or was left open during dusty work.

Who cleans the inside of air handlers?

Qualified HVAC cleaning contractors, coordinated with the mechanical contractor so warranties and settings are protected.

Can coil cleaning damage HVAC equipment?

Yes. Excessive pressure bends coil fins and the wrong chemicals corrode them, which is why this is specialist work.

Is air handler interior cleaning documented?

It should be, with before-and-after photos and a written report for the owner's records.

What else is air handler interior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "AHU coil and plenum cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
