Duct exterior cleaning

Duct exterior cleaning removes construction dust, overspray and labels from the outside of exposed ductwork: spiral round, rectangular and oval duct, fittings and hangers. The duct interior is not opened.

Why it matters

Exposed duct is a finished design element in open-ceiling spaces. Its top surface collects dust, and when the HVAC starts, vibration sheds that dust onto floors and fixtures.

How we do it

HEPA vacuuming of the top and sides with brush heads, then damp microfiber with a pH-neutral cleaner on painted or galvanized surfaces. Duct labels and tape are removed only if the spec calls for it.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of exposed duct and approximate diameters
  • Finish: galvanized, painted or double-wall
  • Height and lift access along runs
  • Whether hangers, straps and fittings are included

Watch-outs

  • Duct interior cleaning is a separate regulated specialty; exterior crews do not open ducts.
  • Fresh duct paint can be softened by solvents; use only water-based cleaners.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our sister brand, AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Common questions

Is duct exterior cleaning the same as duct cleaning?

No. Duct exterior cleaning cleans the outside of exposed ductwork. Duct cleaning usually means cleaning the inside of the system, which is a specialty under NADCA standards.

Why does exposed duct look dusty soon after opening?

Dust on the top surface that was not cleaned is shaken loose by airflow and vibration, then settles again in visible bands.

Can spiral duct be cleaned without scratching it?

Yes, with soft brushes and microfiber. Abrasive pads leave visible marks on galvanized and painted duct.

Should duct labels be removed?

Only if the spec or owner wants them removed. Some labels identify systems for maintenance and should stay.

What else is duct exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "ductwork exterior dusting" and "spiral duct cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
