Door kick and push plate polishing

Door kick and push plate polishing cleans protective plates on doors, removing film, adhesive, fingerprints and scuffs from stainless, brass or plastic plates.

Also called kick plate cleaning

Why it matters

Kick and push plates are installed late and are often still covered in film or adhesive at turnover. Stainless plates show fingerprints immediately.

How we approach it

Crews remove film and adhesive, clean with neutral cleaners and finish stainless with the grain.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number and types of plates
  • Film and adhesive
  • Finish direction
  • Door finish around plates

Watch-outs

  • Adhesive removers can damage the door finish around the plate.
  • Abrasive pads scratch stainless and brass plates.

Questions about door kick and push plate polishing

What is a door kick plate?

A metal or plastic plate on the bottom of a door that protects it from carts, feet and wheelchairs.

How are stainless kick plates cleaned?

With a neutral cleaner, then wiped dry with the grain to avoid streaks.

Do push plates come with protective film?

Often, and it should be removed at final clean.

Are kick plates part of the final clean?

Yes, along with push plates, pulls and other door hardware.

What else is door kick and push plate polishing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "kick plate cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
