Curtain wall cleaning

Curtain wall cleaning at turnover cleans large exterior glass and metal wall systems on mid- and high-rise buildings, using qualified access methods.

Also called curtain wall washing

Why it matters

Curtain walls define the building's appearance. Cleaning them requires specialized access, fall protection and knowledge of coated glass and metal finishes.

How we approach it

Qualified crews use building maintenance units, swing stages, rope access or lifts, cleaning glass and metal with approved products and documenting existing damage.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Building height and facade area
  • Access method and anchorage
  • Glass coatings and metal finishes
  • Fall protection and permits

Watch-outs

  • Only trained, insured crews should perform high access work.
  • Coated glass and metal finishes need approved cleaners.

Questions about curtain wall cleaning

What is a curtain wall?

A non-structural exterior glass and metal wall system.

How is a curtain wall cleaned?

With swing stages, building maintenance units, rope access or lifts.

Why is curtain wall cleaning conditional?

It requires specialized access, training and insurance.

Can curtain wall metal be damaged by cleaners?

Yes, use approved cleaners.

What else is curtain wall cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "curtain wall washing". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
