Revolving door cleaning

Revolving door cleaning details the glass wings, canopy, drum enclosure, floor ring, sweeps and hardware of manual or automatic revolving doors with the door secured.

Why it matters

Revolving doors are the front door of many buildings and are highly visible. They have tight spaces and moving parts that need careful cleaning.

How we do it

Crews clean with the door secured, working wing by wing on glass and metal and vacuuming the floor ring and sweeps.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Door type: manual or automatic
  • Glass and metal finishes
  • Floor and sweep condition
  • Security and lockout

Watch-outs

  • Automatic doors must be switched off by authorized staff.
  • Do not adjust sweeps or sensors.

Common questions

How is a revolving door cleaned?

Wing by wing with the door secured, including glass, metal, canopy, floor and sweeps.

Are automatic revolving doors dangerous to clean?

They must be switched off by authorized staff first, because they can move unexpectedly.

Why clean revolving door sweeps?

Debris in the sweeps affects the door's seal and appearance.

Who services revolving doors?

The door manufacturer's technicians or an authorized door contractor.

What else is revolving door cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "revolving door detailing". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
