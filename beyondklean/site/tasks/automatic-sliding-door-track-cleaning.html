Automatic sliding door track cleaning

Automatic sliding door track cleaning removes debris, dust and grit from floor tracks, sensors and glass of automatic entrance doors.

Also called auto door track cleaning

Why it matters

Debris in floor tracks can stop doors from closing or cause service calls. Sensors covered in dust can misread.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum tracks, wipe sensors gently and clean glass, with the door powered off or in manual mode by authorized staff.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of doors
  • Track type
  • Sensor locations
  • Power or mode control

Watch-outs

  • Do not adjust sensors or door settings.
  • Doors must be off or in a safe mode during cleaning.

Questions about automatic sliding door track cleaning

Why do automatic doors stop working after construction?

Debris in floor tracks or dust on sensors are common causes of doors that hesitate or will not close.

How do you clean an automatic sliding door track?

With the door off or in a safe mode, debris is vacuumed out of the track and the track is wiped.

Can cleaning damage automatic door sensors?

Gentle wiping with a dry cloth should not, but sensors must never be moved or adjusted.

Who services automatic doors?

The automatic door contractor, who adjusts sensors and settings.

What else is automatic sliding door track cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "auto door track cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
