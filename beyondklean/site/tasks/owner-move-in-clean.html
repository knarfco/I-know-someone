Owner move-in clean

An owner move-in clean prepares a finished building for the owner or tenant to move in, cleaning after furniture, equipment and IT installation.

Also called move-in cleaning

Why it matters

Move-in brings new dust and packaging after the final clean.

How we approach it

Crews clean after installers and before occupants arrive.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Move-in date
  • Installers
  • Areas
  • Packaging

Watch-outs

  • Installers re-soil spaces.
  • Timing is tight.

Questions about owner move-in clean

What is a move-in clean?

A clean right before occupants move in.

Why clean again before move-in?

Installers create dust.

Is packaging removed?

Yes.

Who arranges move-in cleaning?

The owner or GC.

What else is owner move-in clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "move-in cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
