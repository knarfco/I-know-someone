Indoor air quality testing coordination

Indoor air quality testing coordination is the preparation and scheduling work that gets a finished building ready for IAQ testing by an independent industrial hygienist or testing firm, including final cleaning, flush-out support and access.

Why it matters

Buildings that fail an IAQ test lose days to recleaning and retesting. Good preparation means the test measures the building, not leftover construction dust.

How we do it

Our crews complete the cleaning the test plan requires, coordinate dates with the GC and the independent testing firm, and leave areas closed and undisturbed before sampling. We do not perform testing.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Testing firm and test plan provided by the owner or GC
  • Areas and sample locations
  • Flush-out or HVAC run time before testing
  • Cleaning completion date before sampling
  • Retest cleaning if results fall short

Watch-outs

  • Cleaning products used right before sampling can raise VOC readings.
  • Trades working in a sampled area during testing can invalidate the results.
  • The testing firm, not the cleaning crew, interprets and reports the results.

Common questions

Does Beyond Klean perform indoor air quality testing?

No. Testing is done by independent industrial hygienists or testing firms hired by the owner or GC. Our crews handle the cleaning and site preparation before sampling.

How do you prepare a new building for an air quality test?

By finishing final cleaning, HEPA vacuuming dust from surfaces and grilles, using low-odor products well ahead of sampling, and keeping trades out of test areas.

Why would a new building fail an IAQ test?

Common causes are leftover construction dust, fresh paint and adhesive vapors, and HVAC systems that were not flushed out. Cleaning and timing address the first two.

How long before IAQ sampling should cleaning be finished?

Ideally a day or more, so dust settles and product odors clear. The testing firm's plan sets the exact window, and our crews schedule around it.

What happens if a space fails its air test?

The testing firm reports which results fell short. Our crews reclean the affected areas as the GC directs, and the firm returns to resample.

Is IAQ testing required for LEED or similar programs?

Some green building programs offer credits for testing or flush-out before occupancy. The owner's consultant tracks the credit; we coordinate the cleaning that supports it.

What else is indoor air quality testing coordination called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "IAQ testing coordination" and "air test coordination". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
