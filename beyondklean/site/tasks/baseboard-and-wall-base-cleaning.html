Baseboard and wall base cleaning

Baseboard and wall base cleaning removes dust, adhesive, paint and scuffs from wood baseboards, rubber cove base and tile base along every wall.

Why it matters

Base is at the junction of floor and wall, where dust and debris collect. Adhesive squeeze-out on rubber base and paint on wood base are common punch items.

How we do it

Crews vacuum along the base, wipe with damp microfiber, and remove adhesive and paint with base-safe products.

Watch-outs

  • Solvents can soften rubber base.
  • Wet mops push dirt into base joints.

Common questions

What is cove base?

Rubber or vinyl wall base that curves out at the bottom where the wall meets the floor.

How is adhesive removed from rubber wall base?

With mild removers safe for rubber, because solvents can soften and discolor it.

Are baseboards cleaned before or after floors?

Before the final floor clean, so dust and residue do not land on clean floors.

Why are baseboards a common punch list item?

They collect dust, paint drips, adhesive and scuffs at the most visible floor edge.

What else is baseboard and wall base cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "baseboard cleaning", "cove base cleaning", "fine dust removal from baseboards" and "baseboard dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
