Gutter and downspout debris removal

Gutter and downspout debris removal clears construction debris, shingle grit, sealant tubes and leaves from gutters, downspouts and splash blocks.

Also called gutter cleaning

Why it matters

Clogged gutters overflow and send water against the foundation and facade during the first heavy rain.

How we approach it

Crews clear gutters by hand from ladders or lifts, flush downspouts to confirm flow and report damaged sections.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Gutter length
  • Access
  • Downspout flow testing
  • Splash blocks

Watch-outs

  • Ladder work requires safe setup and footing.
  • Do not bend gutters by leaning ladders on them.

Questions about gutter and downspout debris removal

Why clean gutters on a new building?

Construction debris and grit collect in them and cause overflows in the first storm.

Are downspouts tested?

Yes. Downspouts are flushed with water after cleaning to confirm they flow freely to the ground or storm system.

Who cleans gutters at turnover?

The cleaning crew or the roofer, depending on the contract and how the gutters are accessed.

How is gutter cleaning done safely?

With proper ladder setup or lifts and without leaning on gutters.

What else is gutter and downspout debris removal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "gutter cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
