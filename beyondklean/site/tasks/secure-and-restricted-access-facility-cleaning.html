Secure and restricted-access facility cleaning

Secure and restricted-access facility cleaning is construction cleaning in areas with controlled entry, such as SCIF-type spaces, where crews work escorted, carry no electronics where prohibited and clean only areas the facility admits them to.

Why it matters

Secure spaces cannot be entered casually, and every visit must follow the facility's rules. A cleaning crew that breaks protocol can delay accreditation or occupancy.

How we do it

Crews work escorted, leave phones and devices outside where prohibited, follow sign-in rules and clean only the rooms the facility clears them into.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Secure room square footage
  • Escort scheduling
  • Device-free work requirements
  • Sign-in and sign-out procedures
  • Construction dust removal before inspection

Watch-outs

  • Escort availability often controls the cleaning schedule.
  • Phones, cameras and smart devices may be prohibited inside.
  • Crews must never enter areas they are not admitted to.

Common questions

Do you clean SCIF construction projects?

Our crews clean secure spaces under escort, without electronics where prohibited, and only in areas the facility clears us into, with access arranged through the prime contractor.

How do cleaners work in a restricted area?

They sign in, work with the facility's escort, follow device rules and clean only the rooms they are allowed into.

Can cleaning crews bring phones into secure rooms?

Not where the facility prohibits them. Devices stay outside in the storage the facility provides.

How do escorts affect cleaning time?

Work happens only when escorts are available, so schedules and pricing account for the slower pace.

Who arranges access to secure areas?

The prime contractor and the facility arrange background checks, badging or escorts as the facility requires.

What else is secure and restricted-access facility cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "secure facility cleaning", "restricted area cleaning" and "SCIF construction cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
