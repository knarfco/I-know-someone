Linear light fixture and lens cleaning

Linear light fixture and lens cleaning removes dust, fingerprints and debris from recessed and surface-mounted troffers, linear LED fixtures and their diffusers in ceilings and walls.

Also called troffer lens cleaning

Why it matters

Recessed fixtures in finished ceilings are installed before the final cleaning, and installers' fingerprints and dust on lenses show clearly when the lights are on.

How we approach it

Lenses and diffusers are wiped with dry or damp microfiber, inside surfaces are vacuumed if the lens opens easily, and trim edges are cleaned without pushing on ceiling tiles.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Fixture count and type
  • Lens type: acrylic, polycarbonate, parabolic louver or open
  • Whether lenses are removable
  • Ceiling height and access

Watch-outs

  • Pushing up on ceiling tiles beside fixtures can crack tiles or shift the grid.
  • Some diffusers scratch easily; use soft cloths only.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about linear light fixture and lens cleaning

Should troffer lenses be removed for cleaning?

Only if they are designed to open and dust is inside. Surface wiping is usually enough at turnover.

Why do new light fixtures have fingerprints?

Installers handle lenses and trims during installation. Fingerprints show as smudges when lights are on.

Can linear fixtures be cleaned with glass cleaner?

Many plastic lenses should not be. Use mild soap and water or the manufacturer's cleaner.

Is light fixture cleaning part of final clean?

Yes. Lenses and trims are standard final clean items in finished ceilings.

What else is linear light fixture and lens cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "troffer lens cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
