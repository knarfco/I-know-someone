Floor protection removal and disposal

Floor protection removal and disposal takes up temporary floor protection at the end of the project and captures the dust trapped beneath it.

Also called floor protection removal

Why it matters

Protection holds dust and debris. Removing it carelessly releases dust into clean areas.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum protection surfaces, roll or fold them inward, dispose of them and then clean the floor.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Protection type and area
  • Disposal
  • Floor cleaning after
  • Timing

Watch-outs

  • Dust trapped under protection spreads.
  • Tape residue needs removal.

Questions about floor protection removal and disposal

When is floor protection removed?

Near final clean.

What happens to dust under protection?

It must be captured.

Is floor protection recycled?

Sometimes.

Who removes floor protection?

The cleaning crew or GC.

What else is floor protection removal and disposal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "floor protection removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
