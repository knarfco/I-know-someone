Precast concrete cleaning

Precast concrete cleaning removes construction soil, mortar and sealant smears, rust stains and efflorescence from precast panels and architectural precast, using cleaners and pressures approved for the finish, after test panels confirm results.

Why it matters

Architectural precast is a visible, expensive finish. The wrong acid or pressure can etch the surface, expose aggregate unevenly or change color permanently.

How we do it

Our crews follow the precast manufacturer's cleaning guidance, test a small area first, pre-wet surfaces, use the mildest effective cleaner and rinse thoroughly with low pressure.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Facade square footage and access method
  • Precast finish type: smooth, sandblasted or exposed aggregate
  • Stains present, such as rust, mortar or sealant
  • Manufacturer cleaning guidance and test panel
  • Protection of glass, metal and landscaping

Watch-outs

  • Muriatic acid can etch precast and stain adjacent glass and metal.
  • High pressure close to the surface can scar smooth precast finishes.
  • Sealers applied over uncleaned stains lock the stains in permanently.

Common questions

How do you clean precast concrete panels after construction?

By pre-wetting, applying a cleaner approved for that finish, gentle agitation and low-pressure rinsing, after a test area shows the method works without damage.

Can rust stains be removed from architectural precast?

Often, with products made for rust on concrete. Our crews test first, since some rust stains go deep and can only be lightened, not fully removed.

Is acid washing safe for precast concrete?

Only in diluted forms the manufacturer allows, on pre-wetted surfaces with thorough rinsing. Strong acid can etch the finish and damage nearby glass and metal.

Why is a test panel needed before cleaning precast?

Precast finishes react differently to cleaners and pressure. A small test shows the result in daylight before a whole elevation is cleaned the same way.

What equipment is used to reach precast on tall buildings?

Boom lifts, swing stages or rope access, depending on height and site. Access method is one of the biggest factors in precast cleaning price.

Should precast be cleaned before or after sealing?

Before. Cleaning removes stains and residues first, then the sealer can be applied to a uniform surface without locking in marks.

What else is precast concrete cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "precast panel cleaning" and "architectural precast cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
