Door vision lite cleaning

Door vision lite cleaning cleans both faces of the glass panels set into doors, plus the glazing stops and frames, removing labels, glazing compound and fingerprints while leaving fire rating marks intact.

Also called door glass cleaning

Why it matters

Vision lites are looked through constantly, so smudges, labels and glazing compound are noticed. Fire-rated glass may have required markings.

How we approach it

Crews remove labels, clean glass with appropriate cleaners and wipe stops, leaving any required fire rating etching untouched.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of vision lites
  • Glass type: fire-rated, tempered or wired
  • Labels and glazing compound
  • Stops and frames

Watch-outs

  • Fire-rated glass markings must remain.
  • Wired glass edges can be sharp if damaged.

Questions about door vision lite cleaning

What is a door vision lite?

A glass panel set into a door so people can see through it.

Should labels on door vision lites be removed?

Temporary labels yes; permanent fire rating marks etched into the glass must stay.

Is fire-rated glass cleaned differently?

It is cleaned normally, but its permanent markings must not be removed or scratched.

Are vision lites part of the final clean?

Yes. Both faces of the glass and the glazing stops are cleaned during the final clean, with any temporary labels removed.

What else is door vision lite cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "door glass cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
