Police, fire and emergency services facility cleaning

Police, fire and emergency services facility cleaning is the construction final clean of police stations, fire stations and EMS stations, including apparatus bays, bunk rooms, decontamination rooms, sally ports and dispatch areas.

Why it matters

First responders move in fast and stay around the clock. Concrete dust in bays, debris in decon rooms or dirty dispatch consoles can affect equipment and crews from the first shift.

How we do it

Our crews scrub apparatus bay floors, clean trench drains, detail decon and bunk rooms, and dry-clean dispatch areas, working around station staff if the building is partly occupied.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Apparatus bays and trench drains
  • Bunk rooms, kitchens and fitness areas
  • Decontamination and gear rooms
  • Sally ports, interview rooms and evidence areas
  • Dispatch and communications rooms

Watch-outs

  • Evidence and armory rooms are restricted and usually cleaned under escort.
  • Trench drains clog with construction debris and must be cleared before apparatus move in.
  • Dispatch consoles need dry methods only around live electronics.

Common questions

What does a fire station construction clean include?

Apparatus bay floors, trench drains, bunk rooms, kitchen, restrooms, gear and decon rooms, and offices are cleaned so the station can take apparatus and crews on move-in day.

How are apparatus bay floors cleaned after construction?

Crews sweep, then machine scrub sealed concrete with neutral cleaner, flush debris from trench drains and dry the floor so coatings and line markings stay intact.

Can you clean a police station with officers already inside?

Yes. We phase work around occupied areas, keep restricted rooms closed unless escorted, and stage equipment so it does not block sally ports or stairways.

Do you clean 911 dispatch rooms during construction?

We do, using HEPA vacuums and dry microfiber around consoles and raised floors, and we coordinate with the systems installer before touching any live equipment.

Is an EMS station cleaned differently than an office?

The decontamination room, supply rooms and bays get extra attention. Crews clean them with disinfectant protocols after construction dust is removed.

How long does a fire station final clean take?

A typical single-company station takes two to four crew days, depending on bay size, finishes and how many trades are still finishing punch items.

What else is police, fire and emergency services facility cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "fire station construction cleaning", "police station cleaning" and "EMS station cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
