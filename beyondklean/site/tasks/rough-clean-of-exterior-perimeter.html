Rough clean of exterior perimeter

A rough clean of the exterior perimeter removes debris, packaging, scraps and windblown trash from around the building footprint during construction.

Also called perimeter debris pickup

Why it matters

Perimeter debris blows into neighbors' property and storm drains, blocks exterior work and gives a poor impression to owners and the public.

How we approach it

Crews walk the perimeter, pick up debris, clear building edges for exterior trades and check storm drain protection.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Perimeter length and site areas
  • Exterior trade schedules
  • Storm drain protection
  • Debris disposal

Watch-outs

  • Do not remove erosion control measures.
  • Watch for nails and sharp debris in soil.

Questions about rough clean of exterior perimeter

Why clean the building perimeter during construction?

To prevent windblown debris and keep exterior work areas clear.

How often should the perimeter be cleaned?

Weekly or more on windy or busy sites.

Does perimeter cleaning help with storm water compliance?

Yes, by keeping debris out of drains.

Who handles perimeter cleanup?

The GC, often through cleanup crews.

What else is rough clean of exterior perimeter called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "perimeter debris pickup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
