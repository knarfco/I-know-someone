Rough clean of exterior perimeter

A rough clean of the exterior perimeter removes debris, packaging, scraps and windblown trash from around the building footprint during construction.

Why it matters

Perimeter debris blows into neighbors' property and storm drains, blocks exterior work and gives a poor impression to owners and the public.

How we do it

Crews walk the perimeter, pick up debris, clear building edges for exterior trades and check storm drain protection.

Watch-outs

  • Do not remove erosion control measures.
  • Watch for nails and sharp debris in soil.

Common questions

Why clean the building perimeter during construction?

To prevent windblown debris and keep the area clear for exterior trades.

How often should the construction perimeter be cleaned?

Weekly on most projects, and more often on windy sites or busy phases when packaging and debris pile up quickly.

Does perimeter cleaning help storm water compliance?

Yes, it keeps debris out of drains and inlet protection.

Who handles perimeter cleanup?

The GC is responsible and usually assigns perimeter cleanup to the cleanup crew along with site housekeeping.

What else is rough clean of exterior perimeter called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "perimeter debris pickup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
