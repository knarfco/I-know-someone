Leasing office final clean

A leasing office final clean prepares the leasing center or sales office for its opening, covering the reception area, offices, model kitchen, restrooms, glass and furniture.

Also called leasing office cleaning

Why it matters

The leasing office is the first stop for every prospective resident, and it usually opens while the rest of the property is still under construction.

How we approach it

Crews detail the leasing office first, then schedule frequent touch-ups because construction dust from nearby work keeps settling.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Opening date
  • Furniture and decor installed
  • Glass and entry
  • Touch-up schedule

Watch-outs

  • Construction nearby re-soils the office quickly.
  • Decor and furniture need gentle cleaning.

Questions about leasing office final clean

Why is the leasing office cleaned first?

It opens to prospects before the rest of the property is finished.

How often does a leasing office need cleaning during construction?

Often several times a week, because dust from active construction keeps settling.

Is furniture cleaned in the leasing office?

Yes, furniture and decor are dusted and wiped.

Is the leasing office entry cleaned?

Yes, glass, doors and the approach are cleaned for a strong first impression.

What else is leasing office final clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "leasing office cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
