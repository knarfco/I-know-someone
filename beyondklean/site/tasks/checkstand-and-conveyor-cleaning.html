Checkstand and conveyor cleaning

Checkstand and conveyor cleaning cleans grocery and retail checkout lanes: counters, conveyor belts, bagging areas, impulse racks and the exteriors of scanners, scales and card readers.

Why it matters

Every customer passes through the checkstands. Construction dust and fingerprints on belts and counters are the last thing shoppers see.

How we do it

Crews wipe belts and counters with cleaners safe for the belt material and wipe electronics with dry or barely damp cloths, never spraying devices.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of checkstands
  • Belt material
  • Electronics
  • Bagging and impulse areas

Watch-outs

  • Do not spray liquids on scanners, scales or card readers.
  • Belts can be damaged by solvents.

Common questions

How are checkout conveyor belts cleaned?

With cleaners safe for the belt material and soft cloths, while the belt is stopped.

Are scanners and scales cleaned before opening?

Exteriors are wiped gently, without spraying liquid onto the device.

Are checkstands cleaned at store opening?

Yes. Checkstands are detailed after registers, scanners and belts are installed and before the store opens.

Who services checkstand belts and equipment?

The fixture or equipment vendor services belts, scanners and scales; cleaning crews clean only exterior surfaces.

What else is checkstand and conveyor cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "checkout cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
