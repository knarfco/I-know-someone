Final HVAC filter replacement coordination

Final HVAC filter replacement coordination schedules the change to new, permanent filters after final cleaning and before occupancy.

Also called filter change before occupancy

Why it matters

Filters used during construction are loaded. New filters are required by green building standards before occupancy.

How we approach it

Crews coordinate with the mechanical contractor so final cleaning finishes before filters are changed.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Filter change date
  • Final cleaning schedule
  • Filter types
  • Documentation

Watch-outs

  • Changing filters before final cleaning wastes them.
  • Filter changes are done by the mechanical contractor.

Questions about final hvac filter replacement coordination

When should HVAC filters be replaced?

After final cleaning and before occupancy.

Why replace filters before occupancy?

Construction filters are loaded with dust.

Who replaces filters?

The mechanical contractor.

Does LEED require new filters?

Construction IAQ credits typically do.

What else is final hvac filter replacement coordination called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "filter change before occupancy". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
