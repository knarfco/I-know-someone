Hard Floors · Resilient floors (VCT, LVT, sheet vinyl, rubber)

LVT and LVP manufacturer-approved cleaning

LVT and LVP manufacturer-approved cleaning cleans luxury vinyl tile and plank using the products and methods the flooring manufacturer specifies, protecting the floor warranty.

Also called luxury vinyl cleaning

Why it matters

Many luxury vinyl floors should not be waxed or stripped, and some cleaners can damage the wear layer or void the warranty.

How we approach it

Crews follow the manufacturer's maintenance guide: dust mop, neutral cleaner, approved pads.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Product and manufacturer
  • Maintenance guide
  • Area
  • Adhesive residue

Watch-outs

  • Wax and strippers can void warranties.
  • Excess water at seams can affect some products.

Questions about lvt and lvp manufacturer-approved cleaning

Can LVT be waxed?

Most should not be; follow the manufacturer.

What cleaner is safe for LVT?

A neutral cleaner approved by the manufacturer.

Can LVT warranties be voided by cleaning?

Yes, by unapproved products or methods.

Is LVP the same as LVT?

LVP is the plank format of luxury vinyl.

What else is lvt and lvp manufacturer-approved cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "luxury vinyl cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
