Door hardware polishing

Door hardware polishing cleans and finishes levers, knobs, pulls, hinges, closers and locksets so they are free of fingerprints, paint, film and residue.

Also called hardware cleaning; lever polish

Why it matters

Hardware is touched first and judged first. Paint on hardware, film left on finishes and fingerprints are classic punch items.

How we approach it

Crews remove film and paint carefully, clean with finish-appropriate products and avoid polishes that strip lacquered or specialty finishes.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Hardware finishes
  • Paint on hardware
  • Protective film
  • Closers and electrified hardware

Watch-outs

  • Some metal polishes strip protective lacquer.
  • Do not adjust closers or electrified hardware.

Questions about door hardware polishing

How do you remove paint from door hardware?

Carefully, with plastic tools and mild removers safe for the finish.

Can brass hardware be polished?

Only if it is unlacquered. Lacquered brass should only be cleaned.

Should hardware film be removed at final clean?

Yes.

Why avoid abrasive pads on hardware?

They scratch finishes.

What else is door hardware polishing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "hardware cleaning" and "lever polish". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
