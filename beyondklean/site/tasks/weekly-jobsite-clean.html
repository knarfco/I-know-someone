Weekly jobsite clean

A weekly jobsite clean is a deeper scheduled clean of active work areas, common spaces and site facilities beyond what the daily sweep covers.

Why it matters

Weekly cleaning catches debris and dust that builds up in corners, stairwells and less-used areas, and prepares the site for weekly safety walks.

How we do it

Crews clean floors, stairs, trailers, break areas and debris points, and coordinate with the superintendent on priority areas.

Common questions

What is included in a weekly jobsite clean?

Deeper cleaning of floors, stairs, break areas, trailers and debris points beyond what the daily sweep covers.

When is the weekly jobsite clean usually done?

Often at the end of the week or before scheduled safety walks and owner visits.

Is a weekly clean enough on a busy site?

Usually it is combined with daily sweeps on busy sites.

Who pays for weekly jobsite cleaning?

Usually the GC, sometimes with back-charges to trades that leave excessive debris.

What else is weekly jobsite clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "weekly site cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
