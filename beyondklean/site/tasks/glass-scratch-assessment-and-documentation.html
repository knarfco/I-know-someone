Glass scratch assessment and documentation

Glass scratch assessment and documentation inspects new glass for scratches, chips and defects before or after cleaning and records them with photos and locations.

Also called scratch survey

Why it matters

Scratch disputes are common between glaziers, GCs and cleaners. Documentation before cleaning shows what was already there.

How we approach it

Crews inspect glass in proper light, mark locations on drawings and photograph defects, sharing the report with the GC.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Glass areas to inspect
  • Lighting conditions
  • Documentation format
  • Reporting to GC and glazier

Watch-outs

  • Inspection angle and light affect what is visible.
  • Not all defects are scratches; some are manufacturing defects.

Questions about glass scratch assessment and documentation

Why document glass before cleaning?

To show what defects existed before cleaning.

How are glass scratches documented?

With photos and locations on drawings.

Who pays for scratched glass?

It depends on the cause and contract.

What is the standard for viewing glass defects?

Industry standards specify viewing distance and lighting.

What else is glass scratch assessment and documentation called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "scratch survey". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
