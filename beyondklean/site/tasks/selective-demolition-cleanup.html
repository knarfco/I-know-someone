Selective demolition cleanup

Selective demolition cleanup is the removal of debris, dust and loose material after part of a building is taken out, while the walls, finishes and systems that stay are protected and left clean for the new work.

Why it matters

Partial demo happens right next to finishes the owner is keeping. Dust and debris that drift into retained areas mean damaged floors, dirty ductwork and tenant complaints.

How we do it

Our crews bag and stage debris by material, HEPA vacuum and wet wipe the demo zone, then clean the boundary with retained areas before barriers come down.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Square footage of the demo zone
  • Retained finishes and equipment to protect
  • Barrier and negative air status
  • Debris separation and container location
  • Work hours if the building is occupied

Watch-outs

  • Dry sweeping next to retained finishes pushes fine dust into rooms that were supposed to stay clean.
  • Hidden conditions found during demo, such as suspect insulation, stop cleanup until the owner clears them.
  • Removing barriers before the final HEPA pass spreads dust into occupied space.

Common questions

What does cleanup after selective demolition include?

Removing loose debris and dust from the demo area, cleaning the edge where demolished work meets retained work, and leaving slabs and walls ready for layout and new framing.

How do you keep demo dust out of the parts of the building we kept?

Our crews work inside the contractor's barriers, use HEPA vacuums and damp methods instead of dry sweeping, and clean the transition zone before any barrier is opened.

Can selective demo cleanup happen while tenants are still in the building?

Yes. We schedule noisy or dusty steps for off hours when needed, keep egress paths clear and clean adjacent corridors daily so occupied areas stay presentable.

Who removes the debris after a partial demolition?

The demolition contractor usually removes bulk material. Our crews pick up what remains, separate recyclables and load the GC's containers so the area is ready for the next trade.

Is selective demolition cleanup priced by the square foot?

Usually, based on the demo zone area, debris left behind, protection needs and whether hauling is included. Heavy leftover debris or night work raises the rate.

When should the cleanup crew come in after selective demo?

As soon as demolition in an area is complete and released, before layout and framing start. Waiting lets dust spread and slows every trade that follows.

What else is selective demolition cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "selective demo cleanup" and "partial demolition cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
