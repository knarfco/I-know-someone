Sectional and dock door cleaning

Sectional and dock door cleaning cleans overhead sectional doors, their panels, windows, tracks, seals and dock shelters at loading docks.

Also called dock door cleaning

Why it matters

Dock doors take heavy dirt from trucks and weather. Dirty panels and windows look neglected, and debris in tracks affects operation.

How we approach it

Crews wash panels and windows, vacuum tracks and clean seals and shelters with methods that do not damage weatherstripping.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of doors and size
  • Windows in panels
  • Seals and dock shelters
  • Operation by authorized staff

Watch-outs

  • Do not adjust springs or cables.
  • Harsh cleaners can damage weather seals.

Questions about sectional and dock door cleaning

What is a sectional overhead door?

A garage-style door made of hinged panels that roll up on tracks, common at loading docks.

Are dock shelters and seals cleaned?

Yes, they collect debris and grime and are cleaned with products that do not damage the material.

Should door springs be touched during cleaning?

No. Springs are under high tension and are serviced only by door technicians.

How often are dock doors cleaned?

At turnover and then as part of regular building maintenance.

What else is sectional and dock door cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "dock door cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
