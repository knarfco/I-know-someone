Government hospital and VA medical facility cleaning

Government hospital and VA medical facility cleaning is the post-construction cleaning of veterans' hospitals, military medical centers and government clinics, following infection-control risk assessment requirements and the facility's protocols.

Why it matters

Patients in these facilities are often vulnerable. Construction dust can carry pathogens, so infection control protocols must be followed before any space reopens.

How we do it

Crews follow the project's infection-control risk assessment, clean top down with HEPA vacuums and approved disinfectants and work within containment as the facility requires.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Patient rooms and clinical areas
  • Infection-control risk assessment requirements
  • Containment and negative air zones
  • Disinfectant products approved by the facility
  • Room-by-room completion records

Watch-outs

  • Breaking containment can spread dust into patient areas.
  • Disinfectants must match the facility's approved product list.
  • Clinical areas often require staged handover before inspection.

Common questions

Do you clean VA hospitals after renovation?

Yes. Our crews clean VA and government medical spaces following the project's infection-control risk assessment and the facility's protocols.

What is ICRA in hospital construction cleaning?

ICRA is an infection-control risk assessment that sets containment, cleaning and air protection rules for construction in healthcare facilities.

How do you clean inside hospital construction containment?

Crews HEPA vacuum and damp wipe all surfaces inside containment before it is taken down, then clean again after.

Do you use hospital-approved disinfectants?

Yes. Crews use only the disinfectants on the facility's approved list and leave them wet for the full labeled dwell time.

Can you clean military hospitals on base?

Yes. Access is arranged through the prime contractor and the installation's access process, with the facility's infection-control rules followed throughout.

What else is government hospital and va medical facility cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "VA hospital cleaning", "military hospital cleaning" and "VA clinic construction cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
