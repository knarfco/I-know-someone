Fire and flood reconstruction cleanup

Fire and flood reconstruction cleanup is the construction cleaning done while a damaged building is rebuilt, removing soot residue, sediment, debris and construction dust so new materials are not installed over contamination.

Why it matters

Soot, silt and moisture hidden behind new drywall cause odors, stains and mold later. Rebuild trades need a clean, dry substrate before they close walls.

How we do it

Crews clean behind the restoration contractor, using HEPA vacuums and damp wiping on structure, respecting moisture readings and documenting each area before walls close.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Areas affected and current restoration status
  • Moisture readings and drying sign-off
  • Soot or sediment still present on structure
  • Insurance documentation requirements
  • Rebuild schedule by area

Watch-outs

  • Closing walls before drying is confirmed traps moisture and invites mold growth.
  • Soot cleaning products and methods differ by fire type, so residue needs identification first.
  • Insurance adjusters may need photos before anything is removed or cleaned.

Common questions

Is reconstruction cleanup the same as fire or water restoration?

No. Restoration is the emergency mitigation and drying. Reconstruction cleanup is the construction cleaning during and after the rebuild, so the new building is turned over clean.

Do you clean soot off framing before new drywall goes up?

Our crews HEPA vacuum and wipe structural surfaces where the scope calls for it, after the restoration contractor confirms the remaining residue is ready for cleaning rather than sealing.

When can rebuild cleanup start after a flood?

Once the building is dried and the restoration contractor or consultant signs off on moisture readings. Cleaning wet materials early just spreads sediment and slows drying.

Can you provide photos for our insurance claim during the rebuild?

Yes. Our crews photograph areas before and after cleaning and can organize them by room or floor so the owner or adjuster has a clear record.

What happens to the debris from a fire rebuild?

Our crews separate burned material, salvage and construction waste as the contractor directs, and load the containers so the rebuild trades work in clear space.

Do you do the final clean when the rebuilt building is finished?

Yes. After reconstruction, our crews provide a standard final construction clean, including windows, fixtures, floors and HVAC grilles, before the owner moves back in.

What else is fire and flood reconstruction cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "fire rebuild cleanup", "flood rebuild cleanup" and "reconstruction cleaning after fire or flood". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
