Semiconductor facility final cleaning

Semiconductor facility final cleaning is the protocol-driven clean of a new fab or cleanroom area that removes construction particles from walls, ceilings, floors and tool areas, bringing spaces to a condition ready for the owner's ISO 14644 certification testing.

Why it matters

Fabs are built to tight particle limits. A single missed surface can keep a room from passing certification and delay tool install for weeks.

How we do it

Our crews gown to the owner's protocol and work top to bottom in staged passes with HEPA vacuums, approved wipes and cleanroom-compatible solutions, coordinating each step with the owner's cleanroom and quality teams.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Target cleanroom class per room
  • Owner's gowning and entry protocol
  • Approved wipes and solutions
  • Number of cleaning passes
  • Coordination with certification testing

Watch-outs

  • Only owner-approved wipes, chemicals and tools may enter the cleanroom.
  • Cleaning must follow the room's airflow direction and top-to-bottom sequence.
  • Certification is performed by the owner's testing agency, not by the cleaning crew.

Common questions

What does a semiconductor fab final clean involve?

Multiple staged passes, from rough HEPA vacuuming through gowned wipe-downs of every surface with approved materials, ending when the room is ready for the owner's particle count and certification testing.

Does Beyond Klean certify the cleanroom?

No. Certification to ISO 14644 is performed by the owner's independent testing agency. Our crews clean to the owner's protocol so the room is ready for that testing.

How many cleaning passes does a new fab need?

Commonly three or more: a construction clean, a precision clean and a final wipe after the filters run. The owner's protocol sets the exact number and sequence.

What cleaning materials can be used in a semiconductor cleanroom?

Only what the owner approves, typically low-lint cleanroom wipes, filtered deionized water and specified solvents. Ordinary mops, paper towels and household cleaners are never allowed.

When does cleaning start relative to fan filter startup?

Heavy cleaning happens before filters run so debris does not load them. Final precision passes follow startup, under clean airflow, as the owner's protocol directs.

Can cleaning crews work while tools are being installed?

Yes, with the owner's permission and area controls. We coordinate around tool hookup so newly cleaned zones are protected and re-wiped after installation activity.

What else is semiconductor facility final cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "fab cleaning" and "semiconductor fab final clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
