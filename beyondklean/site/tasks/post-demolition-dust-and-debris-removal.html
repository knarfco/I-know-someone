Post-demolition dust and debris removal

Post-demolition dust and debris removal is the cleaning pass that takes out the fine dust, small debris and residue demolition leaves on floors, walls, ledges and structure, using HEPA capture and wet methods instead of dry sweeping.

Why it matters

Demolition dust often carries silica from concrete, masonry and plaster. Leaving it means it rises with every footstep and settles into new ductwork and finishes.

How we do it

Our crews pick up loose debris, mist and wet sweep slabs, HEPA vacuum ledges and structure, and clean high to low so dust is not dropped on cleaned surfaces.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Area and number of floors
  • Concrete, masonry or plaster demolition involved
  • Height of ledges and exposed structure
  • Water use limits on the slab
  • Debris volume and haul responsibility

Watch-outs

  • Dry sweeping concrete and masonry dust is restricted under OSHA's silica rules.
  • Compressed air or leaf blowers spread demolition dust instead of removing it.
  • Cleaning floors before overhead surfaces means doing the floors twice.

Common questions

How do you get rid of the dust left after demolition?

By capturing it instead of moving it. Our crews use HEPA vacuums, misting and wet sweeping, working from the highest surfaces down to the floor in each area.

Is demolition dust dangerous to breathe?

It can be. Concrete, brick and plaster dust may contain crystalline silica, which is why our crews avoid dry sweeping and use capture methods with proper respiratory protection.

Why does dust keep coming back after demo cleanup?

Usually because overhead surfaces and structure were skipped, or dust was swept dry and settled again. A top-down HEPA pass removes the source instead of redistributing it.

Do you also remove the small debris after demolition is done?

Yes. Our crews pick up chunks, fasteners, wire and wood scraps along with the dust, sort recyclable material and stage everything at the GC's containers.

How long after demolition should the dust cleanup happen?

Right after the demo crew releases the area and before new work starts. Cleaning early keeps dust out of new framing, ductwork and stored materials.

Can you use water to clean demo dust off a concrete slab?

Yes, light misting and wet sweeping are standard, but our crews control water carefully where it could damage retained finishes or reach floors below.

What else is post-demolition dust and debris removal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "demo dust cleanup" and "after-demo cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
