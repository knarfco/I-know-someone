Mosaic and specialty tile detailing

Mosaic and specialty tile detailing cleans small-format mosaic, glass tile and decorative tile, removing grout haze and residue from many joints.

Also called mosaic cleaning

Why it matters

Mosaics have many joints and small tiles that trap haze and residue. Glass tile scratches easily.

How we approach it

Crews clean with soft pads and brushes, using haze removers compatible with glass and specialty tiles.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Tile type
  • Grout type
  • Haze extent
  • Area

Watch-outs

  • Glass mosaic tile scratches easily with abrasive pads.
  • Some decorative tiles and glazes are sensitive to acids.

Questions about mosaic and specialty tile detailing

How are mosaic tiles cleaned after installation?

With soft brushes and haze removers compatible with glass, stone or ceramic mosaics, rinsing carefully.

Can glass tile be scratched during cleaning?

Yes, abrasive pads and gritty water can scratch glass tile.

Why is grout haze harder to remove from mosaics?

Mosaics have far more grout joints per square foot, so there is much more haze to remove.

Is mosaic detailing part of the final clean?

Yes, especially in restrooms, lobbies and feature walls, where mosaics are highly visible and grout haze is common.

What else is mosaic and specialty tile detailing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "mosaic cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
