Mud and track-out removal at entrances

Mud and track-out removal cleans dirt and mud carried from the site onto building entrances, floors, sidewalks and public streets by workers and vehicles.

Also called track-out cleanup

Why it matters

Mud tracked inside damages finishes and floors. Track-out onto public streets can violate storm water and local ordinances.

How we approach it

Crews maintain walk-off mats and grates, clean entrances and sweep or vacuum track-out from streets and sidewalks without washing it into drains.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Entrances and vehicle exits
  • Walk-off mats and grates
  • Street sweeping needs
  • Storm drain protection

Watch-outs

  • Washing mud into storm drains can violate storm water rules.
  • Mats must be cleaned or replaced when full.

Questions about mud and track-out removal at entrances

What is construction track-out?

Mud and dirt carried off a construction site onto public streets or into buildings on tires and boots.

How is track-out controlled on a construction site?

With stabilized gravel exits, rumble plates, walk-off mats and regular sweeping.

Can track-out be washed into the street drain?

Generally no. Track-out should be swept or vacuumed up, not washed into storm drains.

Who is responsible for track-out on public streets?

The GC is responsible and usually assigns sweeping to a cleanup or sweeping contractor.

What else is mud and track-out removal at entrances called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "track-out cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
