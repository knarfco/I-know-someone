Theater and auditorium post-construction cleaning

Theater and auditorium post-construction cleaning cleans seating, stages, aisles and lobbies after construction.

Also called auditorium cleaning

Why it matters

Theaters have many seats, high ceilings and stage equipment.

How we approach it

Crews clean seating row by row and coordinate with stage contractors.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Seating
  • Stage
  • Lobby
  • Ceiling

Watch-outs

  • Stage rigging is not touched.
  • Seats have mechanisms.
