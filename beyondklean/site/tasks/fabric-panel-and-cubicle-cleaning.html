Fabric panel and cubicle cleaning

Fabric panel and cubicle cleaning vacuums and spot-cleans office workstation panels, removing construction dust, labels and marks from fabric surfaces and wiping work surfaces and trim.

Why it matters

Cubicle panels are installed near the end and catch dust. Fabric panels absorb dust that falls into workspaces.

How we do it

Crews vacuum panels with soft brushes, spot-clean with low moisture and wipe hard parts.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of workstations
  • Panel fabric
  • Hard surfaces
  • Labels

Watch-outs

  • Wet-cleaning panels can leave rings.
  • Do not disconnect power or data.

Common questions

How are cubicle panels cleaned after installation?

Vacuumed with soft brush tools, then spot-cleaned with low-moisture methods where needed.

Do new workstations need cleaning?

Yes, they collect dust during installation and from nearby construction.

Can fabric cubicle panels be shampooed?

Usually only spot cleaning is recommended to avoid water rings.

Who cleans new office workstations?

The cleaning crew cleans new workstations after the furniture installer finishes and before employees move in.

What else is fabric panel and cubicle cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "cubicle panel cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
