Densifier and guard application support

Densifier and guard application support prepares and cleans concrete before densifiers or protective guards are applied, and cleans residue afterward.

Common questions

What is a concrete guard?

A topical protective treatment applied to polished concrete to resist stains and improve shine.

Why must the floor be cleaned before densifier is applied?

Dirt and residue block absorption and cause uneven, blotchy results.

Who applies concrete densifier and guard?

Usually the flooring or concrete polishing contractor, with cleaning support before and after.

Can densifier residue be removed?

Usually, if it is addressed promptly before it hardens on the surface.

What else is densifier and guard application support called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "densifier application". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
