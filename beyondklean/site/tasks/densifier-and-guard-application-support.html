Densifier and guard application support

Densifier and guard application support prepares and cleans concrete before densifiers or protective guards are applied, and cleans residue afterward.

Also called densifier application

Why it matters

Densifiers and guards need a clean surface to work, and residue left after application looks blotchy.

How we approach it

Crews clean and dry the floor before application and remove excess product per the manufacturer.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Product type
  • Floor condition
  • Application schedule
  • Manufacturer instructions

Watch-outs

  • Residue left on the surface can look blotchy.
  • Some products need precise application.

Questions about densifier and guard application support

What is a concrete guard?

A topical protective treatment applied to polished concrete to resist stains and improve shine.

Why must the floor be cleaned before densifier is applied?

Dirt and residue block absorption and cause uneven, blotchy results.

Who applies concrete densifier and guard?

Usually the flooring or concrete polishing contractor, with cleaning support before and after.

Can densifier residue be removed?

Usually, if it is addressed promptly before it hardens on the surface.

What else is densifier and guard application support called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "densifier application". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
