Pre-inspection jobsite clean

A pre-inspection jobsite clean prepares the site for a building, safety, fire or owner inspection by clearing debris, opening access and making the inspected areas presentable.

Also called inspection-ready cleanup; OSHA walk prep

Why it matters

Inspectors judge what they see. A cluttered site can lead to safety findings, missed items and a poor impression with owners and lenders.

How we approach it

Crews focus on the areas being inspected, clear egress and access to equipment, and remove debris and hazards before the inspector arrives.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Inspection type and date
  • Areas being inspected
  • Access requirements
  • Last-minute trade activity

Watch-outs

  • Cleaning too early means trades re-soil the area before inspection.
  • Do not hide or block equipment the inspector needs to see.

Questions about pre-inspection jobsite clean

What should be cleaned before an inspection?

Debris, egress paths, access to equipment and the areas being inspected.

When should a pre-inspection clean happen?

As close to the inspection as possible.

Does a messy site fail inspections?

It can lead to safety findings and a poor impression.

Who arranges a pre-inspection clean?

The GC.

What else is pre-inspection jobsite clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "inspection-ready cleanup" and "OSHA walk prep". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
