Touch-up clean before owner walk

A touch-up clean before the owner walk is a fast, focused pass right before the owner or architect inspection, re-cleaning anything that got dirty since the final clean.

Also called touch-up clean; sparkle clean

Why it matters

The owner walk decides how many items land on the punch list. Fresh footprints, fingerprints and dust from last-minute work can turn a clean building into a long list.

How we approach it

Crews walk the inspection route, re-clean glass, fixtures, floors and entries, and check the areas inspectors look at most.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Owner walk date and route
  • Recent trade activity
  • Focus areas
  • Crew availability

Watch-outs

  • Trades working after touch-up will re-soil areas.
  • Touch-up is not a substitute for a full final clean.

Questions about touch-up clean before owner walk

What is a touch-up clean?

A quick re-clean right before an inspection or owner walk.

When should the touch-up happen?

The day before or morning of the owner walk.

What is cleaned in a touch-up?

Glass, fixtures, floors, entries and anything re-soiled.

Is touch-up cleaning priced separately?

Usually, yes.

What else is touch-up clean before owner walk called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "touch-up clean" and "sparkle clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
