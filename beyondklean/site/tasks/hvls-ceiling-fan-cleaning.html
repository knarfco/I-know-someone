HVLS ceiling fan cleaning

HVLS ceiling fan cleaning removes dust from the large-diameter, high-volume low-speed fans used in warehouses, gyms, barns and big-box stores. Blades, hub, motor housing and guy wires are included.

Also called big fan cleaning; industrial ceiling fan cleaning

Why it matters

HVLS fans move huge volumes of air, so dust on their blades is thrown across the space. Dust buildup can also affect balance and make the fan wobble.

How we approach it

With the fan locked out, crews clean blades from a lift, working blade by blade with HEPA vacuums and damp microfiber, without bending blades or loading the mount.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of fans and blade diameter
  • Mounting height and lift access
  • Lockout responsibility
  • Manufacturer cleaning guidance

Watch-outs

  • The fan must be locked out; a fan that turns during cleaning is a serious hazard.
  • Blades are aligned at the factory; do not bend or put weight on them.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about hvls ceiling fan cleaning

How do you clean a 24-foot ceiling fan?

Locked out, from a lift, one blade at a time, using vacuums and damp cloths. Blades are not bent or loaded.

Why do big warehouse fans wobble?

Uneven dust buildup and damage are common causes. Cleaning can help; persistent wobble needs the fan installer.

How often should HVLS fans be cleaned?

Usually during scheduled high dusting, often once or twice a year depending on dust levels.

Do HVLS fans spread dust?

Yes. Dust on blades is distributed across the space, which is why they belong in the overhead scope.

What else is hvls ceiling fan cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "big fan cleaning" and "industrial ceiling fan cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
