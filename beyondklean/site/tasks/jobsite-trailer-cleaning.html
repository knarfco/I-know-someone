Jobsite trailer cleaning

Jobsite trailer cleaning keeps the superintendent's office trailer, meeting areas and plan rooms clean, including floors, desks, restrooms and trash.

Also called site office cleaning; construction trailer cleaning

Why it matters

The trailer is where owners, architects and inspectors meet. It is also where mud and dust from the site end up.

How we approach it

Crews sweep and mop floors, wipe desks and tables, empty trash and clean trailer restrooms on a set schedule.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Trailer size and rooms
  • Frequency
  • Restroom cleaning
  • Supplies

Watch-outs

  • Avoid disturbing drawings, plans and paperwork.
  • Mud at trailer entrances needs mats.

Questions about jobsite trailer cleaning

Why clean the jobsite trailer?

It is the project's office and the place where owners, architects and inspectors meet.

How often should a construction trailer be cleaned?

Usually weekly, more often on busy projects with frequent meetings.

Does trailer cleaning include the restroom?

Yes, if the trailer has a restroom it is cleaned and restocked on the same schedule as the rest of the trailer.

How is mud kept out of the jobsite trailer?

With walk-off mats at the steps and regular floor cleaning.

What else is jobsite trailer cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "site office cleaning" and "construction trailer cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
