Fine dust removal from door tops and frame heads

Fine dust removal from door tops and frame heads cleans the top edges of doors, frame heads, closers and hardware where dust collects out of sight.

Also called door top dusting

Why it matters

Door tops are hidden dust shelves that inspectors check by running a finger along them. Dust there also falls whenever the door swings.

How we approach it

Crews wipe door tops, frame heads and closers with damp microfiber from a step ladder, checking each door as part of a door-by-door checklist.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of doors
  • Hardware such as closers and stops
  • Heights
  • Checklist documentation

Watch-outs

  • Do not adjust closers or hardware.
  • Fire door labels must stay in place.

Questions about fine dust removal from door tops and frame heads

Why clean the tops of doors at the final clean?

Dust collects there and falls whenever the door swings, and inspectors check it.

Is door top cleaning part of the final clean?

Yes, on a thorough final clean every door top and frame head is wiped.

Should door closers be cleaned?

The closer body and arm are wiped, without adjusting them.

How do inspectors check door tops?

Many run a gloved finger along the top edge of the door.

What else is fine dust removal from door tops and frame heads called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "door top dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
