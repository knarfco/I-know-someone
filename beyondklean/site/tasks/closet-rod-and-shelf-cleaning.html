Closet rod and shelf cleaning

Closet rod and shelf cleaning cleans closet shelves, rods, brackets, walls, floors and doors in offices, apartments, hotels and dormitories before occupancy.

Why it matters

Closets are opened by residents and guests and often skipped by crews. Dust and debris there are noticed.

How we do it

Crews vacuum shelves, wipe rods and brackets, clean walls and floors and check closet doors.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

Watch-outs

  • Wire shelving can trap dust in every wire.
  • Sliding door tracks collect debris.

Common questions

Do closets need cleaning before move-in?

Yes, residents and guests open closets right away, and dust and debris there are noticed.

How are wire closet shelves cleaned?

Each section is vacuumed and wiped because every wire collects dust.

Should closet door tracks be cleaned?

Yes, debris in tracks makes sliding and bifold doors stick.

Are closets part of an apartment unit final clean?

Yes. Closets are on every apartment unit checklist, including shelves, rods, doors, tracks and the closet floor.

What else is closet rod and shelf cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "closet cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
