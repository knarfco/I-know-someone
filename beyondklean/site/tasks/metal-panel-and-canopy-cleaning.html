Metal panel and canopy cleaning

Metal panel and canopy cleaning cleans exterior metal panels and canopies, removing film, dust and residue.

Also called canopy cleaning

Why it matters

Metal panels show streaks and residue.

How we approach it

Crews remove film and wash.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Area
  • Film
  • Height
  • Finish

Watch-outs

  • Abrasives scratch.
  • Film bakes on.

Questions about metal panel and canopy cleaning

How are metal canopies cleaned?

Washing with mild cleaners.

Is film removed?

Yes.

Can abrasives be used?

No.

Who cleans canopies?

Cleaning crews.

What else is metal panel and canopy cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "canopy cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
