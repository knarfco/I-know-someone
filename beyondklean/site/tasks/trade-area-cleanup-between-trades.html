Trade-area cleanup between trades

Trade-area cleanup between trades is a focused clean of an area after one trade finishes and before the next starts, so each crew begins on a clean, ready surface.

Also called trade turnover clean; after-trade clean

Why it matters

Finish trades need clean substrates. Flooring installers need clean slabs, painters need dust-free walls and ceiling installers need clear floors for lifts.

How we approach it

Crews clean the released area right after the trade finishes, focusing on what the next trade needs, and document handoffs when disputes are likely.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Trade sequence and handoff points
  • What the next trade needs
  • Documentation
  • Back-charge policies

Watch-outs

  • Without documentation, back-charge disputes are common.
  • Do not clean areas still in use.

Questions about trade-area cleanup between trades

Why clean between trades?

Each trade needs a clean surface to do quality work.

Who pays for cleaning between trades?

Often the GC, sometimes back-charged to the trade that left the mess.

What is a trade handoff?

When one trade releases an area to the next.

Does cleaning between trades speed up a project?

Often yes, by avoiding delays and rework.

What else is trade-area cleanup between trades called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "trade turnover clean" and "after-trade clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
