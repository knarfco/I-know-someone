Renewable energy facility turnover cleaning

Renewable energy facility turnover cleaning is the final clean of control buildings, substations, inverter pads, access roads and support structures at wind and solar plants, removing construction residue so the owner can accept the facility and begin operations.

Why it matters

Turnover is when the owner's operations team takes responsibility. Clean control rooms, pads and roads show the job is finished and make early operating issues easier to spot.

How we do it

Crews final clean control and O&M buildings, sweep pads and walk roads, then work around energized equipment only with the electrical contractor's release and under the site's safety rules.

Watch-outs

  • Substations and energized yards are entered only under the site's safety rules.
  • Water is not used around live electrical equipment.
  • Remote sites need travel, lodging and water planned in advance.

Common questions

What is cleaned at turnover of a wind or solar plant?

Control buildings, O&M offices, restrooms and warehouses get a full final clean. Inverter pads, laydown yards and access roads are cleared of debris and trash.

Do your crews clean inside substations?

Only non-energized areas released by the electrical contractor and owner. Crews follow the site's safety rules and never clean live equipment.

How do you staff remote renewable sites?

With travel crews that bring their own equipment and supplies. We plan lodging, water and waste disposal ahead because many sites have limited services.

What does turnover cleaning include in the control building?

Floors, windows, restrooms, break rooms, cabinets, light fixtures and equipment exteriors in control rooms, with dust removed from cable trays and raised floors where accessible.

When should turnover cleaning be scheduled?

After punch list work is largely complete and before the owner's acceptance walk. A short touch-up visit is often booked for the day before the walk.

Can laydown yards be cleaned as part of turnover?

Yes. Crews pick remaining debris, stack leftover materials for removal and leave the yard ready for restoration or reuse as the owner directs.

What else is renewable energy facility turnover cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "wind and solar facility turnover" and "renewable plant final clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
