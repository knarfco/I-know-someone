Pallet rack cleaning

Pallet rack cleaning removes dust and debris from racking uprights, beams, decking and tops in warehouses and stores.

Also called warehouse rack cleaning; Top of shelving and racking dusting; rack top dusting

Why it matters

Rack tops collect dust that falls onto stored product. Food and pharmaceutical warehouses are audited for it.

How we approach it

Crews clean from lifts top down, vacuuming rack tops and wiping beams, with product covered.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Rack length and height
  • Loaded or empty
  • Product protection
  • Lift access

Watch-outs

  • Never climb racking.
  • Loaded racks need product protection.

Questions about pallet rack cleaning

How is pallet racking cleaned?

From lifts, vacuuming tops and wiping beams.

Can workers climb racking to clean?

No, use lifts.

Why clean rack tops?

Dust falls onto product.

Is rack cleaning part of high dusting?

Often.

What else is pallet rack cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "warehouse rack cleaning", "Top of shelving and racking dusting" and "rack top dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
