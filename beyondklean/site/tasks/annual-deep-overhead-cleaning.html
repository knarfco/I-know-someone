Overhead & Elevated Surfaces (Above Eye Level) · Recurring overhead maintenance

Annual deep overhead cleaning

Annual deep overhead cleaning is a once-a-year, full overhead clean that covers every surface above eye level, often timed with shutdowns, school summers or inventory periods.

Also called annual high dusting

Why it matters

Some buildings only need overhead cleaning once a year, and others need a yearly deep reset in addition to lighter routine cleaning. It prevents years of buildup.

How we approach it

Crews plan the full building ahead of time, staging lifts by zone and capturing dust with HEPA systems, with detailed documentation for the facility's records.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Complete overhead surface inventory
  • Shutdown or low-activity window
  • Equipment and staging plan
  • Documentation and reporting

Watch-outs

  • A year of buildup takes longer to clean than the same area cleaned quarterly.
  • Plan around operational shutdowns well in advance.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about annual deep overhead cleaning

What is the difference between annual and routine high dusting?

Annual cleaning is usually deeper and broader, covering everything overhead once a year.

When do schools schedule annual overhead cleaning?

Usually in summer, when buildings are empty.

Is annual cleaning enough for a food facility?

Usually not. Food facilities typically need more frequent overhead cleaning.

How long does an annual deep overhead clean take?

It depends on building size and buildup. Large facilities may take several shifts.

What else is annual deep overhead cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "annual high dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
