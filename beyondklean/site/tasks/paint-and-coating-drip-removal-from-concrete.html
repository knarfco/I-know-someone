Paint and coating drip removal from concrete

Paint and coating drip removal from concrete removes dried paint, primer, epoxy and coating drips from concrete floors at final clean.

Also called paint drip removal

Why it matters

Drips on exposed concrete look unfinished, and on polished concrete they are especially visible.

How we approach it

Crews scrape carefully and use removers suited to the coating and floor finish, testing first.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Coating types
  • Floor finish
  • Drip extent
  • Remover testing

Watch-outs

  • Solvents can stain or soften sealers.
  • Scraping can mark polished floors.

Questions about paint and coating drip removal from concrete

How do you remove paint from a concrete floor?

Scrape and use removers suited to the paint.

Can epoxy drips be removed?

Often, but cured epoxy is tough.

Who is responsible for paint drips?

Often the painter, depending on the contract.

Do drips matter on concrete that will be covered?

Yes, they cause bumps under flooring.

What else is paint and coating drip removal from concrete called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "paint drip removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
