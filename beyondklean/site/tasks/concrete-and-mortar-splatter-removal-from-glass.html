Concrete and mortar splatter removal from glass

Concrete and mortar splatter removal from glass takes hardened cementitious splatter off windows and storefronts without etching the glass or damaging frames.

Also called concrete on glass removal

Why it matters

Cement splatter bonds tightly to glass. Acidic removers can etch glass and stain aluminum, and dry scraping can scratch.

How we approach it

Crews soften splatter by soaking, remove it mechanically with lubricated blades, and use cement removers only after testing and with frames protected.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Amount and location of splatter
  • Glass and frame types
  • Remover testing
  • Frame protection

Watch-outs

  • Acidic removers can etch glass and frames.
  • Rinse thoroughly so remover does not dry on surfaces.

Questions about concrete and mortar splatter removal from glass

How do you get concrete splatter off new windows?

Soak it to soften it, remove it mechanically with lubricated tools where the glass allows, and use cement removers only after testing.

Can concrete permanently damage window glass?

Fresh splatter left for a long time can etch glass, and aggressive removal can scratch it, so prompt, careful removal is best.

Do concrete removers damage window frames?

Some do, especially acidic products on aluminum, so frames are protected and everything is rinsed thoroughly.

Who removes concrete from new windows?

Construction window cleaning specialists, or the trade responsible under their protection obligations.

What else is concrete and mortar splatter removal from glass called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "concrete on glass removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
