Natural stone countertop cleaning

Natural stone countertop cleaning cleans granite, marble, quartzite, limestone and soapstone counters with stone-safe, pH-neutral products.

Also called granite counter cleaning

Why it matters

Marble and limestone etch easily with acids, and porous stones absorb oils and stain. One wrong cleaner can leave permanent marks.

How we approach it

Crews clean with stone-safe neutral cleaners, test residue removers first and report etching or stains to the GC rather than trying aggressive fixes.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Stone types
  • Sealer status
  • Residues
  • Fabricator contact

Watch-outs

  • Vinegar and acidic cleaners etch marble and limestone.
  • Unsealed stone stains easily.

Questions about natural stone countertop cleaning

Can granite be cleaned with any cleaner?

Use a stone-safe, pH-neutral cleaner.

What is etching on marble?

Dull marks caused by acids dissolving the surface.

Should stone counters be sealed?

Many natural stones should be sealed periodically.

Who repairs stone counter damage?

The fabricator or a stone restoration specialist.

What else is natural stone countertop cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "granite counter cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
