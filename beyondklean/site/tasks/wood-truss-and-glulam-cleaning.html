Wood truss and glulam cleaning

Wood truss and glulam cleaning removes dust and debris from exposed timber trusses, glued-laminated beams and heavy timber framing. It uses dry and low-moisture methods that protect stained, sealed or natural wood finishes.

Why it matters

Exposed wood structure is a design feature in churches, restaurants, schools and lobbies. Dust dulls the finish and settles in checks and joints, and water or strong cleaners can raise grain, stain or strip the coating.

How we do it

Crews HEPA vacuum with soft brush heads along the grain, then use dry microfiber or lightly damp cloths with a wood-safe cleaner only where the finish is sealed. Bare or oiled wood is cleaned dry.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Truss count, beam lengths and exposed faces
  • Wood finish: stained, clear coat, oiled or unfinished
  • Steel connectors and hardware included
  • Height and lift access around pews, seating or fixed furniture

Watch-outs

  • Unfinished or oiled timber absorbs water and cleaners and can stain permanently.
  • Steel knife plates and bolts on timber connections may have different coatings than the wood and need their own method.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our sister brand, AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Common questions

Can exposed wood beams be washed?

Sealed and clear-coated beams can be damp-wiped carefully. Unfinished, oiled or rough-sawn timber should be cleaned dry with HEPA vacuums and soft brushes.

What is glulam?

Glued-laminated timber: structural beams made from layers of lumber bonded together, common in exposed architectural framing.

Why do new wood ceilings look dull?

Fine drywall and sanding dust settles into the wood grain and texture; careful vacuuming along the grain restores the look.

Who cleans wood ceilings in a new church or restaurant?

An overhead cleaning crew with lift access and experience with wood finishes.

What else is wood truss and glulam cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "truss dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
