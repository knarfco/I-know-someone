Operating room post-construction clean

An operating room post-construction clean is a specialized, protocol-driven clean of new or renovated operating rooms and procedure rooms before air balancing verification, environmental testing and clinical use.

Why it matters

Operating rooms have the strictest environmental requirements in a hospital, and contamination can lead to surgical site infections.

How we do it

Only specialized healthcare crews are assigned after review, working with the hospital's infection prevention and facilities teams.

Common questions

Who cleans operating rooms after construction?

Specialized healthcare cleaning crews working under the hospital's infection prevention and environmental services teams.

Are operating rooms tested after construction cleaning?

Often. Hospitals may require air particle counts, pressure verification and sometimes surface testing before clinical use.

Why is operating room cleaning so strict?

Operating rooms have the highest infection control requirements in a hospital, and contamination can lead to surgical site infections.

Can a general construction cleaning crew clean an operating room?

No. It requires crews trained in healthcare construction protocols and approved by the hospital.

What else is operating room post-construction clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "OR construction cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
