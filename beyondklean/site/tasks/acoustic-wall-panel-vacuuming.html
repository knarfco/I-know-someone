Acoustic wall panel vacuuming

Acoustic wall panel vacuuming removes dust from fabric-wrapped, felt and perforated acoustic wall panels with soft brush attachments and low suction, without liquids.

Also called fabric wall panel cleaning

Why it matters

Acoustic panels absorb sound because they are porous, and that same porosity traps dust. Liquids stain fabric and can damage the core.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum panels with soft brush heads, top to bottom, and refer stains to the manufacturer's spot-cleaning method.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Panel count and size
  • Fabric type
  • Stains and marks
  • Manufacturer guidance

Watch-outs

  • Water stains fabric panels.
  • High suction can pull fabric loose.

Questions about acoustic wall panel vacuuming

How do you clean fabric acoustic panels?

With a soft brush vacuum attachment. Spots are treated only with approved methods.

Can acoustic panels be wiped?

Fabric panels should not be wiped with liquids.

Why do acoustic panels collect dust?

Their porous surfaces absorb sound and trap dust.

Who cleans acoustic panels?

The cleaning contractor at final clean.

What else is acoustic wall panel vacuuming called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "fabric wall panel cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
