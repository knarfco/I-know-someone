Silt fence and erosion control debris cleanup

Silt fence and erosion control debris cleanup removes silt fence and debris when the site is stabilized.

Also called erosion control cleanup

Why it matters

Erosion controls must be removed at closeout.

How we approach it

Crews remove fence and sediment when approved.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Fence length
  • Approval
  • Sediment
  • Disposal

Watch-outs

  • Sediment.

Questions about silt fence and erosion control debris cleanup

When is silt fence removed?

When the site is stabilized.

Who approves?

Permit authority.

Is sediment removed?

Yes.

Who removes it?

Crews.

What else is silt fence and erosion control debris cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "erosion control cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
