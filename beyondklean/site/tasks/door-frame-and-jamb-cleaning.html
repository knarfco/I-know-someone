Door frame and jamb cleaning

Door frame and jamb cleaning removes dust, overspray, caulk smears and fingerprints from hollow metal and wood frames, jambs, stops and hinges.

Also called frame wipe

Why it matters

Frames are painted on site and often get overspray, caulk and fingerprints. Dust in hinge areas and stops is easy to miss.

How we approach it

Crews wipe frames and stops, clean hinges and strike plates and remove light overspray with plastic blades and mild removers.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of frames
  • Frame finish
  • Overspray and caulk
  • Hinges and strikes

Watch-outs

  • Solvents can soften fresh paint.
  • Fire labels on frames must remain.

Questions about door frame and jamb cleaning

What is a door jamb?

The vertical sides of a door frame that the door closes against.

Why do door frames get paint overspray?

Frames are painted near walls and other finishes, and overspray drifts onto adjacent surfaces.

Should door hinges be cleaned at final clean?

Yes, hinges collect dust and paint and are wiped carefully.

Why are fire labels on door frames important?

They show the frame's fire rating, and inspectors check that they are present and legible.

What else is door frame and jamb cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "frame wipe". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
