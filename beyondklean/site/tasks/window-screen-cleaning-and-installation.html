Window screen cleaning and installation

Window screen cleaning and installation cleans insect screens removed for construction or window cleaning, checks them for damage and reinstalls each one in the window it came from.

Also called screen cleaning

Why it matters

Screens collect dust and are often removed for construction and window cleaning. Missing or damaged screens are a punch item.

How we approach it

Crews label screens by window, clean them with mild solution and soft brushes, let them dry and reinstall them.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of screens
  • Labeling and storage
  • Damage check
  • Reinstallation

Watch-outs

  • Screens bend easily if stacked or carried carelessly.
  • Unlabeled screens may not fit other windows.

Questions about window screen cleaning and installation

How are window screens cleaned?

With mild soap, a soft brush and a gentle rinse, then left to dry before reinstalling.

Why should window screens be labeled when removed?

Screens are often slightly different sizes, so labeling ensures each goes back to its own window.

Who replaces damaged window screens?

Usually the window contractor or supplier; cleaners report damage.

Is screen installation part of the final clean?

Often, when screens were removed during construction or window cleaning.

What else is window screen cleaning and installation called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "screen cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
