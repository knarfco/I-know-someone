Marker board and whiteboard wall cleaning

Marker board and whiteboard wall cleaning removes film, dust, marks and adhesive from whiteboards, glass boards and writable wall coatings before first use.

Why it matters

New boards often have protective film, dust or marks from installers. Wrong cleaners can damage writable surfaces and cause ghosting.

How we do it

Crews remove film carefully, clean with the board manufacturer's recommended cleaner and wipe trays and frames.

Watch-outs

  • Abrasive cleaners damage writable surfaces.
  • Some coatings need cure time before use.

Common questions

How should a new whiteboard be cleaned before first use?

Remove the protective film, then clean with the board manufacturer's recommended cleaner and a soft cloth.

Can glass cleaner be used on whiteboards?

Only if the manufacturer allows it; some cleaners leave residue that causes ghosting.

What is ghosting on a whiteboard?

Faint marks that remain after erasing, often caused by the wrong cleaners or markers.

Do writable wall coatings need special care?

Yes, they need cure time before use and the coating manufacturer's cleaning instructions.

What else is marker board and whiteboard wall cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "dry erase wall cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
