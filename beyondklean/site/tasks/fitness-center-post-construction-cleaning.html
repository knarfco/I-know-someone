Fitness center post-construction cleaning

Fitness center post-construction cleaning cleans gyms and fitness rooms after construction, including rubber and wood floors, mirrors, equipment exteriors, locker rooms, showers and group exercise studios.

Also called gym construction cleaning

Why it matters

Fitness centers are full of mirrors, equipment and textured rubber floors that show and trap construction dust. Members and residents use them immediately after opening.

How we approach it

Crews clean mirrors and equipment exteriors with approved products, vacuum and scrub rubber floors with neutral cleaners and detail locker rooms and showers.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Equipment
  • Floors
  • Mirrors
  • Locker rooms

Watch-outs

  • Do not move heavy equipment.
  • Rubber floors need approved cleaners.

Questions about fitness center post-construction cleaning

Are gym mirrors cleaned after construction?

Yes, mirror walls are cleaned carefully so no liquid runs behind the edges.

Is fitness equipment cleaned at turnover?

Equipment exteriors are cleaned with products the equipment manufacturer allows.

How are rubber gym floors cleaned?

Vacuumed and scrubbed with neutral cleaners approved by the flooring manufacturer, because oily products make rubber slippery.

Are locker rooms and showers included?

Yes. Locker rooms and showers are detailed like restrooms, including tile, fixtures, benches and lockers.

What else is fitness center post-construction cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "gym construction cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
