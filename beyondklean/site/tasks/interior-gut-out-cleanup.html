Interior gut-out cleanup

Interior gut-out cleanup clears a building shell after interior walls, ceilings, finishes and systems are stripped to the structure, removing debris, dust, fasteners and adhesive residue so the space is ready for rebuild.

Why it matters

A gut-out exposes the slab, deck and structure that the new build depends on. Leftover nails, mastic and debris cause layout errors, flooring failures and safety hazards.

How we do it

Crews sweep and magnet the floors, scrape loose residue from slabs, HEPA vacuum exposed structure and leave the shell broom clean and photographed for the GC.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Floor area and number of levels
  • Ceiling height and exposed deck
  • Slab scraping or adhesive removal expectations
  • Debris container and recycling plan
  • Photo documentation of turnover

Watch-outs

  • Old flooring adhesives can contain asbestos and must be tested before anyone scrapes them.
  • Open floor penetrations and shafts after a gut-out are fall hazards that need covers.
  • Nails and screws left on the slab puncture tires on lifts and carts.

Common questions

What is the difference between a gut-out and selective demolition?

A gut-out removes nearly everything inside down to the structure. Selective demo removes only certain parts while protecting what stays, so cleanup protection needs differ a lot.

Do you scrape old glue and mastic off the slab after a gut job?

Our crews scrape loose and cured residue when the scope calls for it, but only after the owner confirms through testing that the mastic is not asbestos-containing.

How clean should a gutted space be before the rebuild starts?

Broom clean at minimum, with fasteners removed, slabs scraped of loose material and exposed structure vacuumed, so surveyors can lay out and trades can start on a clean shell.

Can you clean a gutted building floor by floor?

Yes. Most multistory gut-outs are released level by level, and our crews follow that sequence so the rebuild can start on lower floors while demo continues above.

Why vacuum the exposed deck and beams after a gut-out?

Dust on the structure falls onto new finishes for months. Cleaning it while the space is open is far cheaper than cleaning around new ceilings and ductwork later.

Do we get photos of the cleaned shell?

Yes, photo documentation of each area at turnover is part of our standard closeout for gut-out cleanup, which helps the GC show the space was handed over clean.

What else is interior gut-out cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "gut job cleanup", "interior strip-out cleanup" and "tear-out cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
