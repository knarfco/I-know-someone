Hard Floors · Resilient floors (VCT, LVT, sheet vinyl, rubber)

Scuff and heel mark removal

Scuff and heel mark removal takes black marks and scuffs off resilient and hard floors without damaging the floor finish.

Also called scuff removal

Why it matters

Scuffs appear quickly during final construction activity and are noticed during walks.

How we approach it

Crews use soft pads, tennis-ball tools or appropriate cleaners and buff lightly.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Floor type
  • Mark extent
  • Finish condition
  • Timing

Watch-outs

  • Aggressive pads damage finish.
  • Some marks need finish repair.

Questions about scuff and heel mark removal

How are scuff marks removed?

With soft pads or appropriate cleaners.

Do scuffs damage floor finish?

They mark it; heavy scuffs may need recoating.

Why are floors scuffed after construction?

Equipment, carts and shoes.

Is scuff removal part of final clean?

Yes.

What else is scuff and heel mark removal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "scuff removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
