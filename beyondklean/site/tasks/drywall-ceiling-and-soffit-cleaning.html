Drywall ceiling and soffit cleaning

Drywall ceiling and soffit cleaning removes dust, cobwebs and marks from painted gypsum ceilings, bulkheads and soffits.

Also called gyp ceiling cleaning

Why it matters

Hard-lid ceilings show dust lines along edges and fingerprints near access panels. Dust on ceilings also falls onto clean floors.

How we approach it

Crews dust ceilings with microfiber pole mops before floors are cleaned and spot-clean marks with gentle methods.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Ceiling area and heights
  • Paint sheen
  • Access panels and fixtures
  • Sequence before floor cleaning

Watch-outs

  • Flat ceiling paint burnishes easily.
  • Heavy marks may need touch-up painting.

Questions about drywall ceiling and soffit cleaning

Do drywall ceilings need cleaning after construction?

Yes, they collect dust and fingerprints.

When should ceilings be cleaned?

Before walls and floors, because dust falls.

Can ceiling marks be cleaned or should they be painted?

Light marks can be cleaned; heavy marks need touch-up.

How are high drywall ceilings cleaned?

With pole tools or lifts.

What else is drywall ceiling and soffit cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "gyp ceiling cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
