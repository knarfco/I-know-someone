Warehouse post-construction cleaning

Warehouse post-construction cleaning prepares new distribution centers and warehouses for tenants: overhead dusting, slab sweeping and scrubbing, dock cleaning, office and restroom detailing and exterior truck court sweeping.

Why it matters

Warehouse tenants move in fast, and racking installers and operations need a clean slab. Overhead dust and slab debris become product contamination and safety issues later.

How we do it

Crews clean top down: overhead first from lifts, then sweep and ride-on scrub the slab in lanes, then detail docks, offices and restrooms.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Building square footage and clear height
  • Racking installation schedule
  • Office and restroom areas
  • Dock count and truck court

Watch-outs

  • Clean overhead before racking goes in.
  • Wet slabs are slippery for lifts.

Common questions

What is included in warehouse post-construction cleaning?

Overhead dusting, slab sweeping and scrubbing, dock and leveler cleaning, office and restroom detailing, and exterior truck court sweeping.

How long does it take to clean a new warehouse?

It depends on size and clear height; large distribution centers can take several days with multiple ride-on machines and lifts.

Should a warehouse be cleaned before racking is installed?

Yes. An empty building is far faster and easier to clean, especially overhead.

Who sweeps warehouse truck courts?

The cleaning contractor or a power sweeping subcontractor with truck-mounted sweepers.

What else is warehouse post-construction cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "distribution center cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
