Exposed structural steel beam cleaning

Exposed structural steel beam cleaning removes dust, mill scale flakes, overspray and debris from steel beams and girders that remain visible in the finished space. It covers top flanges, webs and bottom flanges and is usually done at final clean from lifts.

Also called beam cleaning; I-beam dusting

Why it matters

The top flange of every beam is a long dust shelf. In open-ceiling stores, restaurants and lobbies that dust falls in visible streaks onto floors and tables as soon as the HVAC starts moving air.

How we approach it

Top flanges are vacuumed first with HEPA brush heads, then webs and bottom flanges are wiped with damp microfiber. Primed or painted steel gets plain water or a pH-neutral solution, never solvents that could soften fresh coatings.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of beam and typical beam depth
  • Height to the top flange and lift access along each line
  • Coating type: primer, finish paint or intumescent fireproofing
  • Whether connection plates and bracing are included

Watch-outs

  • Intumescent fireproofing paint on beams must not be scrubbed or wetted heavily; it is a fire-rated coating and damage has to be repaired by the coating applicator.
  • Beam tops are rarely visible from below, so ask for photo verification at each line.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about exposed structural steel beam cleaning

Why is there so much dust on top of new steel beams?

The top flange is flat and catches everything that rises during construction: drywall sanding dust, concrete cutting dust and spray-applied finishes. Nothing else in the building collects as much per foot.

Can beams be wiped with a wet rag?

The webs and bottom flanges can be damp-wiped once the heavy dust is vacuumed off. Starting with a wet rag on a dusty flange turns the dust into mud streaks.

Is beam cleaning included in a standard final clean?

Often it is not written in. If the beams are exposed in the finished design, list them as a separate overhead line item so the cleaning bid includes lifts and labor.

How do you prove beam tops were cleaned?

Ask for before-and-after photos taken from the lift at representative points along each beam line. It is the simplest way to verify a surface no one can see from the floor.

What else is exposed structural steel beam cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "beam cleaning" and "I-beam dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
