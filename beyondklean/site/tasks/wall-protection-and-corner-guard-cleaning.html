Wall protection and corner guard cleaning

Wall protection and corner guard cleaning removes film, adhesive, dust and marks from crash rails, bumper guards, corner guards and wall protection panels in healthcare, schools and back-of-house areas.

Why it matters

Wall protection ships with protective film and adhesive residue, and its textured surfaces hold dust. It is checked in healthcare inspections.

How we do it

Crews remove protective film, clean adhesive with removers safe for vinyl and PVC, and wipe rails and guards with damp microfiber.

Common questions

What is wall protection in commercial buildings?

Crash rails, bumper guards, corner guards and protective panels that keep carts and equipment from damaging walls.

How are crash rails and corner guards cleaned?

Protective film is removed, adhesive is cleaned with products safe for vinyl and PVC, and surfaces are wiped with damp microfiber.

Are corner guards cleaned at final clean?

Yes, along with all wall protection, because they collect film, adhesive and scuffs.

Why is wall protection common in hospitals?

Beds, carts and equipment constantly hit walls in healthcare buildings.

What else is wall protection and corner guard cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "crash rail cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
