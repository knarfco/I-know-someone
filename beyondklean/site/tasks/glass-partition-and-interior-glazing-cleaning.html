Glass partition and interior glazing cleaning

Glass partition and interior glazing cleaning cleans interior glass walls, office fronts, glass doors and back-painted glass, including frames and film graphics.

Also called glass partition cleaning; glass wall and back-painted glass cleaning; glass wall cleaning

Why it matters

Glass partitions show every fingerprint and streak, and many have film graphics or manifestations that are easily damaged.

How we approach it

Crews clean frames, wash glass with glass cleaners safe for film graphics and avoid scraping across graphics or back-painted surfaces.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Partition areas
  • Film graphics and manifestations
  • Frame types
  • Back-painted glass

Watch-outs

  • Blades can cut or lift film graphics.
  • Back-painted glass coatings can be damaged from the back.

Questions about glass partition and interior glazing cleaning

How are glass office walls cleaned after construction?

With glass cleaner and squeegees, cleaning frames first and working around any graphics or film.

What are glass manifestations?

Markings or film on glass walls and doors that make them visible so people do not walk into them.

Can you scrape near glass graphics?

No. Blades can cut or lift film graphics and manifestations.

What is back-painted glass?

Glass with paint applied to the back face, used as a wall or marker board surface. It is cleaned only on the front.

What else is glass partition and interior glazing cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "glass partition cleaning", "glass wall and back-painted glass cleaning" and "glass wall cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
