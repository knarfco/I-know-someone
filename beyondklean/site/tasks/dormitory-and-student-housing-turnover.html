Dormitory and student housing turnover

Dormitory and student housing turnover cleans residence hall rooms, bathrooms, lounges and corridors after construction or summer renovations before students move in.

Also called dorm cleaning

Why it matters

Move-in day is fixed and students arrive by the hundreds. Rooms must be clean and consistent.

How we approach it

Crews clean room by room with checklists and focus on bathrooms, closets and windows.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Room count
  • Bathrooms
  • Move-in date
  • Furniture

Watch-outs

  • Schedules are compressed.
  • Shared bathrooms need extra attention.

Questions about dormitory and student housing turnover

When are dorms cleaned?

Before move-in, often during summer.

Are dorm bathrooms cleaned?

Yes, they are a focus.

How long does dorm turnover take?

It depends on the number of rooms.

Who sets the dorm cleaning standard?

The housing department.

What else is dormitory and student housing turnover called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "dorm cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
