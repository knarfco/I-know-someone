Fire pump and riser room cleaning

Fire pump and riser room cleaning removes debris and dust from fire pump rooms and sprinkler riser rooms, cleaning floors, walls and the outside of pipes and valves without touching any fire protection equipment settings.

Common questions

What is a sprinkler riser room?

The room where the fire sprinkler system's main control valves, alarms and gauges are located, usually on the ground floor with exterior access.

Do fire inspectors check riser and pump rooms?

Yes. They check access, clearance, labeling, signage and condition, and stored materials are a common violation.

Can cleaning crews touch sprinkler valves?

No. Valves, gauges and controls are operated only by fire protection contractors or authorized staff.

Why must riser rooms be kept clear?

Firefighters need immediate, unobstructed access to valves during an emergency.

What else is fire pump and riser room cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "riser room cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
