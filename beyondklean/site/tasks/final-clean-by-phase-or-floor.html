Final clean by phase or floor

A final clean by phase or floor completes the final clean in one area at a time as each is finished, so it can be inspected, handed over or protected while other areas continue.

Also called phased final clean

Why it matters

Large projects and phased occupancies turn over in parts. Each part needs to be final-cleaned and kept clean until handover.

How we approach it

Crews clean each released area completely, document it and coordinate protection or access control so it stays clean.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Phase plan
  • Handover dates
  • Protection
  • Documentation

Watch-outs

  • Trades working in adjacent phases will re-soil finished areas unless access is controlled.
  • Each extra mobilization adds cost, so agree on phase pricing before work starts.

Questions about final clean by phase or floor

Why do a final clean in phases instead of all at once?

Large buildings and phased occupancies finish one area at a time. Cleaning each area as it is released lets it be inspected, handed over or occupied without waiting for the whole building.

How is a finished phase kept clean while work continues next door?

With floor protection, closed doors or barriers, walk-off mats and controlled access, plus a scheduled touch-up right before handover.

Does a phased final clean cost more?

Often somewhat more, because crews mobilize several times and some touch-up is repeated. It is usually offset by earlier occupancy and fewer delays.

Who decides the phases for final cleaning?

The GC and owner set them based on the occupancy plan, inspections and the construction schedule.

What else is final clean by phase or floor called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "phased final clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
