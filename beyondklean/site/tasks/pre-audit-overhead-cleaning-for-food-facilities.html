Pre-audit overhead cleaning for food facilities

Pre-audit overhead cleaning prepares food plants, commissaries, grocery back rooms and food warehouses for third-party food safety audits by removing dust, debris and residue from overhead structures and equipment.

Also called audit prep high dusting; SQF prep

Why it matters

Food safety auditors look up. Overhead dust, flaking paint and debris above food zones are common findings that can cost points or trigger corrective actions.

How we approach it

Crews clean from the top down in food-safe sequence, using food-safe products, covering exposed product and equipment, and documenting areas cleaned for the audit file.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Audit date and scheme requirements
  • Food zones and production schedules
  • Approved chemicals list
  • Documentation format for the audit file

Watch-outs

  • Only food-safe products approved by the facility may be used.
  • Production must be stopped or product covered in areas being cleaned.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about pre-audit overhead cleaning for food facilities

What overhead areas do food safety auditors check?

Ceilings, beams, pipes, ducts, lights and the tops of equipment and walk-ins above food areas.

How far ahead of an audit should overhead cleaning happen?

Close enough that dust has not returned, but with time to fix anything found. Many facilities schedule it one to two weeks before.

Can overhead cleaning be done during production?

Usually not above exposed product. It is scheduled during sanitation shifts or shutdowns.

Who provides overhead cleaning documentation for audits?

The cleaning contractor provides a record of areas cleaned, dates and products used.

What else is pre-audit overhead cleaning for food facilities called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "audit prep high dusting" and "SQF prep". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
