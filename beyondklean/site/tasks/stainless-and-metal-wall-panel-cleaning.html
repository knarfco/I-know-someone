Stainless and metal wall panel cleaning

Stainless and metal wall panel cleaning removes film, fingerprints, adhesive and streaks from stainless steel, aluminum and metal composite panels on interior walls, elevator lobbies and kitchens.

Why it matters

Metal panels show every fingerprint and streak, and the wrong cleaner or wiping direction leaves visible marks.

How we do it

Crews remove protective film, clean adhesive with metal-safe removers, wash with neutral cleaner and finish with the grain.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Panel areas and metal type
  • Protective film
  • Finish direction
  • Adhesive residue

Watch-outs

  • Chloride cleaners and steel wool can cause stainless to rust.
  • Wiping against the grain leaves streaks.

Common questions

How do you clean stainless steel wall panels without streaks?

Clean with a neutral cleaner, rinse, dry and wipe in the direction of the grain.

Why should steel wool never be used on stainless?

It leaves carbon steel particles that rust and scratches the surface.

When should protective film be removed from metal wall panels?

At the final clean, and before heat and sunlight bake the adhesive on.

Are metal composite panels cleaned the same way?

Similar gentle methods apply, following the panel manufacturer's guidance.

What else is stainless and metal wall panel cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "metal panel cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
