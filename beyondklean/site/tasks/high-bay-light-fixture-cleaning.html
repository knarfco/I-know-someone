High-bay light fixture cleaning

High-bay light fixture cleaning removes dust and debris from the housings, lenses and reflectors of high-mounted LED or HID fixtures in warehouses, gyms, stores and manufacturing spaces.

Also called high bay cleaning

Why it matters

Dust on high-bay lenses reduces light output, and dust on housing tops falls when fixtures warm up and cool down. Lighting is also checked during commissioning and owner walks.

How we approach it

Fixtures are de-energized as required, then HEPA vacuumed on top and wiped with dry or barely damp microfiber on lenses. Plastic lenses get only manufacturer-approved cleaners.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Fixture count, type and mounting height
  • Lens material: glass, acrylic or polycarbonate
  • Lift access around racking or equipment
  • Power-off coordination

Watch-outs

  • Some plastic lenses are damaged by ammonia and solvent cleaners.
  • Do not disturb fixture aiming or sensors.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about high-bay light fixture cleaning

Does cleaning light fixtures make the room brighter?

Yes. Dust on lenses blocks light, so cleaning restores some output, especially in dusty spaces.

Can LED high bays be cleaned while on?

Exterior wiping is sometimes allowed, but de-energizing is safer and is required by many owners. Follow the electrical contractor's direction.

What cleaner is safe for plastic light lenses?

Mild soap and water or the manufacturer's recommended cleaner. Avoid ammonia and solvents.

Are occupancy sensors on fixtures cleaned?

Gently wiped only. Sensors should not be moved or re-aimed.

What else is high-bay light fixture cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "high bay cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
