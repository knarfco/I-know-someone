High-bay light fixture cleaning

High-bay light fixture cleaning removes dust and debris from the housings, lenses and reflectors of high-mounted LED or HID fixtures in warehouses, gyms, stores and manufacturing spaces.

Why it matters

Dust on high-bay lenses reduces light output, and dust on housing tops falls when fixtures warm up and cool down. Lighting is also checked during commissioning and owner walks.

How we do it

Fixtures are de-energized as required, then HEPA vacuumed on top and wiped with dry or barely damp microfiber on lenses. Plastic lenses get only manufacturer-approved cleaners.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Fixture count, type and mounting height
  • Lens material: glass, acrylic or polycarbonate
  • Lift access around racking or equipment
  • Power-off coordination

Watch-outs

  • Some plastic lenses are damaged by ammonia and solvent cleaners.
  • Do not disturb fixture aiming or sensors.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our sister brand, AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Common questions

Does cleaning light fixtures make a warehouse brighter?

Yes. Dust on lenses and reflectors blocks light, so cleaning restores output, especially in dusty spaces.

Can LED high-bay fixtures be cleaned while they are on?

De-energizing is safer and many owners require it. Follow the electrical contractor's direction.

What cleaner is safe for plastic light lenses?

Mild soap and water or the manufacturer's recommended cleaner; ammonia and solvents can cloud plastic.

Are occupancy sensors on fixtures cleaned?

They are wiped gently but never moved or re-aimed.

What else is high-bay light fixture cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "high bay cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
