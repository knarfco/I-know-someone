Post-renovation reset clean

A post-renovation reset clean returns an occupied building to normal after a renovation, cleaning the renovated area and the surrounding spaces where dust spread.

Why it matters

Renovation dust travels beyond barriers through doors, HVAC and foot traffic. Occupants judge the project by how quickly their space feels clean again.

How we do it

Crews clean the renovated area top down, then clean adjacent rooms, corridors and HVAC grilles, coordinating with occupants' schedules.

Common questions

What is a post-renovation reset clean?

A clean that returns the renovated area and surrounding spaces to normal after work ends.

Why clean areas outside the renovation zone?

Renovation dust travels through doors, HVAC and foot traffic, so nearby rooms and corridors get dusty even with barriers in place.

Is the HVAC included in a reset clean?

HVAC grilles and diffusers are cleaned, and filters may need changing if the system ran during the work.

When is the reset clean done?

Right after the renovation ends, often after hours so occupants come back to a clean space.

What else is post-renovation reset clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "renovation cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
