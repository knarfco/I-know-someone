Hard Floors · Resilient floors (VCT, LVT, sheet vinyl, rubber)

VCT strip and refinish

VCT strip and refinish removes old floor finish with a chemical stripper and applies new coats of finish, restoring worn or damaged VCT floors.

Also called VCT stripping and waxing

Why it matters

Floor finish wears and yellows over time, and renovation work scuffs it. Stripping resets the floor.

How we approach it

Crews apply stripper, scrub and recover it, rinse thoroughly, then apply new coats of finish.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Area
  • Number of existing coats
  • Stripper and finish products
  • Downtime

Watch-outs

  • Strippers are strong chemicals; ventilate and use PPE.
  • Residual stripper causes finish failure.

Questions about vct strip and refinish

What does stripping a floor mean?

Removing old floor finish with chemical stripper.

How often do VCT floors need stripping?

Depending on traffic and maintenance, often once a year or less.

Can stripping damage VCT?

Overly aggressive stripping can.

How long does a strip and refinish take?

Often overnight or a day, depending on area.

What else is vct strip and refinish called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "VCT stripping and waxing". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
