Wall fixture and slatwall cleaning

Wall fixture and slatwall cleaning cleans slatwall panels, grid walls, wall standards and display fixtures mounted to walls in retail stores.

Also called slatwall cleaning

Why it matters

Slatwall grooves trap dust, and wall fixtures are at eye level where shoppers look.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum grooves with crevice tools and wipe faces, top to bottom.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Wall fixture area
  • Slatwall inserts
  • Accessories
  • Stocking schedule

Watch-outs

  • Inserts can scratch.
  • Accessories should not be removed.

Questions about wall fixture and slatwall cleaning

How is slatwall cleaned?

Vacuum the grooves and wipe the faces.

Why does slatwall get dusty?

Its grooves trap dust.

Are wall fixtures cleaned before stocking?

Yes.

What is grid wall?

A metal grid display used in stores.

What else is wall fixture and slatwall cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "slatwall cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
