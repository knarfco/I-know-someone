Classroom turnover cleaning

Classroom turnover cleaning cleans individual classrooms after construction or renovation, including boards, casework, windows, floors, desks and technology mounts, so teachers can set up.

Also called classroom cleaning

Why it matters

Teachers often have only a few days to set up rooms. A dusty classroom delays setup and starts the year poorly.

How we approach it

Crews follow a classroom checklist, cleaning top down and detailing boards, casework and sinks before floors.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Classroom count
  • Furniture status
  • Technology installation
  • Teacher move-in date

Watch-outs

  • Do not move technology equipment without permission.
  • Whiteboards need manufacturer-approved cleaners.

Questions about classroom turnover cleaning

What does classroom turnover cleaning include?

Boards, casework, windows, floors, furniture and fixtures, cleaned top down.

How long does it take to clean a classroom after construction?

Usually a few crew-hours per room, depending on dust and furniture.

Are interactive boards cleaned?

Gently, with approved cleaners and never sprayed directly.

When can teachers move in?

After turnover cleaning is complete and signed off.

What else is classroom turnover cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "classroom cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
