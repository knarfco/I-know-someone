Classroom turnover cleaning

Classroom turnover cleaning cleans individual classrooms after construction or summer renovation, including boards, casework, sinks, windows, floors, desks and technology mounts, so teachers can set up their rooms.

Why it matters

Teachers often have only a few days to set up rooms. A dusty classroom delays setup and starts the year poorly.

How we do it

Crews follow a classroom checklist, cleaning top down and detailing boards, casework and sinks before floors.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Classroom count
  • Furniture status
  • Technology installation
  • Teacher move-in date

Watch-outs

  • Interactive boards and projectors must not be sprayed or moved.
  • Whiteboards need manufacturer-approved cleaners to avoid ghosting.

Common questions

What does classroom turnover cleaning include?

Boards, casework, sinks, windows, light fixtures, furniture and floors, cleaned top down to a classroom checklist.

How long does it take to clean a classroom after construction?

Usually a few crew-hours per room, depending on how much construction dust and furniture are present.

Are interactive classroom boards cleaned?

Yes, gently with approved cleaners applied to a cloth, never sprayed onto the board or projector.

When can teachers set up their classrooms?

After the turnover clean is done and the room has been signed off by the school or GC.

What else is classroom turnover cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "classroom cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
