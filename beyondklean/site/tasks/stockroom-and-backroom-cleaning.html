Stockroom and backroom cleaning

Stockroom and backroom cleaning cleans storage rooms, receiving areas, break rooms, offices and restrooms behind the sales floor, which are often used for construction storage and left dirty at opening.

Why it matters

Back rooms are used for storage during construction and are often the dirtiest areas at opening.

How we do it

Crews clear construction debris and leftover materials with the GC's approval, clean shelving top down, scrub floors, and detail break rooms and staff restrooms.

Common questions

Are stockrooms cleaned before a store opens?

Yes, they are cleaned so merchandise and supplies can be stored on clean shelving and floors.

Why are back rooms so dirty at the end of a store build?

Trades use them to store tools and materials and they are often the last areas finished.

Are staff break rooms included in the opening clean?

Yes, break rooms, offices and staff restrooms are part of the back-of-house clean.

Is stockroom shelving cleaned?

Yes, shelving is cleaned top down before merchandise and supplies are stored.

What else is stockroom and backroom cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "stockroom cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
