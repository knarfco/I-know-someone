Stone polishing and honing

Stone polishing and honing uses abrasives and compounds to restore or change the finish of marble, terrazzo and other stone floors. It is restoration specialty work.

Also called marble polishing; stone restoration

Why it matters

Etched or scratched stone can only be fixed by honing and polishing, which requires skill and specialized equipment.

How we approach it

Requests are reviewed and assigned only to stone restoration specialists with the right diamond tooling and slurry control.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Stone type and condition
  • Desired finish
  • Area
  • Downtime

Watch-outs

  • Improper honing can leave swirl marks or uneven finishes.
  • Slurry must be contained and kept out of drains and adjacent finishes.

Questions about stone polishing and honing

What is stone honing?

Grinding the stone surface with fine abrasives to remove etching and scratches and create a smooth, matte finish.

Can etched marble be repaired?

Yes. Honing removes the etched layer and polishing restores the shine.

Is stone polishing a cleaning service?

No, it is restoration work done by stone specialists.

Who polishes natural stone floors?

Stone restoration contractors with diamond tooling and slurry control.

What else is stone polishing and honing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "marble polishing" and "stone restoration". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
