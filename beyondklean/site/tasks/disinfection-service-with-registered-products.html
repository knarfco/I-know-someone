Disinfection service with registered products

Disinfection service with registered products applies EPA-registered disinfectants to cleaned surfaces according to the product label, including dilution, contact time and surface compatibility, with documentation of the product used.

Also called disinfecting; EPA-registered disinfection

Why it matters

Disinfection only works, and is only legal to claim, when a registered product is used exactly as its label directs. Surfaces must be cleaned first, because soil blocks disinfectants.

How we approach it

Requests are routed only to trained providers who clean first, apply the registered product per label, observe contact time and document the product and areas treated.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Product and EPA registration number
  • Label directions and contact time
  • Surfaces and compatibility
  • Documentation

Watch-outs

  • Disinfection claims must match what the label supports.
  • Some disinfectants damage stone, metal or plastics.

Questions about disinfection service with registered products

What is the difference between cleaning and disinfecting?

Cleaning removes soil and residue; disinfecting kills specified microorganisms with a registered product used per label. Cleaning must come first.

Why does the disinfectant label matter?

The label sets the dilution, contact time and surfaces. Using it any other way may not work and may not be lawful.

What is contact time?

How long a disinfectant must stay wet on a surface to kill the microorganisms listed on its label.

Does a new building need disinfecting before opening?

Usually cleaning is enough. Healthcare, food and childcare spaces may add disinfection or sanitizing per their requirements.

What else is disinfection service with registered products called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "disinfecting" and "EPA-registered disinfection". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
