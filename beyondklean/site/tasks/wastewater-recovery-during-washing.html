Wastewater recovery during washing

Wastewater recovery during washing collects water from exterior washing with vacuums and berms.

Also called wash water reclamation

Why it matters

Storm water rules prohibit discharging wash water.

How we approach it

Crews use recovery systems.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Questions about wastewater recovery during washing

Why recover wash water?

Storm water rules.

How?

Vacuums and berms.

Where does it go?

Approved disposal.

Is it required?

Often.

What else is wastewater recovery during washing called?
