Post-construction odor removal

Post-construction odor removal reduces lingering smells from paint, adhesives, sealers, sawdust and new materials in a finished building through source cleaning, ventilation support and low-odor products, rather than covering them with fragrance.

Why it matters

Strong new-construction odors bring tenant complaints and can delay move-in. Masking scents add more chemicals to the air without fixing the cause.

How we do it

Our crews find and clean odor sources such as adhesive smears and sealer residue, HEPA vacuum dust, and coordinate fresh air flush-out with the GC and mechanical contractor.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Rooms or floors with complaints
  • Recent finish work such as paint, flooring or sealers
  • HVAC flush-out availability
  • Fragrance-free product requirement
  • Timing before move-in or air testing

Watch-outs

  • Air fresheners and scented products mask odors and can raise VOC test results.
  • Some odors come from curing materials that only ventilation and time will fix.
  • Musty odors may signal moisture problems that need investigation, not cleaning.

Common questions

How do you get rid of new construction smell?

By removing sources such as adhesive and sealer residue and fine dust, then supporting fresh air flush-out. Time and ventilation finish what cleaning starts.

Do you use air fresheners to cover construction odors?

No. Masking scents add chemicals and can affect air testing. Our crews use fragrance-free, low-odor products and focus on removing the source.

How long does paint and adhesive smell last in a new building?

Usually days to a few weeks, depending on products used and ventilation. Running fresh air through the HVAC system shortens it considerably.

What if the smell in the new building is musty?

A musty odor can point to trapped moisture or mold, which needs investigation by the GC or a qualified consultant before cleaning will help.

Can odor removal be done before an IAQ test?

Yes, and it should be finished well before sampling so cleaning products have cleared and the independent testing firm measures the building itself.

Is ozone used for construction odor removal?

Not by our crews in occupied or newly finished buildings. Ozone can harm people and some materials, so we rely on source cleaning and ventilation.

What else is post-construction odor removal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "new construction odor removal" and "paint and adhesive odor removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
