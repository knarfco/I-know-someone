Wire, conduit and cable offcut pickup

Wire, conduit and cable offcut pickup collects copper and aluminum wire scraps, conduit cutoffs, cable ends and reels left by electrical and low-voltage installers.

Why it matters

Wire scraps are slippery underfoot and tangle in vacuums and floor machines. Copper and aluminum scraps have recycling value.

How we do it

Crews collect wire and conduit scraps into separate containers for metal recycling and pick up cable ends and ties before floors are cleaned.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Electrical and low-voltage activity
  • Recycling containers for copper and conduit
  • Cable reels and packaging
  • Timing with installers

Watch-outs

  • Wire scraps can jam vacuums and floor machines.
  • Do not remove reels or materials the electrician is still using.

Common questions

Is copper wire scrap recyclable?

Yes, and it has real value. Electrical contractors often keep their copper scrap.

Why pick up wire clippings before floor cleaning?

Wire scraps jam vacuums and scrubber brushes and can scratch new floors.

Who keeps electrical scrap on a jobsite?

Usually the electrical contractor; cleanup crews confirm before discarding it.

Are cable ties a cleanup problem?

Yes, cut ties scatter everywhere and must be picked up by hand.

What else is wire, conduit and cable offcut pickup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "electrical scrap pickup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
