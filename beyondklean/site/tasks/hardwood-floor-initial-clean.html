Hardwood floor initial clean

Hardwood floor initial clean cleans newly installed and finished solid hardwood floors, removing dust and residue with wood-safe, low-moisture methods.

Also called wood floor cleaning

Why it matters

New wood floor finishes are sensitive to water and chemicals and need time to cure. Construction grit tracked across them scratches the finish before the owner ever moves in.

How we approach it

Crews dust mop and vacuum, then clean with wood floor cleaners and barely damp microfiber.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Area
  • Finish type and cure time
  • Manufacturer guidance
  • Protection removal

Watch-outs

  • Water and wet mops can cup and damage wood floors.
  • Cleaning or covering before the finish cures can damage it.

Questions about hardwood floor initial clean

How are new hardwood floors cleaned after construction?

Dust mopped and vacuumed first, then cleaned with a wood floor cleaner and barely damp microfiber.

When can new hardwood floor finish be cleaned?

After it cures according to the finish manufacturer, often days for light cleaning and longer for full cure.

Can hardwood floors be mopped?

Only with a barely damp microfiber mop; standing water damages wood.

When can rugs go on newly finished wood floors?

After the finish fully cures, which can take a few weeks for some finishes.

What else is hardwood floor initial clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "wood floor cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
