Tenant improvement final clean

A tenant improvement final clean is the final clean of an interior build-out for a specific tenant before move-in.

Also called TI cleaning; tenant buildout cleaning

Why it matters

TI spaces often share buildings with occupied tenants and must be cleaned without disturbing them.

How we approach it

Crews clean the suite completely and coordinate building access.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Suite size
  • Building rules
  • Access
  • Move-in date

Watch-outs

  • Building rules limit hours.
  • Common areas must stay clean.
