Exterior window cleaning

Exterior window cleaning at construction turnover cleans the outside face of windows and frames, removing construction dust, stucco, paint, sealant and labels.

Why it matters

Exterior glass collects the heaviest construction residue from stucco, painting, masonry and sealants. It determines how the building looks from the street.

How we do it

Crews choose access by height, clean frames first, remove debris with methods suited to the glass and finish with washing or pure-water rinsing.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of windows and floors
  • Access: ground, ladder, lift or rope
  • Residue types on glass
  • Glass coatings

Watch-outs

  • Upper floors need fall protection and trained crews.
  • Acidic masonry cleaners can etch glass and frames.

Common questions

When are exterior windows cleaned on a new building?

After exterior finishes like stucco, paint and sealants are complete and scaffolding or lifts used by other trades are gone.

How are upper-floor exterior windows cleaned?

With lifts, swing stages, building maintenance units or rope access, depending on height and the building's anchorage.

Can stucco and paint be removed from exterior windows?

Usually, with soaking and careful scraping where the glass type allows, by crews experienced with construction glass.

Who cleans exterior windows at turnover?

A construction window cleaning contractor with the right access equipment and insurance.

What else is exterior window cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "outside window washing". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
