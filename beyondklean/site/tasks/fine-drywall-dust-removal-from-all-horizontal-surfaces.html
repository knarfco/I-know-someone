Fine drywall dust removal from all horizontal surfaces

Fine drywall dust removal from all horizontal surfaces captures the powdery gypsum and joint compound dust that settles on every ledge, sill, counter, shelf and top surface after drywall sanding.

Also called drywall dust cleaning; sheetrock dust removal

Why it matters

Drywall dust is fine enough to stay airborne and re-settle for days. Missed horizontal surfaces are where it keeps coming from, which is why buildings look dusty again a week after the final clean.

How we approach it

Crews work top to bottom with HEPA vacuums and damp microfiber, changing cloths often so dust is lifted off the surface rather than pushed into a film.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Every horizontal surface type to include: ledges, sills, counters, shelves, tops
  • Heights and access
  • Sequence with ceiling and light fixture cleaning
  • Inspection standard, such as a white-glove check

Watch-outs

  • Wiping with a dirty or dry cloth smears gypsum into a white film.
  • Dusting before sanding is finished means doing it twice.

Questions about fine drywall dust removal from all horizontal surfaces

Why does drywall dust keep showing up after cleaning?

It was moved instead of captured. HEPA vacuuming and damp microfiber remove it; dry dusting puts it back in the air.

What is the best way to remove drywall dust?

HEPA vacuum first, then wipe with damp microfiber, working top to bottom and changing cloths often.

How many times does drywall dust need to be cleaned?

Often twice: once after sanding finishes and again at the final clean.

Does drywall dust damage finishes?

It can scratch soft finishes if wiped dry, and it clogs HVAC filters.

What else is fine drywall dust removal from all horizontal surfaces called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "drywall dust cleaning" and "sheetrock dust removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
