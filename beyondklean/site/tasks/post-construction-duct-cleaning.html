Post-construction duct cleaning

Post-construction duct cleaning removes drywall dust, sawdust, debris and metal shavings from new or renovated ductwork, registers and air handler sections before the building is occupied, using source removal under negative air.

Why it matters

Systems run during construction pull dust into ducts. When the building opens, that dust blows into finished rooms and loads new filters and coils.

How we do it

Our crews isolate sections, put the system under HEPA-filtered negative air and agitate debris loose, following the NADCA ACR standard as the reference for methods and cleanliness.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of duct and number of air handlers
  • Supply, return and exhaust systems included
  • Access door installation responsibility
  • Before and after photo documentation
  • Coordination with the mechanical contractor and balancer

Watch-outs

  • Cutting access openings without the mechanical contractor's approval can void warranties.
  • Running the HVAC system during drywall sanding undoes the cleaning.
  • Fibrous duct liner needs gentler methods than bare sheet metal.

Common questions

Do new buildings need their ducts cleaned before move-in?

Often, yes. If the system ran during construction or ducts were left open, dust and debris are almost certainly inside, and cleaning before occupancy keeps it out of finished rooms.

What standard is used for construction duct cleaning?

The NADCA ACR standard is the common industry reference for source removal methods and cleanliness checks, and many specifications name it directly.

How is post-construction duct cleaning actually done?

Sections are isolated, a HEPA-filtered negative air machine pulls air through the duct, and brushes or air whips loosen debris so it is captured instead of blown into rooms.

Will you show us the ducts are clean afterward?

Yes. Our crews take before and after photos at access points and registers so the GC and commissioning agent can see the condition of each system.

When should duct cleaning happen on a construction project?

After dusty trades finish and before final filter change, test and balance, and occupancy. Cleaning too early means dust from finish work gets back in.

Can duct cleaning damage new ductwork?

Not when methods match the duct type. Our crews use softer tools on lined and flexible duct and coordinate access openings with the mechanical contractor.

What else is post-construction duct cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "construction duct cleaning" and "NADCA duct cleaning after construction". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
