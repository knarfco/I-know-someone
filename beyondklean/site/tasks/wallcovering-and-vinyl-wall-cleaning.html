Wallcovering and vinyl wall cleaning

Wallcovering and vinyl wall cleaning removes dust, adhesive smears and marks from commercial vinyl wallcovering, textiles and specialty wall finishes using methods the manufacturer allows.

Also called wallpaper cleaning

Why it matters

Wallcovering paste squeezes out at seams and dries shiny, and textile wallcoverings stain easily. The wrong cleaner can remove pattern or texture.

How we approach it

Crews remove paste residue with damp sponges and approved cleaners, clean vinyl with mild solutions and treat textile wallcoverings only with dry or manufacturer-approved methods.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Wallcovering type and manufacturer
  • Seams with paste residue
  • Areas and heights
  • Approved cleaning methods

Watch-outs

  • Textile wallcoverings can water-stain.
  • Solvents can damage vinyl and inks.

Questions about wallcovering and vinyl wall cleaning

How do you remove wallpaper paste from vinyl wallcovering?

With warm water and a damp sponge, rinsing often, ideally before the paste fully hardens.

Can fabric wallcoverings be washed?

Usually not. They need dry cleaning methods or manufacturer-approved spot cleaners.

Why do wallcovering seams look shiny?

Dried wallcovering paste along the seams catches the light and looks shiny. It is removed with warm water and a damp sponge.

Who cleans paste off new wallcovering?

The installer should, but final cleaning crews often finish the job.

What else is wallcovering and vinyl wall cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "wallpaper cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
