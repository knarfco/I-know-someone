Kitchens & Food Facilities · FRP and washable wall systems

FRP wall cleaning

FRP wall cleaning cleans fiberglass reinforced plastic wall panels in commercial kitchens, restrooms, food storage and back-of-house areas, removing construction dust, adhesive and residue from the textured surface.

Why it matters

FRP is chosen because it is cleanable and meets health code, but its pebbled texture traps dust and adhesive. Food facilities must have clean, intact, cleanable walls to pass inspection.

How we do it

Crews clean with neutral, non-abrasive cleaners and soft-bristle brushes that reach into the texture, rinse thoroughly and wipe dry.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Wall area by room
  • Adhesive and residue at seams
  • Trim and corners
  • Food-safe products

Watch-outs

  • Abrasive pads damage the FRP surface and expose fibers.
  • Some solvents soften FRP.

Common questions

What is FRP?

Fiberglass reinforced plastic: textured, washable wall panels used in kitchens, restrooms and food areas.

How is FRP cleaned after construction?

With neutral, non-abrasive cleaners and soft-bristle brushes that reach into the texture, followed by rinsing.

Can FRP be pressure washed?

Usually not. High pressure can force water behind panels and lift trim.

Why is FRP used in commercial kitchens?

It is durable, moisture-resistant and easy to clean, which helps meet health code.

What else is frp wall cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "FRP panel cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
