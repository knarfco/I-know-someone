Kitchens, Food Facilities & FRP · FRP and washable wall systems

FRP wall cleaning

FRP wall cleaning cleans fiberglass reinforced plastic wall panels in kitchens, restrooms and food areas, removing dust, adhesive and residue.

Also called FRP panel cleaning

Why it matters

FRP is a cleanable surface required in food areas, but its texture traps dust and adhesive.

How we approach it

Crews clean with non-abrasive, neutral cleaners and soft brushes, rinsing well.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Wall area
  • Adhesive residue
  • Trim and seams
  • Cleaners

Watch-outs

  • Abrasives damage FRP surfaces.
  • Solvents can damage FRP.

Questions about frp wall cleaning

What is FRP?

Fiberglass reinforced plastic wall panels.

How is FRP cleaned?

With neutral cleaners and soft brushes.

Can FRP be pressure washed?

Usually not.

Where is FRP used?

Kitchens, restrooms and food areas.

What else is frp wall cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "FRP panel cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
