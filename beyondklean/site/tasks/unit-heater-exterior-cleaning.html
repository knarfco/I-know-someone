Unit heater exterior cleaning

Unit heater exterior cleaning removes dust and debris from the housing, louvers and fan guards of suspended gas or electric unit heaters in warehouses, garages, shops and back-of-house areas.

Also called unit heater dusting

Why it matters

Unit heaters hang high and are installed early, so they collect heavy dust. When they fire for the first time, dust on the heat exchanger area can smoke and smell, and louvers spread dust across the room.

How we approach it

With power off and locked out by the authorized trade, crews HEPA vacuum the housing, louvers and fan guard, then damp-wipe the exterior. Internal burners and heat exchangers are left to the mechanical contractor.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of units and mounting height
  • Gas or electric and lockout responsibility
  • Louvers, guards and hangers included
  • Access around racking or equipment

Watch-outs

  • Units must be off and cool before cleaning, with lockout handled by the authorized trade.
  • Internal combustion components are serviced only by the mechanical contractor.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about unit heater exterior cleaning

Why do unit heaters smell when first turned on?

Construction dust burning off the heat exchanger and housing. Cleaning the exterior and louvers before startup reduces it.

Can a cleaning crew clean inside a unit heater?

No. Internal parts belong to the mechanical contractor. Cleaning crews handle the exterior only.

Should unit heaters be off during cleaning?

Yes, off, cool and locked out by the authorized trade.

Where are unit heaters common?

Warehouses, loading docks, garages, shops and back rooms of stores.

What else is unit heater exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "unit heater dusting". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
