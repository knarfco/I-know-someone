Shelving and adjustable shelf cleaning

Shelving and adjustable shelf cleaning cleans shelves, standards, brackets and shelf pin holes in storage rooms, offices, libraries and retail.

Also called shelf cleaning

Why it matters

Shelves are often installed early and collect dust that will end up on stored items. Pin holes trap dust.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum shelves and standards, wipe surfaces and remove labels, working top shelf down.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Shelving count and type
  • Standards and brackets
  • Labels
  • Items stored

Watch-outs

  • Do not overload shelves while cleaning.
  • Brackets can loosen if bumped.

Questions about shelving and adjustable shelf cleaning

How are shelves cleaned after construction?

Vacuumed first, then wiped with damp microfiber from the top shelf down.

Why clean shelf standards and pin holes?

Dust collects in the slots and holes and falls onto stored items.

Are storage room shelves part of the final clean?

Yes. All built-in and installed shelving, including storage rooms, is cleaned during the final clean before anything is stored.

Should shelves be cleaned before stocking?

Yes, it is much easier and more thorough before anything is stored.

What else is shelving and adjustable shelf cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "shelf cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
