Air scrubber filter change and unit wipe-down

Air scrubber filter change and unit wipe-down replaces pre-filters and HEPA filters in portable air scrubbers and negative air machines and cleans the units' exteriors.

Why it matters

Loaded filters reduce airflow and dust capture. Dusty units also shed dust when they are moved.

How we do it

Crews turn off units, bag used filters inside the work zone, install new filters per the manufacturer and wipe unit exteriors before moving them.

Common questions

How often should air scrubber filters be changed?

Pre-filters often, sometimes daily in heavy dust, and HEPA filters according to the manufacturer and pressure readings.

What is an air scrubber?

A portable machine that pulls air through HEPA filters to remove fine dust from a work area.

Can dirty filters reduce air scrubber performance?

Yes, loaded filters restrict airflow and reduce how much dust the unit captures.

Who changes air scrubber filters?

The contractor responsible for dust control, or a cleaning crew assigned to it.

What else is air scrubber filter change and unit wipe-down called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "air scrubber maintenance". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
