Glass shard and broken glazing cleanup

Glass shard and broken glazing cleanup safely collects broken glass from damaged windows, mirrors, shower doors or glazing units, including fine fragments in tracks and carpet.

Also called broken glass cleanup

Why it matters

Broken glass is a serious cut hazard and fragments travel far. Tempered glass breaks into thousands of small pieces that hide in carpet and floor seams.

How we approach it

Crews isolate the area, collect large pieces with gloved hands and tools, vacuum fragments with a HEPA vacuum suitable for glass, and dispose of it in labeled puncture-resistant containers.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Location and type of glass broken
  • Isolation of the area
  • Puncture-resistant containers
  • Vacuum suitable for glass

Watch-outs

  • Tempered glass fragments travel far; check a wide area.
  • Do not put broken glass loose in trash bags.

Questions about glass shard and broken glazing cleanup

How do you clean up broken tempered glass?

Collect larger pieces carefully, then vacuum fine fragments and check a wide area, including tracks and carpet.

Is broken glass cleanup a cleaning task?

Yes, but replacement of the glazing is done by the glazing contractor.

How should broken glass be disposed of?

In puncture-resistant, labeled containers, never loose in bags.

Can glass fragments damage floors?

Yes, they scratch floors when stepped on, so cleanup should be thorough.

What else is glass shard and broken glazing cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "broken glass cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
