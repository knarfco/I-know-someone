Correctional and detention facility cleaning

Correctional and detention facility cleaning is construction cleanup and final cleaning of jails, prisons and detention centers, carried out under strict tool control, contraband rules and escorted work inside secure areas.

Why it matters

Anything left behind in a secure facility can become contraband or a weapon. Missing tools, loose screws and stray debris can delay activation, and dust in security electronics causes faults.

How we do it

Our crews check tools in and out on written inventories, work only under escort in secure zones, remove every piece of debris and clean security hardware with approved methods.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Housing units, cells and dayrooms
  • Tool control inventory and check-in procedures
  • Escort schedule and secure-zone access
  • Security glazing, detention hardware and control rooms
  • Kitchen, laundry and medical units

Watch-outs

  • A single missing tool can lock down the site, so inventories are checked at every exit.
  • Personal items and phones are often banned inside the secure perimeter.
  • Escort availability sets the pace, so production rates run lower than commercial work.
  • Detention-grade glazing scratches if cleaned with abrasive pads.

Common questions

What is tool control on a jail construction project?

Every tool is logged in and out on a written inventory, kept within reach, and counted before crews leave a secure area, so nothing can become contraband or a weapon.

Do cleaners need an escort inside a prison?

In secure zones, almost always. We schedule work around the escort plan set by the facility and the prime contractor, which affects daily output and pricing.

What contraband rules apply to cleaning crews in a detention center?

Phones, lighters, glass containers and some chemicals may be banned. Crews bring only approved items, and chemicals are logged and stored where the facility allows.

How are cells cleaned after construction?

Crews remove all debris, vacuum and wipe detention fixtures, clean security glazing with non-abrasive products and check that no screws, wire or packaging are left behind.

Are security control rooms included in the final clean?

Yes, using HEPA vacuums and dry microfiber around consoles, and only with permission from the electronics installer when systems are live.

Why does detention facility cleaning cost more than office cleaning?

Escorts, tool counts, screening at every entry and contraband rules all slow production, so the same square footage takes more labor hours than a commercial final clean.

What else is correctional and detention facility cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "jail construction cleaning", "prison cleaning" and "detention center cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
