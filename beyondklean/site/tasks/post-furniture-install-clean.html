Post-furniture install clean

A post-furniture install clean removes packaging, cardboard, plastic wrap, dust and debris left after furniture installers finish, and cleans the new furniture and the floors around it.

Also called after FF&E clean

Why it matters

Furniture installation creates large volumes of packaging and leaves dust, fingerprints and scuffs. It often happens after the final clean, right before move-in.

How we approach it

Crews remove packaging, vacuum upholstery, wipe work surfaces and storage, clean floors and check for scuffs on walls and floors caused by installation.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Furniture installation schedule
  • Packaging volume and recycling
  • Furniture types and finishes
  • Floor and wall touch-ups

Watch-outs

  • Do not rearrange furniture placed per the plan.
  • Report scuffs and damage instead of hiding them.

Questions about post-furniture install clean

Why clean after furniture installation?

Installers leave packaging, dust and scuffs, and they usually work after the final clean.

Is new furniture cleaned before occupants arrive?

Yes, work surfaces and storage are wiped and upholstery vacuumed.

Who removes furniture packaging?

Furniture installers sometimes take it; otherwise the cleaning crew removes and recycles it.

When should the post-furniture clean happen?

Right after furniture installation finishes and before occupants move in, so packaging and dust are gone on move-in day.

What else is post-furniture install clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "after FF&E clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
