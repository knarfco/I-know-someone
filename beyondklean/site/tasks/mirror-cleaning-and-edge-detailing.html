Mirror cleaning and edge detailing

Mirror cleaning and edge detailing cleans mirrors and their edges, frames and clips, without letting liquid get behind the mirror where it damages the silvering.

Also called mirror cleaning

Why it matters

Mirrors are installed late, have labels and smudges, and show every streak. Liquid behind the edges causes black edges over time.

How we approach it

Crews spray cleaner onto cloths rather than mirrors, wipe edges carefully and remove labels with mirror-safe methods.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number and size of mirrors
  • Frames and clips
  • Labels
  • Restroom mirrors

Watch-outs

  • Liquid behind mirror edges damages silvering.
  • Ammonia cleaners can damage some mirror edges.

Questions about mirror cleaning and edge detailing

Why do mirror edges turn black?

Moisture and cleaners that seep behind the edges attack the silver backing and cause black edges over time.

How should mirrors be cleaned?

Spray cleaner onto a cloth, not the mirror, and wipe without letting liquid run to the edges.

Are mirror labels removed at final clean?

Yes. Labels and protective film are removed carefully at the final clean, without letting adhesive remover reach the mirror edges.

Can mirrors be scraped?

Only carefully, to lift small specks of paint or debris with a fresh blade held flat. Mirrors scratch more easily than most people expect.

What else is mirror cleaning and edge detailing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "mirror cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
