Grout line deep cleaning

Grout line deep cleaning scrubs the joints between tiles to remove embedded soil, residue and staining, restoring grout to its original color before turnover or sealing.

Why it matters

Grout is porous and absorbs dirt, and dirty grout makes the whole floor look dirty.

How we do it

Crews scrub with grout brushes or machines with suitable cleaners, then extract or rinse.

Watch-outs

  • Harsh acids weaken cementitious grout.
  • Unsealed grout re-soils quickly after cleaning.

Common questions

How is dirty grout cleaned?

With grout brushes or machines and cleaners suited to the grout, followed by extraction or rinsing.

Can grout be restored to its original color?

Deep cleaning often restores it; grout colorant is an option for stains that will not lift.

Why does grout get dirty so fast?

Cementitious grout is porous and absorbs dirt and spills.

Should grout be sealed after deep cleaning?

Often yes, to slow re-soiling, once the grout is completely dry.

What else is grout line deep cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "grout cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
