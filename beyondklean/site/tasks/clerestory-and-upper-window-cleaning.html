Clerestory and upper window cleaning

Clerestory and upper window cleaning cleans interior and exterior glass of high windows and clerestory bands that are above normal reach, plus frames and sills.

Also called high window cleaning

Why it matters

High windows let in daylight that shows every smudge and streak. They are often missed because they need lifts or poles and are not visible until the space is lit by daylight.

How we approach it

Crews use pure-water poles where possible, or lifts for scraping and detail work, cleaning frames and sills first so debris does not fall on clean glass.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number and size of upper windows
  • Interior, exterior or both
  • Access method: pole, lift or rope
  • Construction debris on glass

Watch-outs

  • Water-fed poles cannot remove paint or labels; detail work needs a lift.
  • Exterior upper glass may require fall protection and qualified crews.
Keep it clean after turnover. Overhead dust comes back. Recurring high dusting for this surface is handled through our partner program at AboveEyeLevelCleaning.com.

Questions about clerestory and upper window cleaning

How are high windows cleaned inside a building?

With water-fed poles for routine cleaning and lifts when debris must be removed by hand.

What is a clerestory window?

A band of windows high on a wall, above eye level, used to bring daylight into a space.

Are upper windows part of construction window cleaning?

They should be. Confirm they are listed in the window cleaning scope.

Do clerestories need exterior cleaning too?

Yes, usually from the roof or a lift, depending on the building.

What else is clerestory and upper window cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "high window cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
