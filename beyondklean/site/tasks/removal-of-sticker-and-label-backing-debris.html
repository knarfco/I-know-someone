Rough Clean · Rough clean (phase 1)

Removal of sticker and label backing debris

Removal of sticker and label backing debris collects the paper and plastic liners, tags and labels that installers peel off fixtures, doors and equipment.

Also called label debris cleanup

Why it matters

Label backings are small, slippery and everywhere. They stick to floors, clog vacuums and end up under new flooring if not picked up.

How we approach it

Crews pick up label debris by hand and vacuum during rough and progress cleans, focusing on areas where fixtures and doors were installed.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Areas with recent installations
  • Timing
  • Debris collection
  • Flooring schedules

Watch-outs

  • Label debris under flooring creates bumps.
  • Backings stick to wet floors.

Questions about removal of sticker and label backing debris

Why pick up label backings during construction?

They are slippery, clog vacuums and get trapped under new flooring.

Where does label backing debris come from?

Doors, fixtures, appliances and equipment as installers peel off labels.

Is label debris part of rough cleaning?

Yes, it is collected during rough and progress cleaning.

Can label backings cause flooring problems?

Yes, backings trapped under resilient flooring create visible bumps.

What else is removal of sticker and label backing debris called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "label debris cleanup". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
