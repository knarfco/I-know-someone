Corridor and hallway detailing

Corridor and hallway detailing cleans corridors from ceiling to floor, including walls, doors, signage, lights, wall protection and floors.

Also called corridor cleaning

Why it matters

Corridors connect every space and are walked by every visitor.

How we approach it

Crews clean top down and finish floors last.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Corridor length
  • Finishes
  • Doors
  • Floors

Watch-outs

  • Traffic during cleaning.
  • Corner guards and rails.

Questions about corridor and hallway detailing

What is included in corridor cleaning?

Walls, doors, signage, lights and floors.

Are corridors cleaned last?

Often, because trades use them to reach rooms.

Are corner guards cleaned?

Yes.

Is corridor cleaning part of final clean?

Yes.

What else is corridor and hallway detailing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "corridor cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
