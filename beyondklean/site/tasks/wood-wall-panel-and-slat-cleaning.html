Wood wall panel and slat cleaning

Wood wall panel and slat cleaning removes dust and fingerprints from wood veneer panels, slat walls and wood feature walls using finish-safe methods.

Also called wood veneer wall cleaning

Why it matters

Wood walls are feature elements, and slat walls trap dust between every slat. Water and harsh cleaners can damage wood finishes.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum slat gaps with soft crevice tools and wipe faces with dry or barely damp microfiber and wood-safe cleaners.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Wall area and wood finish
  • Slat spacing
  • Acoustic backing behind slats
  • Fingerprints and marks

Watch-outs

  • Excess water raises grain and damages finishes.
  • Acoustic felt behind slats can stain if wet.

Questions about wood wall panel and slat cleaning

How do you clean a wood slat wall?

Vacuum between the slats with a soft crevice tool, then wipe the faces with barely damp microfiber.

Can wood veneer walls be cleaned with all-purpose cleaner?

Use only wood-safe cleaners suited to the finish; all-purpose products can dull or damage it.

Why do slat walls get so dusty?

The gaps between slats trap dust, and acoustic felt behind them holds it.

Are wood walls part of the final clean?

Yes, feature walls are detailed during the final clean.

What else is wood wall panel and slat cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "wood veneer wall cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
