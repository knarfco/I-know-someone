Handrail and guardrail cleaning

Handrail and guardrail cleaning cleans stair and ramp handrails, guardrails, brackets and infill panels made of wood, metal, glass or cable.

Also calledrailing cleaning

Why it matters

Handrails are touched by everyone and collect fingerprints, overspray and dust. Glass and cable infill are easy to miss.

How we do it

Crews wipe rails and brackets with appropriate cleaners and clean infill panels, checking both sides.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of rail
  • Materials
  • Infill type
  • Access on stairs

Watch-outs

  • Stair work needs stable footing.
  • Wood rails may need wood-safe cleaners.

Common questions

How often are handrails cleaned?

At turnover, then frequently during operation because they are touched constantly.

Are guardrail infill panels cleaned?

Yes, glass, mesh and cable infill are cleaned on both sides.

How are stainless steel handrails cleaned?

With a neutral cleaner, then wiped dry with the grain.

Why are handrails a focus during final cleaning?

Everyone touches them, and fingerprints and dust show immediately.

What else is handrail and guardrail cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "railing cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
