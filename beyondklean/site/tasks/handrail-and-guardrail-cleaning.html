Handrail and guardrail cleaning

Handrail and guardrail cleaning cleans stair and ramp handrails, guardrails, brackets and infill panels made of wood, metal, glass or cable.

Also called railing cleaning

Why it matters

Handrails are touched by everyone and collect fingerprints, overspray and dust. Glass and cable infill are easy to miss.

How we approach it

Crews wipe rails and brackets with appropriate cleaners and clean infill panels, checking both sides.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of rail
  • Materials
  • Infill type
  • Access on stairs

Watch-outs

  • Stair work needs stable footing.
  • Wood rails may need wood-safe cleaners.

Questions about handrail and guardrail cleaning

How often are handrails cleaned?

At turnover, then frequently during operation.

Are guardrail infill panels cleaned?

Yes, both sides.

How are stainless handrails cleaned?

With neutral cleaner and wiping with the grain.

Why are handrails a focus?

They are touched constantly.

What else is handrail and guardrail cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "railing cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
