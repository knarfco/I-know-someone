Military hangar and aircraft maintenance facility cleaning

Military hangar and aircraft maintenance facility cleaning removes construction dust, debris and residue from new or renovated hangars, shops and flightline support buildings, with strict foreign object debris control before aircraft or equipment move in.

Why it matters

A loose bolt, wire tie or grit on a hangar floor can be pulled into an engine. Foreign object debris control is a safety and mission requirement, not a cosmetic step.

How we do it

Crews sweep and scrub hangar floors in grid passes, clean trench drains and high surfaces, run FOD walks with the contractor and document each cleared area.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Hangar floor square footage
  • High dusting of trusses and lighting
  • Trench drains and fire suppression trenches
  • Shops, offices and support rooms
  • FOD walks and documentation

Watch-outs

  • Small debris left in trench drains can surface later as FOD.
  • Lift work near new coatings needs floor protection.
  • Wash water may need recovery under base environmental rules.

Common questions

What is FOD control in hangar cleaning?

Foreign object debris control means finding and removing every loose item, from screws to grit, that could be pulled into an aircraft engine or damage equipment.

Do you clean new hangars on military bases?

Yes. Our crews clean hangar bays, shops and offices after construction, with access arranged through the prime contractor and the installation's process.

How do you clean a large hangar floor?

We sweep, then scrub with ride-on equipment in overlapping passes, detail edges and trenches by hand and finish with a documented FOD walk.

Do you clean hangar trusses and lights?

Yes, from lifts, top down, so dust from high work lands on the floor before the final scrub.

How is hangar cleaning priced?

Usually per square foot of floor, with high dusting, trench cleaning and lift rental noted separately.

What else is military hangar and aircraft maintenance facility cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "hangar cleaning" and "aircraft maintenance hangar construction cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
