Chiller and boiler exterior cleaning

Chiller and boiler exterior cleaning cleans the outside of central plant equipment, removing dust and overspray without opening or adjusting equipment.

Also called chiller cleaning

Why it matters

Central plant equipment is large and visible during commissioning and owner tours.

How we approach it

Crews clean cool, safe exterior surfaces with mechanical contractor approval.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Equipment
  • Approval
  • Hot surfaces
  • Labels

Watch-outs

  • Hot surfaces cause burns.
  • Do not touch controls.

Questions about chiller and boiler exterior cleaning

Who cleans chiller interiors?

Mechanical service contractors.

Can boilers be cleaned while hot?

No, only cool surfaces.

Why clean central plant equipment?

For commissioning and turnover.

Are labels left on?

Yes.

What else is chiller and boiler exterior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "chiller cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
