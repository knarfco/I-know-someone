Glass protective film removal

Glass protective film removal takes off temporary protective films applied to glass during construction, and removes any adhesive residue left behind.

Also called glass film removal; Window and glass protection removal; window film removal

Why it matters

Films protect glass during construction, but left too long in sun and heat they bake on and leave residue.

How we approach it

Crews peel film at the right angle and speed, remove residue with glass-safe products and dispose of film properly.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Glass with film
  • Film age and sun exposure
  • Residue removal
  • Timing

Watch-outs

  • Baked-on film is hard to remove.
  • Blades can scratch while removing residue.

Questions about glass protective film removal

When should glass protective film be removed?

Near the end of construction, before it bakes on.

Why does film leave residue?

Heat and UV make the adhesive bond harder.

How is film residue removed?

With glass-safe adhesive removers.

Who removes window film?

The glazier or cleaning crew, depending on the contract.

What else is glass protective film removal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "glass film removal", "Window and glass protection removal" and "window film removal". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
