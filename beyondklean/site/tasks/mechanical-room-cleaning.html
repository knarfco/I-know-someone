Mechanical, Electrical & Construction IAQ · Mechanical and electrical rooms

Mechanical room cleaning

Mechanical room cleaning removes construction debris, dust and packaging from boiler rooms, pump rooms and mechanical equipment rooms, cleaning floors, walls and equipment exteriors without touching controls.

Also called boiler room cleaning; MER cleaning

Why it matters

Mechanical rooms hold the building's most expensive equipment, and dust drawn into motors, filters and controls shortens its life. Commissioning agents and fire inspectors walk these rooms before turnover.

How we approach it

Crews clear debris first, HEPA vacuum equipment tops and floors, then damp-wipe exteriors and pipe insulation jackets, keeping liquids away from motors, panels and controls.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Room list and equipment installed
  • Access and lockout coordination with the mechanical contractor
  • Floor finish, sealed or painted
  • Commissioning and inspection dates

Watch-outs

  • Never adjust valves, switches or controls; leave equipment exactly as found.
  • Housekeeping pads and floor drains often hold grout and debris that must be cleared, not washed down.

Questions about mechanical room cleaning

What is included in mechanical room cleaning after construction?

Debris removal, HEPA vacuuming and damp wiping of equipment exteriors, piping, walls and floors, plus clearing floor drains, all without touching controls or opening equipment.

Why do commissioning agents care about clean mechanical rooms?

Dust and debris can clog filters, coils and motors during testing, and a clean room makes leaks and labeling problems easy to spot.

Can a cleaning crew wipe down boilers and pumps?

Crews can wipe the cool, de-energized exterior surfaces. Anything inside the equipment, and any energized or hot surface, is left to the mechanical contractor.

Should mechanical room floors be sealed before turnover?

Many specs call for sealed or painted floors in mechanical rooms. Cleaning should be finished before any sealer goes down, and the sealer manufacturer's cleaning guidance followed afterward.

What else is mechanical room cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "boiler room cleaning" and "MER cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
