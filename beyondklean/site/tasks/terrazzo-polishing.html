Hard Floors · Tile, stone and terrazzo

Terrazzo polishing

Terrazzo polishing grinds and polishes cement or epoxy terrazzo floors with diamond tooling to remove scratches, etching and wear and restore the finish. It is restoration specialty work.

Also called terrazzo restoration

Why it matters

Worn, etched or scratched terrazzo can often be restored instead of replaced, but improper grinding damages the matrix and chips.

How we approach it

Requests are reviewed and assigned only to terrazzo restoration specialists, who grind and polish in stages and control slurry.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Terrazzo type: cement or epoxy
  • Condition and damage
  • Area and access
  • Downtime

Watch-outs

  • Improper grinding can expose voids or damage chips.
  • Slurry must be controlled.

Questions about terrazzo polishing

Can old terrazzo floors be restored?

Often yes, by grinding and polishing to remove wear and etching.

Is terrazzo polishing done by cleaning crews?

No. Terrazzo grinding and polishing is done by terrazzo restoration specialists with heavy diamond tooling and slurry control.

How long does terrazzo polishing take?

It depends on area and condition, often several days for large lobbies.

Does terrazzo need sealing after polishing?

Cement terrazzo is usually sealed after polishing to resist stains. Epoxy terrazzo often needs less sealing, depending on the manufacturer.

What else is terrazzo polishing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "terrazzo restoration". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
