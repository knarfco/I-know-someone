Hard Floors · Tile, stone and terrazzo

Terrazzo polishing

Terrazzo polishing grinds and polishes terrazzo to restore its finish. It is restoration specialty work.

Also called terrazzo restoration

Why it matters

Worn or damaged terrazzo can be restored by specialists, but improper polishing damages it.

How we approach it

Terrazzo restoration specialists are routed only after review.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Terrazzo type
  • Condition
  • Area
  • Downtime

Watch-outs

  • Improper grinding damages terrazzo.
  • Slurry control is required.

Questions about terrazzo polishing

Can old terrazzo be restored?

Often, by grinding and polishing.

Is terrazzo polishing done by cleaners?

No, by restoration specialists.

How long does terrazzo polishing take?

It depends on area and condition.

Does terrazzo need to be sealed after polishing?

Often.

What else is terrazzo polishing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "terrazzo restoration". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
