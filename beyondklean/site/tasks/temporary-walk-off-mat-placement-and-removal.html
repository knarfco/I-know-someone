Temporary walk-off mat placement and removal

Temporary walk-off mat placement and removal places mats at entrances during construction to catch dirt and removes them at the end.

Also called temporary mats

Why it matters

Mats reduce dirt tracked onto finishes.

How we approach it

Crews place, clean and replace mats.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Entrances
  • Mat type
  • Cleaning
  • Removal

Watch-outs

  • Mats can be trip hazards.
  • Full mats stop working.

Questions about temporary walk-off mat placement and removal

Why use temporary mats?

To catch dirt.

How often are mats changed?

When full.

Are mats trip hazards?

If not flat.

When are they removed?

At the end.

What else is temporary walk-off mat placement and removal called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "temporary mats". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
