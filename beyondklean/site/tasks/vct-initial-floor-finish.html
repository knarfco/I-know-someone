Hard Floors · Resilient floors (VCT, LVT, sheet vinyl, rubber)

VCT initial floor finish

VCT initial floor finish prepares newly installed vinyl composition tile by removing the factory coating and adhesive residue, then applying multiple coats of floor finish.

Also called VCT initial wax; new VCT finishing

Why it matters

New VCT has a factory coating that floor finish does not bond to well. Skipping preparation leads to peeling finish and a floor that never looks right.

How we approach it

Crews clean or strip the new tile per the manufacturer, rinse, apply sealer if required, and lay down several thin coats of finish with proper drying time.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Area of VCT
  • Manufacturer initial maintenance instructions
  • Number of finish coats
  • Cure time before traffic

Watch-outs

  • Too-thick coats cause poor drying and soft finish.
  • Traffic before cure damages the finish.

Questions about vct initial floor finish

Why does new VCT need an initial floor finish?

New vinyl composition tile has a factory coating that floor finish does not bond to well. It must be prepared and then built up with several coats.

How many coats of finish go on new VCT?

Commonly four to six thin coats, depending on the specification and traffic.

When can people walk on new floor finish?

After it cures per the product, usually light traffic after a few hours and full traffic after a day or more.

Is floor finish the same as wax?

Floor finish is a polymer coating, but people commonly call it wax.

What else is vct initial floor finish called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "VCT initial wax" and "new VCT finishing". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
