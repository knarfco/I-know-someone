Raised access floor cleaning

Raised access floor cleaning cleans removable floor panels and the plenum below in data centers and offices, using HEPA vacuums and antistatic methods.

Also called raised floor cleaning; data center floor tile cleaning

Why it matters

Dust under raised floors is distributed through cooling air into equipment.

How we approach it

Trained crews clean panels and the plenum with HEPA vacuums, lifting panels with proper tools.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Area
  • Plenum depth
  • Panel lifting
  • Cable management

Watch-outs

  • Do not remove too many panels at once.
  • Do not disturb cables.

Questions about raised access floor cleaning

What is a raised access floor?

Removable panels over a plenum for cables and air.

Why clean under raised floors?

Dust is distributed through cooling air.

Who cleans raised floors?

Trained crews.

How are raised floor panels lifted?

With suction lifters.

What else is raised access floor cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "raised floor cleaning" and "data center floor tile cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
