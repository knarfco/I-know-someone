Mechanical, Electrical & Construction IAQ · Mechanical and electrical rooms

Electrical room non-invasive cleaning

Electrical room non-invasive cleaning cleans the floors, walls and outside of enclosures in electrical rooms using dry and HEPA methods only, with no liquids near equipment and nothing opened.

Also called electrical room cleaning

Why it matters

Electrical rooms collect drywall and concrete dust that can work into switchboards and panels. At the same time, they are among the most dangerous rooms in the building to clean.

How we approach it

Only crews approved by the electrical contractor enter, under their rules. Floors are HEPA vacuumed and dry or barely damp mopped away from gear, and enclosure exteriors are dry-wiped only.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Permission and escort from the electrical contractor or owner
  • Which surfaces may be cleaned
  • Energized status of equipment
  • Required PPE and approach limits

Watch-outs

  • No liquids, sprays or metal tools near electrical equipment.
  • Never open panel doors or remove covers; that is electrical work.

Questions about electrical room non-invasive cleaning

Can a cleaning company clean an electrical room?

Only non-invasive cleaning of floors, walls and the outside of enclosures, with the electrical contractor's permission and following their safety rules.

Why is water not allowed in electrical rooms?

Liquids near energized equipment create shock and arc-flash hazards and can damage gear.

Who cleans inside switchgear and panels?

Qualified electrical workers. Internal cleaning is maintenance work done de-energized by electricians.

Do inspectors check electrical rooms for debris?

Yes. Stored materials and debris in front of panels violate required working clearances and are a common finding.

What else is electrical room non-invasive cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "electrical room cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
