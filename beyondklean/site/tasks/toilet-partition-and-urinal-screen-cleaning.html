Toilet partition and urinal screen cleaning

Toilet partition and urinal screen cleaning removes film, labels, adhesive, fingerprints and dust from restroom stall partitions, doors, pilasters and urinal screens.

Also called partition cleaning

Why it matters

Partitions often ship with protective film and labels. Their surfaces and hardware are inspected closely in restrooms.

How we approach it

Crews remove film and labels, clean surfaces with material-appropriate cleaners and detail hardware and brackets.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Partition material
  • Film and labels
  • Hardware
  • Pilaster shoes

Watch-outs

  • Solvents can damage plastic and powder-coat.
  • Do not adjust door hardware.

Questions about toilet partition and urinal screen cleaning

How are toilet partitions cleaned after installation?

With cleaners matched to the material: stainless, powder-coated steel, plastic laminate or solid plastic.

Do toilet partitions come with protective film?

Often, and it is removed at the final clean along with labels.

What are pilaster shoes on toilet partitions?

Trim pieces at the base of partition pilasters that collect dirt and are cleaned with the partitions.

Are urinal screens cleaned the same way?

Yes, using the same products as the partitions.

What else is toilet partition and urinal screen cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "partition cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
