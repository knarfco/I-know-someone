Walk-in cooler interior cleaning

Walk-in cooler interior cleaning cleans the walls, ceiling, floor, shelving, door gaskets and evaporator housing exterior of new walk-in coolers before they are started and stocked.

Also called walk-in cleaning

Why it matters

Dust and debris in a walk-in end up on food. Once the box is cold and stocked, cleaning is much harder.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum and wipe panels, shelving and floors with food-safe cleaners, clean door gaskets and leave evaporator coils to refrigeration technicians.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Box size
  • Shelving
  • Floor type
  • Refrigeration startup date

Watch-outs

  • Do not spray water into electrical components.
  • Coils are serviced by refrigeration contractors.

Questions about walk-in cooler interior cleaning

When should a walk-in cooler be cleaned?

Before it is started and stocked.

Can cleaning crews clean evaporator coils?

No, refrigeration contractors handle coils.

Why clean door gaskets?

Dirty or damaged gaskets affect the seal.

What cleaners are used in walk-ins?

Food-safe cleaners.

What else is walk-in cooler interior cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "walk-in cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
