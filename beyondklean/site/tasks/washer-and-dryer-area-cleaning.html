Washer and dryer area cleaning

Washer and dryer area cleaning cleans laundry closets and rooms, including appliance exteriors and drums, shelves, floors, walls and the area behind and beside machines.

Also called laundry room cleaning

Why it matters

Laundry closets collect drywall dust and debris during construction, and the gaps around machines are easy to miss.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum behind and around appliances where accessible, wipe drums and exteriors, and clean shelving and floors.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Laundry area size
  • Appliance access
  • Shelving
  • Dryer vent connection

Watch-outs

  • Do not pull appliances out without permission; connections can be damaged.
  • Dryer vents should be checked separately.

Questions about washer and dryer area cleaning

Are washer and dryer drums cleaned at turnover?

Yes, drums are wiped to remove dust and packaging debris.

Is the area behind the washer cleaned?

Accessible areas are vacuumed; appliances are not moved without permission.

Do laundry closets get dusty during construction?

Yes, they collect drywall dust and debris.

Is the dryer vent cleaned too?

The vent connection is checked separately for debris.

What else is washer and dryer area cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "laundry room cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
