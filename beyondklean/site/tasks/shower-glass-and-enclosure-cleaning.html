Shower glass and enclosure cleaning

Shower glass and enclosure cleaning removes labels, silicone haze, dust and water spots from new shower doors, panels and their hardware.

Also called shower door cleaning

Why it matters

Shower glass is installed late, often with silicone haze and labels. Water spots and hard water appear quickly after first use.

How we approach it

Crews remove labels and silicone residue carefully, clean glass and hardware with non-acidic cleaners and dry with squeegees.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of enclosures
  • Glass coatings
  • Silicone residue
  • Hardware finishes

Watch-outs

  • Some shower glass has protective coatings that harsh cleaners damage.
  • Do not disturb fresh silicone joints.

Questions about shower glass and enclosure cleaning

How is silicone haze removed from new shower glass?

With careful scraping on glass that allows it and silicone removers safe for glass and any coating.

Can protective coatings on shower glass be damaged?

Yes. Abrasive pads and harsh cleaners can damage factory-applied water-repellent coatings.

Are shower doors cleaned at the final clean?

Yes, glass, hardware, tracks and seals are cleaned and labels removed.

How do you prevent water spots on new shower glass?

Dry the glass with a squeegee or cloth after cleaning and after each use.

What else is shower glass and enclosure cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "shower door cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
