Epoxy floor preparation cleaning

Epoxy floor preparation cleaning removes dust, oil, grease and contaminants from concrete slabs before epoxy, urethane or other resinous coatings are applied, so the coating bonds properly.

Why it matters

Common questions

Why is surface preparation so important for epoxy floors?

Epoxy only bonds to clean, sound concrete. Dust, oil and residues are the main causes of coating failure.

What contaminants cause epoxy floors to fail?

Dust, oil, grease, curing compounds, old sealers and moisture coming up through the slab.

Who prepares concrete floors for epoxy coatings?

The coating contractor is responsible for preparation, often with cleaning support beforehand.

Can epoxy be applied over sealed concrete?

Usually only after the sealer is removed by grinding or blasting.

What else is epoxy floor preparation cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "epoxy prep clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
