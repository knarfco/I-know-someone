Natural stone floor initial clean

Natural stone floor initial clean cleans newly installed marble, limestone, travertine, granite and slate floors with stone-safe, pH-neutral products and coordinates with sealing.

Also called marble floor cleaning; stone floor cleaning

Why it matters

Natural stone is sensitive to acids and absorbs stains. The first cleaning sets the tone for the floor's life.

How we approach it

Crews use pH-neutral stone cleaners, soft pads and careful residue removal, and coordinate with sealing.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Stone types and finishes
  • Sealer status
  • Residues
  • Manufacturer or fabricator guidance

Watch-outs

  • Acids etch marble, limestone and travertine.
  • Unsealed stone absorbs stains from spills and residue.

Questions about natural stone floor initial clean

How are new marble floors cleaned?

With pH-neutral stone cleaners and soft pads, never acidic or harsh alkaline products.

Can grout haze be removed from marble?

Only with non-acidic haze removers made for calcareous stone.

Should natural stone floors be sealed?

Many should, especially porous stones, according to the installer and stone supplier.

Who repairs etched or scratched stone?

Stone restoration specialists, who hone and polish the surface.

What else is natural stone floor initial clean called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "marble floor cleaning" and "stone floor cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
