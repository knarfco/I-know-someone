FRP trim and seam detailing

FRP trim and seam detailing cleans the plastic or metal division bars, inside and outside corners, cap moldings and seams between FRP panels, removing dust, adhesive and grime.

Also called FRP joint cleaning

Why it matters

Trim and seams are where dirt and adhesive collect on FRP walls. Gaps and dirty joints are a sanitation concern in food areas.

How we approach it

Crews clean trim with soft brushes and neutral cleaner, remove adhesive at joints and report open seams or loose trim for repair.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Linear feet of trim
  • Seam and joint condition
  • Adhesive at trim
  • Loose or missing trim

Watch-outs

  • Do not pull or pry loose trim pieces.
  • Open seams should be reported for sealing.

Questions about frp trim and seam detailing

What is FRP trim?

Plastic or metal moldings that join FRP panels and finish corners and edges.

Why do FRP seams need detailing?

Dirt and adhesive collect in joints and are hard to see until the wall is finished.

Are FRP seams sealed?

In food areas, seams are often sealed so they stay cleanable.

Is FRP trim cleaned during kitchen turnover?

Yes. FRP trim and seams are cleaned along with the panels as part of the kitchen wall cleaning.

What else is frp trim and seam detailing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "FRP joint cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
