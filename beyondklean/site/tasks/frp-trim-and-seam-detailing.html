FRP trim and seam detailing

FRP trim and seam detailing cleans the plastic or metal trim pieces and joints between FRP panels.

Also called FRP joint cleaning

Why it matters

Trim and seams trap dirt and adhesive.

How we approach it

Crews clean trim with soft brushes and neutral cleaners.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Trim length
  • Seam conditions
  • Cleaners
  • Damage

Watch-outs

  • Do not pull trim loose.
  • Report gaps.

Questions about frp trim and seam detailing

What is FRP trim?

Pieces that join and finish FRP panels.

Why clean FRP seams?

They trap dirt.

Are FRP seams sealed?

Often.

Is trim cleaning part of kitchen turnover?

Yes.

What else is frp trim and seam detailing called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "FRP joint cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
