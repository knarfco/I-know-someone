Sub-floor plenum cleaning in data centers

Sub-floor plenum cleaning in data centers cleans the space beneath raised access floors, where cooling air flows to equipment, removing construction dust and debris with HEPA equipment.

Why it matters

Dust and debris under the floor are blown straight into IT equipment through perforated tiles. Construction leaves cable scraps, dust and trash in the plenum.

How we do it

Trained crews lift panels in limited sections with proper lifters, HEPA vacuum the subfloor and pedestals, and replace panels before moving on.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Plenum area and depth
  • Panel lifting plan
  • Cabling and fiber in the plenum
  • HEPA equipment

Watch-outs

  • Removing too many panels at once weakens the floor grid.
  • Cables and fiber must not be pulled or stepped on.

Common questions

Why clean under a data center raised floor?

The plenum delivers cooling air, so dust there is blown into equipment.

How is a raised floor plenum cleaned?

Panels are lifted in sections and the subfloor is HEPA vacuumed, then panels are replaced.

Who cleans data center subfloors?

Trained critical-environment crews who use HEPA vacuums, antistatic methods and proper panel lifters.

Is subfloor cleaning done before equipment is installed?

Ideally yes, as part of the post-construction clean, because the plenum is far easier to clean before cabling and equipment fill it.

What else is sub-floor plenum cleaning in data centers called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "underfloor cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
