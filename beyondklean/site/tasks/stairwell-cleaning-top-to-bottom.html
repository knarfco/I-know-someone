Stairwell cleaning top to bottom

Stairwell cleaning top to bottom details stair towers from the roof landing to the ground floor, including rails, treads, risers, landings, walls and doors.

Also called stair tower cleaning

Why it matters

Every trade uses the stairs, so stairwells are the dirtiest spaces at turnover. They are also exit routes that fire inspectors check.

How we approach it

Crews start at the top floor, dust rails and ledges, clean walls and doors, then treads and landings, working down so nothing falls on cleaned areas.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Number of stair towers and floors
  • Tread finish
  • Doors and hardware
  • Inspection date

Watch-outs

  • Wet stairs are slippery; post signs and work in sections.
  • Keep the exit route usable during cleaning.

Questions about stairwell cleaning top to bottom

Why are stairwells cleaned from the top down?

So dust and debris fall onto areas not yet cleaned.

Do fire inspectors check stairwells?

Yes, they are exit routes.

What is included in stairwell cleaning?

Rails, treads, risers, landings, walls and doors.

How long does it take to clean a stair tower?

It depends on height and dirt; tall towers can take a full shift.

What else is stairwell cleaning top to bottom called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "stair tower cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
