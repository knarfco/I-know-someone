Railway and transit station cleaning

Railway and transit station cleaning is the construction cleanup and final clean of train, subway, light rail and bus stations, removing dust, debris and residue from platforms, concourses, stairs, elevators and canopies before the station opens or reopens.

Why it matters

Stations carry large crowds on hard surfaces. Construction dust on platforms becomes slippery, and debris near tracks creates safety hazards for trains and maintenance crews.

How we do it

Crews work during track outages or night windows under the transit agency's safety rules, keep debris away from the trackway, and clean platforms, glass and finishes top to bottom.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Platform and concourse area
  • Track access windows and protection
  • Elevators, escalators and stairs
  • Glass canopies and shelters
  • Signage and fare equipment exteriors

Watch-outs

  • Crews never enter the trackway without the transit agency's protection and approval.
  • Debris must be kept from falling onto tracks or into drainage.
  • Escalators and elevators are cleaned only with the maintenance contractor's coordination.

Common questions

When are transit stations cleaned during construction?

During night windows or scheduled track outages set by the transit agency. Final cleaning is scheduled just before the station opens or the platform returns to service.

Do cleaners need safety training to work at a rail station?

Yes. The transit agency or GC sets roadway worker or site safety training. Our crews complete what the project requires before working near tracks.

What is cleaned on a new train platform?

Platform surfaces, tactile warning strips, benches, shelters, glass, lighting fixtures, signage frames and railings. Debris near the platform edge is removed carefully away from the track.

Can you clean escalators and elevators in a new station?

We clean cab interiors, glass and exterior panels. Steps, combs and machinery are coordinated with the elevator and escalator contractor, who controls running and lockout.

How do you remove concrete dust from station platforms?

With HEPA vacuums and auto-scrubbers where water is allowed, and damp mopping elsewhere. Dry sweeping is avoided because it spreads silica dust into the air.

Who arranges access to the station for cleaning crews?

The general contractor coordinates with the transit agency for badges, escorts and work windows. We schedule crews to match the approved access plan.

What else is railway and transit station cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "train station construction cleaning" and "transit station final clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
