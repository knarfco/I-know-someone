Rough clean by floor or phase

A rough clean by floor or phase cleans one floor, wing or area at a time as it is released by drywall and MEP trades, rather than waiting for the whole building.

Also called partial rough clean

Why it matters

Large projects rarely finish rough-in everywhere at once. Phasing the rough clean lets finish trades start sooner in each released area.

How we approach it

Crews follow the GC's release schedule, cleaning each area completely and documenting it as ready before the next trade enters.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Phase or floor release schedule
  • Area sizes
  • Documentation of released areas
  • Crew availability for multiple mobilizations

Watch-outs

  • Multiple mobilizations can cost more; agree on pricing per phase.
  • Adjacent active areas can re-soil cleaned ones.

Questions about rough clean by floor or phase

Why do a rough clean floor by floor?

So finish trades can start in each area as soon as it is released, instead of waiting for the whole building.

Does a phased rough clean cost more?

It can, because crews mobilize several times, but it usually saves schedule time.

How are rough clean phases defined?

By floors, wings or zones that match the GC's construction schedule.

How is a rough-cleaned area kept clean?

With barriers, mats and coordination with trades working nearby.

What else is rough clean by floor or phase called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "partial rough clean". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
