Escalator and moving walk cleaning

Escalator and moving walk cleaning cleans the balustrades, handrails, skirt panels, decking and exterior panels of escalators and moving walks, only with the escalator contractor's lockout and approval.

Also called escalator cleaning

Why it matters

Escalators are complex, moving equipment. Debris in steps and comb plates causes damage and safety problems.

How we approach it

Requests are reviewed and routed only with the escalator contractor's lockout and approval.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Units
  • Lockout
  • Exterior surfaces
  • Contractor approval

Watch-outs

  • Never clean a running escalator.
  • Comb plates and steps are serviced by the contractor.

Questions about escalator and moving walk cleaning

Who cleans escalators in a new building?

Exterior surfaces can be cleaned by trained crews with the escalator contractor's lockout and approval. Steps, comb plates and mechanisms are serviced by the escalator contractor.

Why is escalator cleaning treated as conditional work?

Escalators are moving machinery with pinch, entanglement and fall hazards, so access and lockout are controlled by the escalator contractor.

Are escalator handrails cleaned at turnover?

Yes, with cleaners approved by the escalator manufacturer, because some products damage handrail rubber.

Can construction debris damage escalators?

Yes. Grit and screws in steps and comb plates can damage the machine and create trip hazards.

What else is escalator and moving walk cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "escalator cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
