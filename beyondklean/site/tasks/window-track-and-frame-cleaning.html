Window track and frame cleaning

Window track and frame cleaning removes debris, dust, sealant and paint from window tracks, frames, weep holes and operable window hardware.

Also called window track cleaning

Why it matters

Debris in tracks makes windows hard to operate and blocks drainage. Frames are visible and collect residue.

How we approach it

Crews vacuum tracks and weep holes, wipe frames and hardware and check that operable windows open and close freely.

Methods follow our capture-first, least-aggressive-chemistry standard.

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Window types and count
  • Operable windows
  • Weep holes
  • Frame finishes

Watch-outs

  • Blocked weep holes cause leaks.
  • Do not adjust window hardware.

Questions about window track and frame cleaning

Why are window tracks cleaned after construction?

Debris in tracks makes operable windows hard to open and close and can block drainage.

What are window weep holes?

Small openings in the frame that drain water out. Blocked weep holes can cause leaks inside.

How are window tracks cleaned?

Debris is vacuumed out with crevice tools, then tracks are wiped and weep holes checked.

Are window frames cleaned before or after the glass?

Before, so dust from frames does not fall onto clean glass.

What else is window track and frame cleaning called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "window track cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a request under any of them reaches the same qualified contractors.
