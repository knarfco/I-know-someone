Hurricane and storm reconstruction cleanup

Hurricane and storm reconstruction cleanup is construction cleaning during the rebuild of storm-damaged buildings, clearing wind-driven debris, sediment, roofing scraps and construction dust inside and around the structure so repairs can proceed.

Why it matters

Storm damage leaves sand, silt, glass and roofing debris in places new work will cover. Rebuilding over it leads to failed finishes and repeat repairs.

How we do it

Our crews clear debris in sequence with the rebuild, clean interiors once drying is confirmed and sweep exterior areas for nails and glass before occupants return.

Our capture-first method

Scope checklist for your bid

  • Interior and exterior areas in scope
  • Drying and moisture sign-off status
  • Roofing and window replacement schedule
  • Debris sorting rules for the local haul program
  • Magnetic sweeping of grounds

Watch-outs

  • Storm debris often hides broken glass and nails that need magnetic sweeps.
  • Crew availability after a major storm is limited, so confirm dates before committing.
  • Local storm debris programs may require debris separated by type at the curb.

Common questions

How soon after a hurricane can rebuild cleanup start?

Once the site is safe to enter, utilities are stable and the restoration team has finished drying. Exterior debris clearing can usually start earlier than interior cleaning.

Do you remove sand and silt that came in with storm surge?

Yes. Our crews shovel, HEPA vacuum and wet clean sediment from slabs and lower walls, once the restoration contractor has removed wet materials that cannot be saved.

Can you clean up roofing debris after a storm roof replacement?

Yes. Our crews collect shingles, nails and flashing scraps from the roof edge, gutters, landscaping and walkways, finishing with a magnetic sweep of the grounds.

Do you work on multiple storm-damaged buildings at once?

We plan crew assignments across sites based on rebuild schedules, and after large storms we are clear about available dates before anyone commits to a turnover.

Is storm reconstruction cleanup charged by the hour or by area?

Interior work is usually priced by square foot, while heavy exterior debris clearing is often by crew-hour or load because conditions vary so much after a storm.

What should be cleaned before tenants return after a storm rebuild?

Floors, windows, fixtures, HVAC grilles, cabinets and the grounds around entrances. A full final clean plus a nail and glass sweep outside is the usual standard.

What else is hurricane and storm reconstruction cleanup called?

Contractors, specs and buyers also call it "hurricane rebuild cleanup" and "storm damage reconstruction cleaning". On Beyond Klean, all of these names point to the same scope, so a bid request under any of them gets the same crew and the same line item.
