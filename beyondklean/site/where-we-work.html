How travel crews work

  • A crew lead and core team travel to the site, sized to the schedule.
  • Lodging, per diem and mobilization are shown as their own lines on the bid.
  • Local labor can be added under our supervision for very large scopes.
  • The same methods, checklists and closeout documents as our home crews.
