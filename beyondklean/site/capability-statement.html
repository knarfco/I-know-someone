Core competencies

  • Post-construction cleaning by phase: rough, final, touch-up and punch
  • Overhead and high-structure cleaning from lifts: decks, joists, ducts, fixtures
  • Construction indoor air quality support: HEPA capture, filter changes, flush-out readiness
  • Initial floor care: VCT finish, sealed concrete, tile and grout haze, carpet extraction
  • Construction glass and window cleaning, film and label removal
  • Commercial kitchen, FRP and food-facility turnover cleaning
  • Occupied-facility and phased cleaning under security and infection-control rules
  • Debris management, recycling separation and closeout documentation

Differentiators

  • Itemized, phase-by-phase bids priced from a documented unit-price book
  • Capture-first methods: HEPA vacuums and wet methods aligned with OSHA 1926.1153 silica rules
  • Least-aggressive chemistry matched to manufacturer care guides, protecting finish warranties
  • Closeout package: room checklists, photo documentation and product SDS
  • Home crews in the Southeast, travel crews nationwide
