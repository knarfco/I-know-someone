Safety and compliance

Safe crews, clean paperwork

General contractors and owners need more than a clean building. They need a crew that follows the site safety plan and hands over the documents at the start and the end of the job.

How our crews work safely

  • Silica-aware housekeeping. No dry sweeping or compressed air where silica dust is present. HEPA vacuums and wet methods, per OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1153.
  • Work at height. Trained lift operators, fall protection in boom lifts and site-specific lift plans for overhead cleaning.
  • Hazard communication. Safety Data Sheets on site for every product, labeled containers and trained crews.
  • Coordination with other trades. Lockout and equipment-start coordination with the electrical and mechanical contractors before cleaning equipment rooms.
  • Site rules. We follow the GC's safety plan, attend orientation and toolbox talks, and report incidents and near misses the same day.
  • Regulated work. Mold, abatement, biohazard and hood cleaning go only to licensed, certified technicians, with the required notifications.

Secure, government and military sites

Crews complete the access process each site requires, such as background checks, base credentials or escorts, arranged with the prime contractor before mobilization. Federal work follows the contract's wage determination and reporting requirements.
