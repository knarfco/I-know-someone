Government contracting and procurement

Government, military and defense facility cleaning

Construction and renovation cleaning for federal, state, municipal and military facilities, performed by our crews under the prime contractor's site plan and the facility's access rules. Send us your solicitation or bid package and we respond with an itemized bid and a capability statement.

How we work with government buyers

Federal contracting

We work as a subcontractor to prime contractors and directly where a contract allows. Send solicitations, scopes of work or bid packages and we respond with an itemized bid and a capability statement.

State and local government

City, county, state, school district and university projects, bid through the owner's procurement process or under the general contractor.

Military and defense subcontracting

Cleaning scopes on installation construction and renovation, coordinated with the prime contractor's safety plan, schedule and the installation's access process.

General contractor partnerships

Teaming with GCs on bids: we price the cleaning scope early so it is complete in the GC's proposal and does not turn into a change order later.

Bid and RFP requests

Send the solicitation number, due date, scope and location. We confirm crew availability and return pricing before your deadline.

NAICS and procurement information

Our NAICS and PSC codes, registrations and company data are on the capability statement. Registration details are listed only once verified.

Facility access and security

Background checks, badging, escorts and site security rules are arranged through the prime contractor and the facility before mobilization. We do not claim clearances we do not hold.

NAICS codes

  • 561720 Janitorial Services
  • 561790 Other Services to Buildings and Dwellings
  • 562119 Other Waste Collection
  • 238990 All Other Specialty Trade Contractors

Registrations such as SAM.gov UEI and CAGE are listed on the capability statement once verified.

What we never claim

We do not claim security clearances, facility clearances, certifications, set-aside status or contract vehicles we have not verified. Restricted-access, hazardous-material and defense-related work is performed only by appropriately qualified and authorized personnel, arranged with the prime contractor and the facility.
