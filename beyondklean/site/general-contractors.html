For general contractors

One crew for every cleaning scope on your job

Rough clean, final clean, glass, floors, overhead, kitchens and the touch-up before the owner walk. Send us the scope and we bid it line by line, then show up when your schedule says.

Where we fit in your schedule

What we keep off your punch list

  • Final cleaning scopes that leave out overhead structure, glass or floor finish, then turn into change orders.
  • Dust swept into the air that settles back on finishes a week later.
  • Residue removal that etches stone, scratches glass or voids a floor warranty.
  • Missing insurance certificates and unclear re-clean terms at the end of the job.
  • A failed Substantial Completion walk because the building was not inspection-clean.

The scope items most often missed

  1. Project typeResidential, commercial, industrial, institutional, government or military
  2. Needed byThe date it has to be done
  3. LocationCity and ZIP code
  4. SizeApproximate square footage
  5. TasksWhat needs cleaning

What you get at closeout

  • Room checklistsEvery room signed off
  • Photo documentationBefore and after, by area
  • Safety Data SheetsEvery product used
  • Certificate of insuranceWith required endorsements
  • Punch re-clean sign-offItems closed with the GC

What helps us sharpen the number

  • Drawings and the finish schedule
  • Your Division 01 cleaning section (often 01 74 13, 01 74 23 or 01 77 00)
  • Phase dates, inspection dates and the owner walk date
  • Site access rules, working hours and who provides water, power and lifts
