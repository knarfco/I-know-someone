Questions about hospitals, clinics and medical offices

What is an ICRA and why does it matter for cleaning?

An infection control risk assessment sets the containment and cleaning rules for construction in a healthcare facility. Our crews follow it so dust does not reach patient areas.

What is a terminal clean in a new clinical space?

A complete top-to-bottom clean and disinfection of every surface before the room is used for patients.

Can you work in an operating hospital?

Yes, inside the containment and schedule the facility's infection prevention team approves.
