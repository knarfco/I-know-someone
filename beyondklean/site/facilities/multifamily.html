Facility type

Apartments, condos and housing

Multifamily projects release units in waves, and every unit has to be inspection-clean for lease-up or closing. The scope repeats unit by unit and adds corridors, amenity spaces, garages and model units for leasing.

Questions about apartments, condos and housing

How are apartment units cleaned during lease-up?

Unit by unit as they are released, with a checklist for each unit and a touch-up before the resident walk.

Do you clean amenity spaces and model units?

Yes. Clubhouses, fitness rooms, pools, lobbies and model units are cleaned to a higher finish for leasing tours.

How is unit cleaning priced?

Usually per unit by size, with common areas priced by square foot and touch-ups per visit.
