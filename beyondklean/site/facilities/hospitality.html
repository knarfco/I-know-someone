Facility type

Hotels and hospitality

Hotels open floor by floor and are judged by guests on day one. Construction cleaning covers guest rooms, bathrooms, corridors, ballrooms, restaurants and back-of-house, often in a renovation while the rest of the hotel stays open.

Request a Bid Services for this building type

Questions about hotels and hospitality

Can you clean hotel floors as they are renovated?

Yes. Floors are cleaned and turned over to housekeeping as each one is released, so rooms come back into inventory quickly.

What is different about cleaning a new hotel room?

Every surface guests touch is detailed: bathroom grout and glass, under beds, inside drawers and closets, and fabrics vacuumed.

Do you clean ballrooms and restaurants?

Yes, including chandeliers and high ceilings from lifts, and kitchens to food-safety standards.
