Facility type

Schools and universities

School construction runs on the academic calendar: buildings must be ready for the first day of class, usually after a summer of renovation. The scope covers classrooms, labs, gyms, cafeterias and dorms, often across many buildings at once.

Questions about schools and universities

Can you clean a school over the summer break?

Yes. Summer renovations are the busiest season for school cleaning, and crews are scheduled building by building to meet the first day of class.

How are gym floors handled after construction?

Wood sport floors are dust-mopped and cleaned only with products approved by the floor's finish system, and protected from grit and moisture.

Do you clean dorms before move-in?

Yes. Dorm and student housing turnovers are cleaned room by room on the move-in schedule.
