Facility type

Industrial and manufacturing plants

Plants and production facilities need equipment, process areas and utilities clean before commissioning and startup. Metal fines, blast media, fireproofing overspray and construction dust all have to come out of machinery and rooms before validation and production begin.

Questions about industrial and manufacturing plants

Why does a new plant need cleaning before startup?

Construction debris and metal fines damage bearings, contaminate product and fail inspections. Cleaning before commissioning protects equipment and the startup schedule.

Do you clean inside production equipment?

Exterior and accessible surfaces, under lockout and with the owner's or installer's direction. Internal process cleaning follows the equipment maker's procedures.

How is abrasive blasting residue cleaned up?

With HEPA vacuums and wet methods rather than sweeping, and under the lead plan if the old coating contains lead.
