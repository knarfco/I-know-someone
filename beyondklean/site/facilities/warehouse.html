Facility type

Warehouses and distribution centers

Warehouses are huge, tall and dusty: open roof decks, high-bay lights, racking and concrete floors measured in acres. The cleaning scope is driven by lifts, auto-scrubbers and schedule, often while racking and conveyors are still being installed.

Questions about warehouses and distribution centers

How do you clean a warehouse ceiling after construction?

From scissor or boom lifts with HEPA vacuums and microfiber, top down, before racking is loaded so dust does not fall on product.

How are large warehouse floors cleaned?

With ride-on sweepers and auto-scrubbers in planned passes, then detail work at dock doors, columns and edges.

Can you clean around racking and conveyor installation?

Yes. Crews coordinate with the installers' schedule and clean zones as they are released.
