Questions about government and military facilities

Do you work as a subcontractor on government projects?

Yes. Most government cleaning scopes are subcontracted under the prime contractor, following its safety plan and submittals.

How is access handled on military installations?

Background checks, base credentials or escorts are arranged through the prime contractor and the installation's access process before mobilization.

Do government projects have special wage rules?

Often. Davis-Bacon or Service Contract Act wage determinations and certified payroll apply where the contract requires them.
