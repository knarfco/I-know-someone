Facility type

Restaurants and food service

Restaurants and commercial kitchens cannot open until they pass a health inspection, and construction dust in a kitchen is a food-safety problem. The scope adds FRP walls, stainless equipment, hood exteriors, walk-in coolers and floor drains to the standard construction clean.

Questions about restaurants and food service

Does a new restaurant need a special final clean?

Yes. Food-contact areas, equipment, walk-ins and drains need cleaning to food-safety standards before the health inspection, not just a general construction clean.

Who cleans the kitchen hood after construction?

Hood exteriors are part of the final clean. Exhaust duct and fan cleaning to NFPA 96 is done by hood-cleaning technicians and documented for the fire marshal.

What is FRP and how is it cleaned?

Fiberglass reinforced plastic wall panels. They are washed with non-abrasive cleaners to remove adhesive, dust and marks without scratching the textured surface.
