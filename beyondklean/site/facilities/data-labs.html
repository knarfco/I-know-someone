Facility type

Data centers, labs and controlled environments

Data centers, laboratories and cleanrooms have contamination limits that ordinary construction cleaning will not meet. Particle counts, static control and documented sequences matter as much as how the space looks.

Questions about data centers, labs and controlled environments

Why does a data center need special construction cleaning?

Dust is pulled into servers by cooling fans and causes failures. Cleaning uses HEPA, antistatic methods and often particle testing before equipment is energized.

Can you clean a cleanroom to a certification test?

Our crews clean in the sequence the owner's protocol and the certifier require, so the room is ready to test. The independent certifier performs the testing.

Do you clean under raised access floors?

Yes. The sub-floor plenum is vacuumed tile by tile with HEPA equipment before the space goes live.
