Airports, transit and infrastructure

Airports, transit stations, parking garages and marinas open to the public on fixed dates under security and environmental rules. The scope covers huge concrete areas, glass, escalators and public restrooms, with wash water kept out of waterways.

Questions about airports, transit and infrastructure

How does cleaning work at an airport construction site?

Crews are badged through the airport's process, follow airside rules and FOD control, and clean in phases around passenger operations.

How are new parking garages cleaned?

Power sweeping, auto-scrubbing and washing with water recovery, timed around striping and sealers.

Do you clean escalators and elevators in public stations?

Yes, with the elevator contractor's lockout and in the order the opening schedule requires.
