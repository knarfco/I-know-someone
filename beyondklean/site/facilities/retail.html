Facility type

Retail stores and openings

Retail cleaning runs on a hard opening date. Fixtures arrive late, merchandise follows right behind, and the store has to look perfect for the grand opening. The scope covers construction cleaning, fixture cleaning, storefront glass and floors, often overnight in malls and live shopping centers.

Questions about retail stores and openings

When should cleaning be scheduled for a store opening?

A rough and final clean before fixtures, a fixture clean after they are set, and a touch-up the night before opening, after merchandise is stocked.

Do you clean store fixtures and shelving?

Yes. Gondolas, wall systems, cases and checkout counters are cleaned of dust, labels and adhesive before merchandise goes on them.

Can cleaning happen overnight in a mall?

Yes. Most mall and shopping-center work is scheduled after hours under the property's rules for access and noise.
