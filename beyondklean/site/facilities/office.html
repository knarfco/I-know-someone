Facility type

Offices and tenant improvements

Office projects range from a single-suite tenant improvement to a full headquarters, often finished floor by floor while other tenants stay in the building. The cleaning scope centers on fine dust, glass, carpet and ceiling grid, with work scheduled around occupied floors and building rules on freight elevators and hours.

Questions about offices and tenant improvements

How long does a final clean take on an office build-out?

A 10,000 square foot suite usually takes one to three days with a full crew. Larger floors are cleaned in phases as areas are released, so occupancy can start on schedule.

Can you clean an office suite while the rest of the building is occupied?

Yes. Crews follow the building's rules for hours, freight elevator use and corridor protection, and clean common areas the work affected.

What gets missed most on office final cleans?

Tops of ceiling grid and light lenses, window tracks, inside cabinets and the dust behind and under desks and partitions once furniture lands.
