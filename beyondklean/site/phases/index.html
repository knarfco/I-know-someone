Construction cleaning by project phase

Construction cleaning runs with the schedule. Each phase has its own scope, spec section and timing, and one bid can cover all of them.

Rough cleanAfter drywall and rough-in, before flooring, ceilings and finish carpentry.Progress cleaningThroughout construction, from the first trades on site until final cleaning starts.Final cleanAfter finishes, fixtures and equipment are installed, before the inspection and owner walk.Touch-up and punch cleaningAfter punch work, the day before or the morning of the owner walk.Turnover and move-in cleaningAt occupancy, lease-up or handover to the owner.Recurring and maintenance cleaningAfter the building opens, on a monthly, quarterly or annual schedule.Specialty cleaning at any stageWhenever the scope calls for it, from demolition through closeout.
