About Beyond Klean

Beyond Klean is a construction and specialty cleaning company. We clean new and renovated buildings of every type, from a single apartment turn to a full campus, with methods built for new finishes.

What we do

Our crews handle rough, final and touch-up cleaning, glass, floors, kitchens, overhead structure, exteriors and closeout for general contractors, property managers and building owners. Every bid is itemized, and every job closes out with checklists and photos.

Where we work

Home crews cover the Southeast. Travel crews mobilize nationwide for larger projects. Send the city and ZIP code and we'll confirm availability before we bid.

How we work

Capture dust instead of moving it. Use the least aggressive chemistry that does the job. Follow the manufacturer's care guide on every new finish. Assign regulated work only to licensed, certified technicians.
