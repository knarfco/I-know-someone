About Beyond Klean

Beyond Klean is a national specialty cleaning procurement platform. We built the most complete library of construction and specialty cleaning services we know of, 438 services and 1,510 answers, so buyers can specify the work correctly and find the right contractor to do it.

What we do

We help general contractors, developers, property owners and facility managers scope specialty cleaning, then connect them with independent, verified contractors. We handle coordination and documentation. The contractor that quotes the job performs it, insures it and warrants it.

What we don't do

We don't sell your request to a list of companies, invent local offices, or claim capabilities our network doesn't have. Where we don't yet have verified coverage, we say so.

Our partner program

Recurring overhead and high dusting is handled through Above Eye Level Cleaning, which keeps open ceilings, structure and fixtures clean after the building opens.
