    Our methods

    Capture it. Don't move it.

    Construction dust that is swept or blown just lands somewhere else. Our crews capture it with HEPA vacuums and microfiber, use water and pH-neutral products before stronger chemistry, and keep wash water out of storm drains.

    How we clean differently

    Overhead and high dusting

    The ceiling gets cleaned once. Then it gets dusty again.

    Open decks, beams, ducts and fixtures collect dust long after turnover. Beyond Klean schedules recurring high dusting for stores, warehouses and food facilities.
