    How it works

    Scoped first. Matched second. Quoted by the contractor who does the work.

    1. Describe the work. Facility type, project stage, square footage, heights, surfaces, deadline, plans and photos.
    2. We qualify the scope. We check for missing items like overhead, glass and floor care, and flag regulated work.
    3. We match a verified contractor. Insurance, specialty and current capacity are confirmed before any introduction.
    4. The contractor quotes. The company performing the work prices it, signs it and warrants it.
    5. Work is documented. Checklists, photos and product data sheets for your closeout package.

    Our methods

    Capture it. Don't move it.

    Construction dust that is swept or blown just lands somewhere else. Our network captures it with HEPA vacuums and microfiber, uses water and pH-neutral products before stronger chemistry, and keeps wash water out of storm drains.

    How we clean differently

    Above Eye Level Cleaning

    The ceiling gets cleaned once. Then it gets dusty again.

    Open decks, beams, ducts and fixtures collect dust long after turnover. Our partner program schedules recurring high dusting for stores, warehouses and food facilities.
