Prepared October 8, 2026. Applies to any building type and any location. ## What overlaps

There are three kinds of overlap, and each one is handled differently on the website:

**1. Merge: the same work under two names (16).** These become one page, and the second name becomes a search term on that page. Examples:
- "Grout haze removal" = "Grout and setting material haze removal"
- "Pre-substantial completion clean" = "Final clean of entire building"
- "Pallet rack cleaning" = "Top of shelving and racking dusting"
- "Silica-aware wet sweeping" = "Silica-aware housekeeping cleanup"

**2. Variant: the same method on a different surface (15 clusters).** These get one strong parent page with surface-specific sections or child pages. Examples:
- Adhesive residue on resilient floors, under floor protection, on FRP, and tape residue
- Paint residue on concrete, carpet, glass, and slabs
- Labels and stickers on glass, restroom fixtures, and appliances
- Protective film on stainless, glass, carpet, and appliances

**3. Bundle: separate tasks that are bought together (15 clusters).** These keep separate pages, are cross-linked, and are quoted as a package. Examples:
- Overhead MEP: ducts + piping + sprinkler pipe + cable tray + conduit. This is the core AELC bundle.
- Ceiling plane: tiles + grid + diffusers
- Dock area: bay + dock wash + truck court + dock doors
- Construction IAQ: temporary filters, final filters, pre-flush-out clean, photo log

The other thing that overlaps is the **names**. The 438 unique tasks are sold and searched under 982 names. Pages are built on tasks, and the extra names go inside those pages as wording that matches searches. This is what keeps the site from becoming thousands of near-duplicate pages.

## The green and safer-chemistry angle: what the research supports

- **HEPA capture and wet methods are both safer and required practice.** OSHA's construction silica standard (29 CFR 1926.1153) prohibits dry sweeping and dry brushing where they could contribute to silica exposure, unless wet sweeping, HEPA vacuuming or other low-exposure methods aren't feasible. It also restricts using compressed air to clean. That makes "we capture dust, we don't blow it around" a factual, compliance-backed selling point.
- **Construction IAQ is a recognized green-building discipline.** LEED's construction IAQ credit is built on the SMACNA guidelines: HVAC protection, source control, pathway interruption, housekeeping, scheduling, plus filter changes and flush-out before occupancy. That's a natural service line for a green construction cleaning brand (Family 12).
- **Safer-chemistry claims need a recognized standard behind them.** EPA's Safer Choice program certifies *products*, screening every ingredient and setting pH and VOC limits. Green Seal GS-42 certifies *cleaning services*, covering procedures, purchasing and training. Specifying certified products and moving toward GS-42 gives the green positioning real proof.
- **Watch the wording.** The FTC Green Guides say that "non-toxic" means no risk to people *or* the environment and needs solid scientific evidence. They also warn against unqualified "green" or "eco-friendly" claims. So the site should describe **methods and reasons** (HEPA, microfiber, pH-neutral, low-VOC, water reclamation) and name certified products only once you have their documentation.
- **Glass is a known liability area.** The window cleaning association (IWCA) treats final construction cleaning and debris removal as non-routine, damage-prone work and recommends a glass-scratch waiver on construction window contracts. That's worth building into the Glass family and the contractor requirements.

## What this research does not prove yet

- **Search volume per task.** This list covers the full range of work, not demand. The next step is pulling keyword volumes to rank which pages to build first.
- **Prices or production rates.** None are included. Those should come from real subcontractor quotes.
- **Full spec text.** The CSI section text (01 74 13 Progress Cleaning, 01 74 23 Final Cleaning, 01 77 00 Closeout) is licensed. The final-clean requirements here come from public owner and agency specs.
