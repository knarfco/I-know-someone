Step 1

Create the Channel

  1. Use a company Google account, not a personal one.Example: the company Gmail. The channel should belong to the business.
  2. Go to youtube.com and sign in.On a computer is easiest for setup.
  3. Tap your profile picture → Create a channel.If you already have one, go to Settings → Add or manage your channels.
  4. Enter the name and handle from the next slide.
  5. Add the logo and the website link.We'll send you the logo file sized for YouTube.
Keep the login safe.
Write down which account owns the channel and turn on 2-step verification. Matt and Sam stay the owners.

You don't need to give us your password. We only need the video links.

Step 3

Upload Your Videos

The Backlog

The footage already on your phones and drives.

  • Upload in batches from a computer
  • Start with your biggest and best jobs
  • Then a few at a time, whenever you have a minute

New Jobs

Going forward, right from the job site.

  • Upload from the YouTube app on your phone
  • Same day is great; same week is fine
  • Wi-Fi saves your data plan

Visibility

PublicBest choice. Anyone can find it on YouTube and on the website.
UnlistedFine for sensitive jobs. Works on the website, hidden from YouTube search.
PrivateDon't use. Nobody can see it, including the website.

YouTube Playbook · Confidential10

On the job site

Filming Tips

📱 Hold It Sideways

Horizontal fills the screen on YouTube and the website.

📍 Same Spot, Three Times

Before, during and after from the same place. That's the shot that sells.

🌄 Start Wide

Show the whole site first, then move in on the machines.

✋ Keep It Steady

Slow pans. Brace your arm or set the phone on the truck.

⏱️ Short Is Fine

Thirty seconds to a few minutes. Real beats polished.

🎙️ Say What It Is

Optional: "Clearing ten acres of pepper trees for a new pond." It helps people follow along.

YouTube Playbook · Confidential11

Before you post

Privacy Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Ask the owner or agency before posting their job
  • Use Unlisted when a client wants it low-key
  • Show the crew in hard hats, vests and proper gear
  • Show the site cleaned up at the end

Don't

  • Show house numbers, license plates or people's faces without their OK
  • Film where a contract or agency says no cameras
  • Post anything that looks unsafe or like an accident
  • Mention prices, bids or client disputes
