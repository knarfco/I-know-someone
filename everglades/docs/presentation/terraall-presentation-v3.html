05 · Every job, Matt's choice

Take It. Team It. Route It. Or 86 It.

Everglades does itTake It

Large clearing, restoration, government and prime work, storm response. The crew performs and the job becomes proof.

Everglades leadsTeam It

Everglades runs the job and brings in a licensed partner for a specialty or extra capacity.

A partner does itRoute It

Too small, too far or too specialized? A vetted partner performs — and Everglades earns points on the work.

No obligation86 It

Not a fit? Pass. No cost, no commitment. The request still teaches the system what buyers want.

TerraAll never obligates Everglades to anything. The partner and referral structure is set up once with the company's attorney before launch.
