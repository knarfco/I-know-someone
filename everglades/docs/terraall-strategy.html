01 · The idea

Every Job Gets the Call

From the $100 driveway culvert to the $100 billion restoration program, every earthwork request should come to one place first — and that place should be yours.

Nobody is going after the small jobs. Almost nobody can handle the giant ones. Everglades can do both.

Today, most earthwork demand is scattered: homeowners search for "lot clearing near me," developers call whoever they used last time, agencies post bids on a dozen portals, and prime contractors scramble for qualified subs. No single company is set up to catch all of it.

TerraAll changes that. It's a digital front door built to catch every request — then put it in front of the one person who knows exactly what to do with it.

Found

On Google, Maps, YouTube, AI assistants and government bid portals — for every service, every size of job.

Trusted

Real licenses, real job videos, real compliance — in plain view, the moment someone lands.

Routed

Every request sorted and put in front of Matt, who decides what happens to it.

02 · The name

Why "TerraAll"

Terra means earth. All means every job, every size, every place.

The name says what the company does without boxing it into a region. It's short, easy to say, easy to remember and easy to type — the kind of name people repeat to each other.

Why not just "Everglades"?

  • Everglades stays. It's the licensed, bonded, insured company with 35 years of trust — and the name procurement officers see on SAM.gov, licenses and bonds.
  • But on its own, "Everglades" reads as one region, and online it competes with the government's massive "Everglades restoration" program for attention.
  • TerraAll is the wide-net front door; Everglades is the proven company behind it. Together they cover every buyer.

05 · Every job, Matt's choice

Take It. Team It. Route It. Or 86 It.

TerraAll never obligates Everglades to anything. It simply makes sure every opportunity gets the call — and Matt chooses what happens next.

Everglades does it

Take It

The jobs that fit Everglades best — large clearing, restoration, government and prime work, storm response. The crew does the work and the job becomes proof.

Everglades leads

Team It

Everglades runs the job and brings in a licensed partner for a specialty or extra capacity. Bigger jobs, without turning anything away.

A partner does it

Route It

Jobs that are too small, too far or too specialized go to a vetted, licensed, insured partner — and Everglades earns points on the work.

No obligation

86 It

Not a fit? Pass. No cost, no commitment, no hard feelings. The request still taught the system what buyers want.

Why this matters

Today, the small jobs go nowhere and the far-away jobs go to someone else. With TerraAll, every request either grows Everglades directly, grows it through a partnership, or earns on someone else's work. Matt's expertise becomes revenue in more ways than one.

The referral and partner structure (how points are earned, partner licensing and insurance requirements, and what's disclosed to customers) is set up once with the company's attorney before launch.

07 · The thinking

Why We're Building It This Way

Procurement first

Big contracts are won on trust. Licenses, registrations, bonding, compliance and a one-page capability statement sit where contracting officers look — on every page.

Real proof, not stock photos

Every claim links to evidence: dated job videos, credentials and compliance policies. Buyers believe what they can see.

Growth search engines reward

Search engines penalize mass-produced, thin pages. Every TerraAll page is backed by a real job, a real answer or a real guide — so the library can grow very large, safely.

Built for voice and AI

Short, direct answers first, then the detail — the format voice assistants and AI tools use when they recommend a company.

Video as the engine

YouTube is one of the biggest places people search. Every video does double duty: proof for buyers and a new door for search.

Clean, fast, accessible

A calm, professional design that loads fast on any phone and meets accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA) — something agencies increasingly expect.

One engine, many doors

One question bank, one video pipeline, one sorting system — powering both the Everglades site and TerraAll.

Owned outright

The site, the content, the videos and the domain belong to the company. Nothing is rented; everything compounds.
