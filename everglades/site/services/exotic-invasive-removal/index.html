Exotic & Invasive Plant Removal

Melaleuca, Brazilian pepper and Australian pine removed mechanically, at scale — on preserves, conservation land, development sites and private property.

Request a BidCall (XXX) XXX-XXXX

Invasive trees choke out native wetlands, block drainage, and are often required to be removed before a property can be developed or a preserve can be signed off.

We remove them mechanically — cutting, mulching, root-raking and hauling — and coordinate follow-up treatment so they don't come back.

What's Included

  • Melaleuca removal
  • Brazilian pepper removal
  • Australian pine removal
  • Preserve and conservation-easement clean-up
  • Exotic removal required for development sign-off
  • Follow-up treatment by licensed applicators FDACS license # XXX or partner

Questions

Is Brazilian pepper illegal to keep?

Florida lists Brazilian pepper as a prohibited invasive plant, and many counties require it removed when land is developed. We can tell you what applies to your property.

Will the plants grow back?

They will if the stumps aren't treated. We coordinate licensed follow-up treatment as part of the job.
