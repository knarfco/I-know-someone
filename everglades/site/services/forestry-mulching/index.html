Forestry Mulching

Clear underbrush, palmetto and small trees in one pass — no burn piles, no hauling, and the topsoil stays where it belongs.

Request a BidCall (XXX) XXX-XXXX

A forestry mulcher grinds brush and small trees into a layer of mulch right where they stand. It's the fastest, cleanest way to open up overgrown land without tearing up the ground.

It's ideal for fence lines, trails, fire breaks, view corridors, survey lines, and lots where you want to keep the good trees and lose everything else.

What's Included

  • Underbrush and palmetto clearing
  • Selective clearing that saves the trees you want to keep
  • Fire breaks and wildfire-risk reduction
  • Trail, fence-line and survey-line clearing
  • Invasive plant knock-down (Brazilian pepper and more)

Also called: brush mulching, mulching service, underbrush clearing, palmetto clearing, fire break clearing, trail cutting, fence line clearing.

Questions

Is forestry mulching better than regular land clearing?

For brush, palmetto and small trees, usually yes — it's faster and leaves the soil protected. For big trees, stumps or building pads, traditional clearing is often the better fit. Read more →

What happens to the mulch?

It stays on the ground as a protective layer that holds moisture, reduces erosion and breaks down naturally.
