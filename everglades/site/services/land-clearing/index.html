Land Clearing in South Florida

Residential lots, acreage, ranch land and commercial sites, cleared right the first time by a crew that has been doing it since 1992.

Request a BidCall (XXX) XXX-XXXX

Whether it's a single homesite in Southwest Ranches or two hundred acres of overgrown pasture near Lake Okeechobee, land clearing in South Florida is its own kind of work. Limestone just under the surface, high water tables, protected trees and wetland edges all change how a site has to be cleared.

We plan every job around what comes next: a house pad, a pond, a pasture, a solar field, or a restored wetland. That means the right machines, the right permits, and a site left ready for the next trade.

What's Included

  • Residential lot clearing for new homes, pools and house pads
  • Commercial and development site clearing
  • Acreage, ranch and pasture clearing
  • Right-of-way, utility and solar-field clearing
  • Stump removal and root raking
  • Debris hauled off, or mulched on site to save hauling costs

Also called: lot clearing, brush clearing, underbrush removal, palmetto removal, acreage clearing, tree and stump removal, pasture clearing, right-of-way clearing.

Questions

How much does land clearing cost per acre?

It depends on how thick the growth is, the size of the trees, whether there are wetlands, and whether debris is hauled away or mulched. See our full answer, with typical South Florida ranges, on the cost page. Read more →

Do I need a permit to clear my land?

In most of South Florida, yes — tree removal and any work near wetlands usually need a county or city permit, and sometimes state or federal approval. We help you sort it out before work starts. Read more →

Do you haul everything away?

Your choice. We can haul and dispose of everything, or mulch it on site, which is often faster and less expensive.
