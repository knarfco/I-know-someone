Canal, Lake & Pond Excavation

Retention ponds, lakes, canals and ditches dug, cleaned and shaped to spec — and lake banks rebuilt when erosion takes them.

Request a BidCall (XXX) XXX-XXXX

South Florida runs on water management. New developments need retention and detention ponds, older neighborhoods need canals and ditches cleaned out, and HOA lake banks wash away a little more every storm season.

We dig, clean and restore them — with long-reach equipment for canal work and the know-how to work around the drainage districts and permits that govern South Florida water.

What's Included

  • Retention and detention pond construction
  • Lake and pond digging for homes, ranches and developments
  • Canal and ditch cleaning, dredging and muck removal
  • Lake bank and canal bank erosion repair
  • Swale and ditch excavation
  • Fill produced from excavation, used on site or hauled

Also called: pond digging, retention pond construction, detention pond, lake excavation, canal dredging, canal cleaning, muck removal, lake bank erosion repair.

Questions

Do I need a permit to dig a pond?

Almost always. Ponds and canal work usually involve the county, the South Florida Water Management District, and sometimes a local drainage district. We'll walk you through it. Read more →

Can you fix our HOA's eroded lake banks?

Yes. Lake bank restoration is one of the most common jobs we see for HOAs and property managers across Broward and Palm Beach.
