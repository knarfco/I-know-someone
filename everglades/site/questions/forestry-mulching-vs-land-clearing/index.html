Forestry mulching or traditional land clearing — which do I need?

Short answerChoose forestry mulching for brush, palmetto and small trees when you want a fast, clean job that protects the soil. Choose traditional clearing when you need big trees and stumps out, or a bare site ready for a pad, pond or road.
