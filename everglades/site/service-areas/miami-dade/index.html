Land Clearing & Excavation in Miami-Dade County

South Miami-Dade is limestone country. In the Redland, Homestead and Florida City, clearing and site prep often mean rock plowing, and the Everglades edge brings wetland rules into play. Miami-Dade requires tree permits, a Class IV permit for work in wetlands, and Class I permits for mangroves.
