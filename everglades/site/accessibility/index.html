Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who are blind, have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or use a keyboard or assistive technology.

Effective: Month Day, 2026

Our commitment

Everglades Restorations & Land Clearing Inc. is committed to making this website accessible. Our goal is to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard referenced under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and aligned with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.

What we've done

  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and a text label for the logo.
  • Clear page structure with headings, landmarks and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including menus, forms, the before-and-after slider and the service checklist.
  • Color contrast that meets WCAG AA for text and controls.
  • Labeled form fields with plain-language instructions.
  • Pages that resize and reflow on phones and when zoomed.
  • Respect for the “reduce motion” setting on your device.
  • Automated testing of every page with axe-core accessibility checks, most recently on October 24, 2026.

Video and audio

Job videos are hosted on YouTube. We are adding captions to our videos and written descriptions on each job page, so the key information is available without sound or sight. If a video you need is missing captions, tell us and we will provide the information in another format.

Known limitations

  • Some third-party content, such as embedded YouTube players and maps, is controlled by those providers.
  • Some older videos may not yet have captions.
  • Downloadable documents are being reviewed; ask us for an accessible version of any document.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or need information in a different format, contact us and we will help:

Phone: (XXX) XXX-XXXX
Email: info@XXX.com

We aim to respond within 2 business days. If you are a government agency or procurement office reviewing our materials, we can provide accessible versions of our capability statement and other documents on request.

Ongoing review

We review the site for accessibility as we add new pages and videos. This statement was last reviewed on October 24, 2026.
