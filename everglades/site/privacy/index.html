Privacy Policy

What information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective: Month Day, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Everglades Restorations & Land Clearing Inc. (“we,” “us”). This policy explains how we handle information collected through this website, including our bid request form, service checklist, phone and email links, and text messages.

Information you give us

  • Bid requests and contact forms: your name, company or agency, phone number, email address, job location, project details, permit status, solicitation numbers and anything else you choose to send.
  • Service checklist: your name and your selections.
  • Calls, emails and texts: your phone number, email address and the content of your messages.
  • Photos or videos you send us of a job site.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the page that referred you and the date and time. When you submit a bid request, the form also records which page you first landed on and how you found us, so we know which parts of the site help people most.

Browser storage: the site uses your browser's local storage to remember your service checklist answers and your first landing page during a visit. This information stays on your device unless you submit a form.

Analytics: We use Google Analytics / no analytics — confirm. If analytics are used, they collect information about how visitors use the site in aggregate. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request, prepare estimates and bids, and do the work you hire us for.
  • To contact you by phone, email or text about your request.
  • To keep records required for contracts, permits, insurance and government reimbursement (such as FEMA documentation).
  • To improve the website and understand how people find us.
  • To protect against fraud, spam and misuse.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to serve you:

  • Partner contractors and specialists: when your project needs a licensed partner (for example, a specialty trade or a crew closer to your site), we may share your project details and contact information with that partner so they can help with your request.
  • Service providers who help us run the business, such as our website host, email and phone providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Government agencies and prime contractors when required by a contract, permit, reimbursement program or law.
  • Legal reasons: to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect our rights and safety.

Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages, we use your number only to communicate about your request or project. See our Text Messaging Terms. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

How long we keep information

We keep information as long as needed to respond to you, perform our work and meet legal, tax, contract and insurance record-keeping requirements. Public-contract records may be kept longer where public records laws require it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No website or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as bank account numbers or Social Security numbers through the website.

Children

This website is for businesses, agencies and property owners. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information you have given us, subject to records we must keep by law or contract.
  • Opt out of texts by replying STOP, and out of emails by asking us.
  • Clear your browser storage at any time to remove saved checklist answers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date below shows when it last changed.
