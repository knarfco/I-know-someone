Compliance & Certifications

The policies and programs government agencies, prime contractors and owners look for — in one place.

Effective: Month Day, 2026

Drug-Free Workplace

Everglades Restorations & Land Clearing Inc. maintains a Drug-Free Workplace program. The unlawful manufacture, distribution, possession or use of controlled substances is prohibited in our workplace and on job sites. Our program includes a written policy provided to all employees, employee awareness, drug and alcohol testing — pre-employment / random / post-incident / reasonable suspicion — confirm, and reporting requirements consistent with the federal Drug-Free Workplace Act of 1988 and Florida's drug-free workplace provisions (Section 287.087, Florida Statutes).

E-Verify

Everglades Restorations & Land Clearing Inc. is registered with and uses E-Verify, the federal employment eligibility verification system, to confirm the work authorization of all newly hired employees, as required for contractors working with Florida public agencies under Section 448.095, Florida Statutes. We require our subcontractors on public projects to provide affidavits that they do not employ, contract with or subcontract with unauthorized workers. E-Verify Company ID: XXX.

Equal Opportunity & Non-Discrimination

Everglades Restorations & Land Clearing Inc. is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate in employment, subcontracting or service on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status or any other characteristic protected by law. We comply with Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act, the Age Discrimination in Employment Act and applicable state and local laws, and with the equal opportunity clauses included in our public contracts.

Safety

Safety comes first on every job. Our safety program includes:

  • Written safety program and site-specific safety planning.
  • OSHA-compliant practices and training: OSHA 10 / OSHA 30 / HAZWOPER — confirm.
  • Daily job briefings and equipment inspections.
  • Experience Modification Rate (EMR): X.XX · Safety record: XXX.

Ethics & Integrity

  • We do not offer or accept kickbacks, bribes or improper gifts, consistent with the federal Anti-Kickback Act and state procurement ethics laws.
  • We disclose potential conflicts of interest.
  • We bid honestly and do not collude with other bidders.

Public Records

On contracts with Florida public agencies, we comply with the Florida Public Records Law (Chapter 119, Florida Statutes) as required by contract, including keeping and providing public records related to the work.

Contractor licenses, insurance, bonding, SAM.gov registration (UEI / CAGE), NAICS codes, FEMA vendor and Army Corps information are listed on our Licenses & Credentials page and in our Capability Statement.
