Text Messaging Terms

How our text messages work and how to stop them.

Effective: Month Day, 2026

Program

Everglades Restorations & Land Clearing Inc. sends text messages about bid requests, scheduling, project updates and service follow-ups to people who give us their mobile number and agree to receive texts.

Consent

By checking the text-message box on our forms, or by texting us first, you agree to receive text messages from Everglades Restorations & Land Clearing Inc. at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on your request or project. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop or get help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one confirmation and no further texts unless you opt in again. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (XXX) XXX-XXXX or info@XXX.com.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with or sold to third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
