Good questions

Frequently asked.

How much does a new floor cost?

It depends on material, square footage and subfloor condition. That’s why the in-home estimate is free: we measure, test and give you a written number with no surprises.

How long does installation take?

Most single rooms take 1–2 days. A whole home is typically 3–10 days. Refinishing needs extra time for the finish to cure, and we’ll plan around your life.

Can you install hardwood in South Florida?

Yes — with the right product and prep. We moisture-test the slab, use proper barriers and often recommend engineered wood, which handles humidity beautifully.

Do you move furniture and remove old flooring?

We do. Furniture moving, tear-out, haul-away and disposal can all be included in your quote.

Do you work on commercial projects?

Absolutely. Restaurants, offices, retail and multi-unit properties, often after hours so you stay open.
