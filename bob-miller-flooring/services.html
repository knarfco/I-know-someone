Good questions

Frequently asked.

Which floor is right for my home?

It depends on the room, your slab or subfloor, pets, kids and budget. Use the Floor Finder above, then let Bob bring samples so you can see them in your own light.

Do you really install carpet, linoleum and tile too?

Yes. Bob has installed every kind of floor over six decades, and his crew includes carpet specialists and tile and stone setters who work to his standard.

Is laminate (like Pergo) as good as vinyl?

They’re different. Quality laminate is harder against scratches; luxury vinyl is fully waterproof. For kitchens, baths and Florida slabs, we usually recommend vinyl or tile.

How much does a new floor cost?

It depends on material, square footage and subfloor condition. That’s why the in-home estimate is free: we measure, test and give you a written number with no surprises.

How long does installation take?

Most single rooms take 1–2 days. A whole home is typically 3–10 days. Tile needs time to cure, refinishing needs time to dry, and we’ll plan around your life.

Do you move furniture and remove old flooring?

We do. Furniture moving, tear-out (including old carpet, tile and glued-down vinyl), haul-away and disposal can all be included in your quote.

Are you licensed and insured?
